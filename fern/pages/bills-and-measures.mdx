California bills and measures move through a public legislative process. A bill number alone does not tell you whether a proposal is current, enacted, chaptered, or operative.

## Official legislative sources

- [California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/) — search bills, measures, statuses, analyses, votes, and bill text.
- [California Legislature](https://www.legislature.ca.gov/) — learn about the Senate and Assembly.
- [California Governor](https://www.gov.ca.gov/) — review executive actions and signing information.

## Status checkpoints

When tracking a measure, record:

- the house of origin and current committee;
- introduced, amended, engrossed, enrolled, and chaptered versions;
- committee and floor votes;
- the Governor's action; and
- the effective or operative date.

<Warning>
A proposed bill, an enacted statute, and a provision that is currently operative are different legal objects. Cite the correct version for the question you are answering.
</Warning>

## Research practice

Save the bill number, version date, author, source URL, and the date you accessed the material. For historical research, compare versions rather than relying only on the latest text.
