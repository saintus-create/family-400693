An invitation to comment gives the public an opportunity to respond to a proposed regulation, rule, policy, or other public action. The notice controls the deadline, submission method, and scope of comments requested.

## Before submitting a comment

1. Read the complete notice and any incorporated materials.
2. Confirm the deadline, time zone, and accepted submission channels.
3. Identify the specific section, proposal, or question addressed.
4. Support factual assertions with reliable sources.
5. Keep a copy of the submitted comment and any confirmation.

## Find official notices

Start with the [California Legislative Information portal](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/) and the website of the agency or court responsible for the proposal. Use the notice's own contact information when the source of authority is unclear.

<Note>
Public comment is not the same as filing a legal pleading. Do not include confidential, privileged, or unnecessary personal information in a public submission.
</Note>
