Public legal information is distributed across legislative, judicial, and agency systems. The best source depends on the record type, date, and custodian.

## Common starting points

- [California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/) — bills, statutes, journals, and legislative history.
- [California Courts](https://www.courts.ca.gov/) — court information, rules, forms, and opinions.
- [California Public Records Act](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=7923.100.&lawCode=GOV) — statutory framework for access to public records.

## Build a reliable record trail

Record the title, issuing body, document date, version, URL, and access date. Prefer a first-party source and preserve downloaded copies only when permitted by the source's terms and applicable law.

## Requesting records

If a record is not available online, identify the agency likely to maintain it and review that agency's request instructions. Be specific about the subject, date range, format, and custodial office. Response times and exemptions may vary.

<Warning>
Public availability does not mean every use is appropriate. Handle personal information carefully and follow applicable privacy, copyright, and court-access rules.
</Warning>

This page is an access guide, not legal advice.
