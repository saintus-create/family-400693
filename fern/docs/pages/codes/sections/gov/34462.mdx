## Text

(a) A charter commission established for a city and county pursuant to this chapter shall complete a proposed or amended charter and submit the charter to the voters of the city and county within two years of the date of the election of the charter commissioners, and at the expiration of that period is abolished.

(b) A charter commission may submit portions of the proposed or amended charter to the voters periodically.
