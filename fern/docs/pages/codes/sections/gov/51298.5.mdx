## Text

(a) This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2035.

(b) A capital investment incentive program established pursuant to this chapter before January 1, 2035, may remain in effect for the full term of that program, regardless of whether this chapter becomes inoperative or is repealed.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 581, Sec. 3. (AB 2922) Effective September 25, 2024. Note: Termination clause affects Chapter 8, commencing with Section 51298.)
