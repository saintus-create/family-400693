## Text

(a) The California Housing and Homelessness Agency and the Business and Consumer Services Agency, along with their respective departments, shall coordinate state policy, programs, and funding to further the state’s objectives related to housing, homelessness, civil rights, and consumer protections and facilitate administrative continuity following the dissolution of the Business, Consumer Services, and Housing Agency on July 1, 2026.

(b) The Secretary of California Housing and Homelessness and the Secretary of Business and Consumer Services shall maintain and expand upon data, policy, and programmatic partnerships between departments within their respective agencies and evaluate opportunities to enhance interagency coordination and policy alignment in furtherance of statutory mandates.
