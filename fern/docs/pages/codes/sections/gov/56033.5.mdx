## Text

“Disadvantaged unincorporated community” means inhabited territory, as defined by Section 56046, or as determined by commission policy, that constitutes all or a portion of a “disadvantaged community” as defined by Section 79505.5 of the Water Code.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 513, Sec. 3. (SB 244) Effective January 1, 2012.)
