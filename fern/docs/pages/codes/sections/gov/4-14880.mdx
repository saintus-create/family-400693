## Text

The department has general supervision of the distribution of all public documents and other publications printed for any state agency and the custody of all state documents and other publications subject to public distribution, except those printed for the special use of the Legislature, the Governor, or the elective officers of the state.

The department shall fix the price and dispose of or sell the documents and publications.
