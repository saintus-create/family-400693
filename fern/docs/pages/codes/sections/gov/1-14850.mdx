## Text

All state printing shall be done in the Office of State Printing.

State work only shall be done in the Office of State Printing. The Office of State Printing may also be known as the Office of State Publishing.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 784, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
