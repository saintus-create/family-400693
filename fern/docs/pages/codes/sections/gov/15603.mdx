## Text

The annual salary of each member of the State Board of Equalization representing one of the equalization districts of the state is provided for by Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 11550) of Part 1. The member shall devote his or her entire time to the services of the state in performing the duties imposed upon the board and its members by the Constitution and statutes of this state.
