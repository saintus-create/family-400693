## Text

If the Legislature diminishes the number of judges of the superior court in any county or city and county, the offices which first become vacant, to the number of judges diminished, shall be deemed to be abolished.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 161.)
