## Text

The office’s headquarters shall be in Sacramento. A hearing office shall be located in Sacramento, Fresno, and Los Angeles.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 16, Sec. 13. (AB 102) Effective June 27, 2017. Operative July 1, 2017, pursuant to Section 15680.)
