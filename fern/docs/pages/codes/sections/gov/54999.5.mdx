## Text

Capital facilities fees paid by school districts for public utility facilities to serve school facilities for which an application for funding is filed on or after the effective date of this chapter may, for purposes of Chapter 12 (commencing with Section 17000) of Part 10 of the Education Code, be included by the State Allocation Board as a “cost of project” within the meaning of subdivision (b) of Section 17702 of the Education Code.
