## Text

If the legislative body determines that the consents have been filed, it shall by resolution finally confirm the proceedings, the assessment and diagram, and the proposals. The clerk shall attach to the assessment and diagram a certified copy of the resolution. The legislative body may thereupon proceed pursuant to this chapter.
