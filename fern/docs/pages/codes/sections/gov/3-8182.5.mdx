## Text

The authority shall, in cooperation with the City of Sacramento, prepare an appropriate plan for the development and redevelopment of that portion of the project area set forth in paragraph (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 8180 (the R Street Area), that furthers the purposes and objectives set forth in the Capitol Area Plan. The plan shall be approved after published notice and at least one public hearing by the authority.
