## Text

The county succeeds to all of the rights of the city in the debts and may collect or sue for them in the name of the county.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 541, Sec. 3. Effective September 9, 1985. Operative January 1, 1986, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 541.)
