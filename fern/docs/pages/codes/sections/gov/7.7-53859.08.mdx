## Text

A grant or loan anticipation note or notes shall not be issued pursuant to this article in an amount at any time outstanding which shall exceed 95 percent of the grant or loan funds stated in writing by the granting or loaning authority as committed, appropriated and that shall be paid on a specified date or dates, or event or events, within a 36-month period from the dating of such notes.
