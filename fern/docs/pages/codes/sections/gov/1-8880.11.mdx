## Text

“Lottery Commission” or “Commission”

“Lottery Commission” or “Commission” means the five members appointed by the Governor pursuant to this Chapter to oversee the Lottery and the Director.

## History

(History: Added November 6, 1984, by initiative Proposition 37.)
