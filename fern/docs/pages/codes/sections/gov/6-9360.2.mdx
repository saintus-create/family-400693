## Text

As used in this chapter, “disability” and “incapacity for performance of duty” as a basis of retirement, mean disability of permanent or extended and uncertain duration, as determined by the board on the basis of competent medical opinion.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1109.)
