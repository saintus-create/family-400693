## Text

“Domestic partner” means an adult in a domestic partnership, as defined in Section 22771, with an employee or annuitant of an employer subject to this part, who is eligible for enrollment pursuant to Section 22818.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
