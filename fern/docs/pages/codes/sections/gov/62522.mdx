## Text

An authority shall only have the power to exercise the land use authority and set development parameters as provided in Section 62520 if the authority includes the underlying land use jurisdiction. If an authority includes territory of multiple land use jurisdictions, each land use jurisdiction shall be a member of the authority before the authority can exercise these powers. Nothing in this title shall be construed to authorize an authority to exercise these powers beyond the specific circumstances described in Section 62520.
