## Text

Not later than 30 days after the close of each month there shall be placed on file for public inspection during business hours at the offices of the Controller, Treasurer and Director of Finance:

(a) A report by the Treasurer with respect to investments made under this article and deposits in banks and savings and loan associations showing transactions during such month and any related information the board deems should be included; and

(b) A copy of each designation made by the board under this article.
