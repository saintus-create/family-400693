## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of the Emergency Management Assistance Compact, as set forth in Section 179.5, the state shall not deploy any personnel under the compact to render aid to a party state for any conditions resulting from a labor controversy, and the state shall not receive aid from a party state for conditions resulting from a labor controversy.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 414, Sec. 1. Effective October 10, 2007. Inoperative March 1, 2038, pursuant to Section 179.9. Repealed as of January 1, 2039, pursuant to Section 179.9.)
