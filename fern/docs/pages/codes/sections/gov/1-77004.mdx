## Text

As used in this chapter, “option county” means a county which has adopted the provisions of this chapter for the current fiscal year.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 945, Sec. 10. Effective September 16, 1988. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 77400.)
