## Text

Monthly survivors’ allowances shall be based upon the following table:MonthlyMember’s survivorsallowancesSurviving spouse caring for one child $591.80 Surviving spouse caring for two or more children 690.40One child only 295.90Two children only 581.80Three or more children 690.40Widow or widower age 62 (no child) 327.10Each of two dependent parents at age 62 295.90Sole dependent parent at age 62 325.50Lump sum payment 255.00
