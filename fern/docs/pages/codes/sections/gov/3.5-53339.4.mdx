## Text

The clerk of the legislative body shall give notice of the hearing in the same manner and within the same time as provided for the giving of notice of a hearing on a resolution of intention to establish a community facilities district, as required by Section 53322. Notice pursuant to Section 53322.4 may be mailed to the registered voters and landowners within the territory proposed to be annexed or proposed to be annexed in the future.

The notice shall do all of the following:

(a) Contain the text or a summary of the resolution.

(b) State the time and place for the hearing.

(c) State that at the hearing the testimony of all interested persons for or against the annexation of territory or the future annexation of territory to the community facilities district or the levying of special taxes within the territory proposed to be annexed or proposed to be annexed in the future will be heard.
