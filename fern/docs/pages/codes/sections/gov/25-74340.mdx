## Text

This article applies to the municipal court established in a district embracing that portion of the City of San Diego not included within the South Bay Municipal Court District.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 447. Effective January 1, 2003. Repealed as of January 1, 2074, pursuant to Section 74343.)
