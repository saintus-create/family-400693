## Text

This chapter shall be interpreted in a manner that will promote the security of the bond holders and the expeditious acquisition, construction, renovation, or improvement of correctional facilities.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 932, Sec. 2. Effective September 25, 1985.)
