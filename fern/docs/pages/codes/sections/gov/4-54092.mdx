## Text

Any city, county, or other local agency that allows any property owned, operated, or controlled by it to be used as a means of access to any public beach shall allow free access over that property to all persons regardless of ancestry, residence, or any characteristic listed or defined in Section 11135.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 568, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 2008.)
