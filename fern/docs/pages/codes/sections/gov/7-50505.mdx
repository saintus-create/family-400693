## Text

Unless the power to proceed has ceased, at the conclusion of the hearing and on a determination of all questions arising, the legislative body shall declare its finding that the owners of more than one-half of the area of the property in the assessment district created for the purchase or improvement of the property have not made written protest to the leasing of the property. The finding shall be declared by resolution entered in the minutes.
