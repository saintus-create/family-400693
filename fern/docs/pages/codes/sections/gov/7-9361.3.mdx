## Text

Optional Settlement 2 consists of the right to have a retirement allowance paid him or her until his or her death and thereafter to his or her beneficiary for life.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 93. Effective January 1, 2003.)
