## Text

If a nonmember, as defined in Section 21291, withdraws accumulated contributions in accordance with Section 21292, the member may redeposit those contributions pursuant to this article.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 855, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
