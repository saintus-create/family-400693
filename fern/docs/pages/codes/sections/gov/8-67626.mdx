## Text

The authority may cooperate and contract with the United States of America under any act of Congress heretofore or hereafter enacted authorizing or permitting such cooperation. The authority may enter into any contract, arrangement, or agreement with, and may cooperate with the Department of Transportation or any other department or agency of the State of California or any public corporation for the development, construction, improvement, or maintenance of the regional transit terminal or any part thereof.
