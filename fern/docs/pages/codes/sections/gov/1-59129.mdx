## Text

Wherever the State, or any county, city, school district, public corporation, or political subdivision owns or has an interest in lands within a district whose indebtedness is proposed to be refunded, it has the same right as any private landowner to appear and be heard in the proceedings or in any suit on them. All the curative clauses of this chapter apply to it.
