## Text

The ordinance shall provide that any person possessing all the minimum qualifications for the position is eligible to take any civil service examination, regardless of age, and that the commission, the personnel officer, or any appointing power shall not adopt any rule prohibiting the employment of any person, otherwise qualified, in any city employment solely because of age.
