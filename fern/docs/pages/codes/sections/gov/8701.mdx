## Text

The following definitions shall govern the construction of this chapter:

(a) “Office” means the Office of California-Mexico Affairs.

(b) “Conference” means the Southwest Border Regional Conference.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 77, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2006.)
