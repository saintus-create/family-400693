## Text

Title to all property acquired by the authority and the revenues and income therefrom, is in the name of the authority. The title to any moneys, revenues, sinking funds, reserve funds and other funds of the authority and the income thereof pledged to the payment of the principal or any interest on any bonds issued hereunder is subject to the trusts hereby created in favor of the bondholders. The management, operation and control of the regional transit terminal and of every part thereof acquired, constructed or completed by the authority shall be vested in the authority; provided, however, that any indenture may contain provisions with respect to the exercise thereof.
