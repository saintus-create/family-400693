## Text

Price, fitness, and quality being equal, any body, officer, or other person charged with the purchase, or permitted or authorized to purchase supplies for the use of the state, or of any of its institutions or offices, or for the use of any county or city shall always prefer supplies manufactured or produced in the state, and shall next prefer supplies partially manufactured or produced in the state.
