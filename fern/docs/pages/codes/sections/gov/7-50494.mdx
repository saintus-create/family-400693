## Text

Upon receiving the petition, the legislative body may adopt a resolution of intention to lease the property according to the terms of the petition. The resolution of intention is sufficient if it states in general terms:

(a) The terms of the petition.

(b) The location of the property.

(c) The purpose for which the property is proposed to be leased.
