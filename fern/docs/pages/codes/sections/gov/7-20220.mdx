## Text

The board shall, on the request of a board of retirement of a county retirement system supply information and data necessary for administration of that system as it is affected by membership in and service credited under this system.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 906, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1997.)
