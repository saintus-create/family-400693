## Text

In order to carry out the provisions and purposes of this article, a city selection committee shall formulate and adopt rules and regulations to govern the conduct of their meetings and the selection of city representatives. Such rules and regulations shall include, but not be limited to, the term of office of the chairman and vice chairman of the city selection committee, such term to be not less than one year nor more than four years, the time and place of the committee’s regular meetings, a procedure for nominating and selecting city representatives, and the manner in which voting by the city selection committee shall be conducted.
