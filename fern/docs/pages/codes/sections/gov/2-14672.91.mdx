## Text

Notwithstanding Section 14670, the Director of General Services, with the consent of the Department of Corrections, may lease to the County of Humboldt a 5.5-acre portion of the property situated in the County of Humboldt known as the Eel River Conservation Camp for a period not to exceed 20 years for purposes of operating a refuse disposal facility.
