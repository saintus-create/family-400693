## Text

At least 20 days prior to the adoption of the state transportation improvement program, the executive director shall make available to the commission, the department, and the transportation planning agencies and county transportation commissions the recommendations of the staff on the program.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 95, Sec. 7.)
