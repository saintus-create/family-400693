## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the Department of Finance may allocate and charge a fair share of the administrative costs to all funds directly. The Department of Finance shall certify to the Controller the amount determined to be the fair share of administrative costs due and payable from each state fund. The Department of Finance, at any time during the year, may direct the Controller to advance a reasonable amount for administrative costs from a fund designated in accordance with Section 11271. Upon order of the Department of Finance of the timing and the amounts to be transferred, the Controller shall transfer the amount of the administrative costs from special and nongovernmental cost funds to the Central Service Cost Recovery Fund or the General Fund.
