## Text

Any employer that fails to withhold and submit an employee’s normal contributions required by this chapter within the applicable time limitations shall notify the system and shall take no action until authorized by the system.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 130, Sec. 23. (AB 966) Effective January 1, 2010.)
