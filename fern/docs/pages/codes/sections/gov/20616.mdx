## Text

Sections 20469, 20470, 20502, 20512, 20570, 20571, and 20572 do not apply to contracts made pursuant to this chapter. The county superintendent of schools shall have no authority to exercise any election under any provision of this part, other than Section 21623.6, that applies to a contracting agency only on its election to be subject to it.
