## Text

On and after the effective date of the consolidation, all ordinances, if any, of the successor city shall have full effect throughout the successor city.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 541, Sec. 3. Effective September 9, 1985. Operative January 1, 1986, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 541.)
