## Text

“Policeman” as used in this part also includes persons employed in the classification listing in Section 20411 for the purposes of Section 218(d)(5)(A) of the Social Security Act.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 678, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1999.)
