## Text

As used in this article, “petition” means any petition to which a registered voter has affixed the voter’s own signature.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
