## Text

“State agency” means every state office, department, division, bureau, board and commission, and the Legislature.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 727, Sec. 6. Operative July 1, 1985, by Sec. 12 of Ch. 727. Note: This section was added on June 4, 1974, by initiative Prop. 9.)
