## Text

Unless the context otherwise requires, the definitions contained in this chapter govern the construction of this part. The definition of a word applies to any variants thereof and the singular tense of a word includes the plural.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1459, Sec. 1.)
