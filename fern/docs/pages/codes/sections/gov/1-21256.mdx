## Text

Any allowance may be paid directly to a trust. A trustee of the trust shall have the right to make tax withholding elections and to change the address for annuitant payments and correspondence.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
