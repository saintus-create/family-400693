## Text

This chapter shall not affect bonds approved by the voters of the state or local government issuer prior to the effective date of this chapter, to the extent that this chapter is inconsistent with the measure authorizing those bonds.

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1389, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1986.)
