## Text

A successor agency, pursuant to Section 20508, for the El Dorado County Fire Protection District and the Diamond Springs-El Dorado Fire Protection District may provide employees the defined benefit plan or formula that those employees received from their respective employer prior to the annexation.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 888, Sec. 1. (SB 1240) Effective September 28, 2024.)
