## Text

The Attorney General shall lease facilities and equipment to connect the State Teletype System with the sheriff’s office in the counties of:

(a) Alpine.

(b) Del Norte.

(c) Lake.

(d) Lassen.

(e) Nevada.

(f) Plumas.

(g) Sierra.
