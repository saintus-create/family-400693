## Text

The El Dorado County Transportation Planning Agency is hereby created, as a local area planning agency, and not as a part of the executive branch of the state government, to provide regional transportation planning for the area of El Dorado County, exclusive of the Tahoe Basin. The agency may be known by any other name it chooses.
