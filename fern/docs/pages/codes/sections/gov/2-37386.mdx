## Text

A city may lease such tide and submerged lands and uplands for:

(a) Industrial uses.

(b) Improvement and development of city harbors.

(c) Construction and maintenance of wharves, docks, piers, or bulkhead piers.

(d) Other public uses consistent with the requirements of commerce or navigation in city harbors.
