## Text

Property sold hereunder may not be sold for less than the amount of the judgment plus postjudgment interest and authorized costs without the consent of the owners of 75 percent by value of the outstanding bonds.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1365, Sec. 23.)
