## Text

County commissioners and employees of the county commission shall not be individually liable in any way to any person for any good faith activity of the county commission, county commissioners, or employees.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 229, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
