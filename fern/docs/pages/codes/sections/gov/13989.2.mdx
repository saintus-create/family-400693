## Text

For the purposes of this chapter the following definitions shall apply:

(a) “Peer-reviewed manuscript” means a manuscript after it has been peer reviewed and in the form in which it has been accepted for publication in a scientific journal.

(b) “Research grant” means a grant to a researcher that is provided in whole or in part by a state agency.

(c) “State agency” has the same meaning as defined in Section 10295.1 of the Public Contract Code
