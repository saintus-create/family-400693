## Text

If the original board is not selected on or before January 1, 1990, this chapter shall cease to be operative.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1327, Sec. 1. Note: Condition for termination of Chapter 5.5 (commencing with Section 6599.01) failed.)
