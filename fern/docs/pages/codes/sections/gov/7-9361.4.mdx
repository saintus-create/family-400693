## Text

Optional Settlement 3 consists of the right to have a retirement allowance paid him or her until his or her death, and thereafter to have one-half of his or her retirement allowance paid to his or her beneficiary for life.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 93.1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
