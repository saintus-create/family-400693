## Text

(a) An intermittent, part-time interpreter may not be an employee of more than one trial court, but may accept appointments to provide services to more than one trial court through cross-assignments.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2025.

## History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 8) and added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 556, Sec. 9. (AB 1032) Effective January 1, 2024. Operative January 1, 2025, by its own provisions.)
