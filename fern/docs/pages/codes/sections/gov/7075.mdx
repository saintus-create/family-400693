## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall preclude a county or local municipality from implementing additional requirements and standards related to the purchase, use, and reporting of military equipment by local law enforcement agencies.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 406, Sec. 2. (AB 481) Effective January 1, 2022.)
