## Text

Each person who is a member of the system pursuant to Section 9355.45 shall contribute 612 percent of his or her compensation for each year of service rendered as a member of the system. Those contributions shall be paid as provided in Section 9357.15.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 90.9. Effective January 1, 2003.)
