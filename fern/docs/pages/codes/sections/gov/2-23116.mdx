## Text

The boundaries of Kings County are as follows:

12 miles, more or less, to the point where said township line intersects the center line of the main channel of Kings River; thence northeasterly and easterly following the meander of the said center line of the main channel of Kings River to the point where said center line intersects the boundary line between Fresno and Kings; thence northeasterly along said boundary line to the corner common to Tulare, Fresno, and Kings; thence east along the fourth standard parallel line south, M. D. B. & M., to the point of beginning.
