## Text

“Lottery Contractor”

“Lottery Contractor” means a person or organization with whom the Lottery has contracted for the purpose of providing goods and services required by the Lottery.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 917, Sec. 1. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
