## Text

After the adoption by the committee of any resolution in conformity with Section 16731, 16731.5, or 16731.6, the Treasurer shall arrange for the preparation of the requisite number of suitable bonds or notes, in accordance with the resolution, and in so doing shall arrange for the imprinting thereon of the matters necessary and appropriate thereto after the sale of the bonds and the awarding thereof to the successful bidder.
