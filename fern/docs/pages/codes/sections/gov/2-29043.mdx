## Text

The auditor shall provide the estimates for bonded debt service requirements. The auditor shall also provide or furnish to the responsible authority, as applicable, the estimates for bonded debt service requirements of:

(a) School districts.

(b) Any special district, the records for which are maintained in the auditor’s office as required by law.
