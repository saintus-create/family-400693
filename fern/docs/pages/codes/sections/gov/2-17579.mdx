## Text

Any bill introduced or amended for which the Legislative Counsel has determined the bill will mandate a new program or higher level of service pursuant to Section 6 of Article XIIIB of the California Constitution shall contain a section specifying that reimbursement shall be made pursuant to this chapter or that the mandate is being disclaimed and the reason therefor.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 890, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 2005.)
