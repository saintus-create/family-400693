## Text

If the board of supervisors calls any special election within all or any part of any district, any references in the principal act to the board of directors of the district and to the clerk or secretary of the district shall be deemed to mean the board of supervisors and the elections official, respectively.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 47, Sec. 7. (AB 2795) Effective January 1, 2011.)
