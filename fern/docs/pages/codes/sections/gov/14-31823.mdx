## Text

Actions brought by the board under this article shall be commenced within three years after the liability of the retirement system to pay benefits is fixed. Liability of the retirement system is fixed at the time the board approves the payment of benefits under this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 159, Sec. 1.)
