## Text

The commission shall make its own provision for necessary quarters, equipment, and supplies as well as personnel. The commission may choose to contract with any public agency or private party for personnel and facilities.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 73. Effective January 1, 2001.)
