## Text

“Policeman,” as used in this part, also includes persons designated as peace officers by subdivision (e) of Section 20391 for the purposes of Section 218(d)(5)(A) of Title 42 of the United States Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 785, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2000.)
