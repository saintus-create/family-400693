## Text

In San Joaquin County, each superior court reporter shall be paid a salary to be established by the San Joaquin County Board of Supervisors.

The number of superior court reporters shall not be less than the number of San Joaquin County Superior Court judicial positions.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 703, Sec. 2.5.)
