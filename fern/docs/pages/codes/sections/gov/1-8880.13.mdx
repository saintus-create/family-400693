## Text

“Lottery Game Retailer”

“Lottery Game Retailer” means a person or organization with whom the Lottery Commission may contract for the purpose of selling tickets or shares in Lottery Games to the public.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 55, Sec. 3. Effective April 16, 1986. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
