## Text

The members of an agency qualified to vote on any question may vote by mail or another appropriate method.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 938, Sec. 50. Effective January 1, 1996. Operative July 1, 1997, by Sec. 98 of Ch. 938.)
