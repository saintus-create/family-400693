## Text

In the event the participant dies without a valid beneficiary designation on file, any balance remaining in the participant’s account shall be payable to the participant’s survivors in the following order:

(a) The participant’s spouse.

(b) The participant’s natural or adopted children.

(c) The participant’s parents.

(d) The participant’s estate.
