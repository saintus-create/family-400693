## Text

The Secretary of California Housing and Homelessness may develop programs for technical and fiscal assistance to facilitate nonprofit, self-help community vegetable gardens and related supporting activities.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 28, Sec. 48. (SB 170) Effective June 29, 2026. Operative July 1, 2026, pursuant to Section 14477.)
