## Text

The clerk of the local agency shall cause the notice, protest, and hearing procedures to comply with Section 53753. The mailed notice shall also contain the name and telephone number of the person designated by the legislative body to answer inquiries regarding the protest proceedings.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 262, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2001.)
