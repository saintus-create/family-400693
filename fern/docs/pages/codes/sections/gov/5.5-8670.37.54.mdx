## Text

(a) For the purposes of this chapter, financial responsibility may be demonstrated by evidence of insurance, surety bond, letter of credit, qualifications as a self-insurer, or any combination thereof or other evidence of financial responsibility.

(b) In adopting requirements under this article, the administrator may specify policy or other contractual terms, conditions, or defenses which are necessary or which are unacceptable in establishing evidence of financial responsibility, in order to effectuate the purposes of this article.
