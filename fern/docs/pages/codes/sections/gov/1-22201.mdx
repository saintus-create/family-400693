## Text

The board shall, upon application by any public agency except the state, in accordance with the provisions of this part execute on behalf of the state an agreement with the federal agency for the coverage of employees of such public agency under the federal system in conformity with the provisions of Section 218 of the Social Security Act and applicable federal regulations.
