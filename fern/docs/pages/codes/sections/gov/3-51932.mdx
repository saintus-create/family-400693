## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, the election, if a special election, shall be conducted as other special elections in the county or, if a general election, as other general elections in the county. Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the analysis of the proposed charter, prepared pursuant to Section 9160 of the Elections Code, shall not exceed 1,500 words.
