## Text

The board may adopt an amortization period of 40 years for any unfunded actuarial liability for the benefits applicable to all state miscellaneous members and all state peace officer/firefighter members.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
