## Text

The State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission, in consultation with the department, shall study the potential cost-effectiveness and energy efficiency of utilizing retroreflective sheeting materials on highway signs. In completing the study, that commission shall review any existing studies to the extent feasible and report its findings to the Legislature on or before May 1, 2002.

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 825, Sec. 3.5. Effective January 1, 2002.)
