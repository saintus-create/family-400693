## Text

The Department of General Services shall direct the preparation of the statutes of each session of the Legislature after each regular session. The statutes for each special session shall be cumulated and prepared with the statutes for the next succeeding regular session, except that upon the written request of the Legislative Budget Committee the department shall direct the preparation of the statutes of a particular special session after such special session.
