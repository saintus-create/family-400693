## Text

Any provision that the authority may include in a trust agreement, indenture, or resolution pursuant to this part may alternatively be included in a bond with the same effect.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 261, Sec. 3. (SB 1463) Effective January 1, 2015.)
