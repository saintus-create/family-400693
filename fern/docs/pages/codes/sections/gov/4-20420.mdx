## Text

“Local safety member” includes all local police officers, local sheriffs, firefighters, safety officers, county peace officers, and school safety members, employed by a contracting agency who have by contract been included within this system.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 871, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
