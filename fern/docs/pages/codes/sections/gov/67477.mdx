## Text

A business that is located within the site shall be eligible for any applicable tax benefit as provided in Section 17049 or Section 23005 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 861, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999. Conditionally operative as prescribed by Stats. 1998, Ch. 861, Sec. 5.)
