## Text

No fee which is greater than the actual cost incurred shall be charged by any county, city, city and county, or special district for media production which occurs entirely on private property.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 302, Sec. 20. (AB 3256) Effective January 1, 2019.)
