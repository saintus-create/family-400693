## Text

“Medicare health benefit plan” means a health benefit plan that provides benefits in coordination with Medicare Parts A and B, including, but not limited to, a managed Medicare health benefit plan providing coverage through the Medicare+Choice program or a Medicare supplement health benefit plan that provides coverage in coordination with the traditional Medicare program.
