## Text

The department may accept for distribution or disposal documents and other publications from the Legislature, the Governor, or the elective officers of the state, when requested to do so.

The department shall fix the price and dispose of or sell the documents and publications.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
