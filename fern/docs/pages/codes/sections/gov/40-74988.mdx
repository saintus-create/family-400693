## Text

The marshal and employees of the office of the marshal who provide court security services, except reserve deputy marshals, are employees of the Shasta County Superior Court for all purposes.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 496. Effective January 1, 2003.)
