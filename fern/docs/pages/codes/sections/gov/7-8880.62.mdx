## Text

Funds shall be disbursed from the State Lottery Fund by the Controller for any of the purposes authorized by this chapter.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 764, Sec. 13. Effective November 14, 2008. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
