## Text

The commission may include capacity-increasing projects in the adopted state transportation improvement program adopted pursuant to Section 14529 only if the project studies report has been completed for that project pursuant to Section 65086.5.

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 878, Sec. 1.)
