## Text

A member may not secure employment with, or be employed by, bond underwriters, bond counsel, security brokerages or dealers, or financial services firms, with whom the treasurer is doing business during the period that the person is a member of the committee or for one year after leaving the committee.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 32, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
