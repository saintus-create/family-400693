## Text

This chapter is intended to provide a full, fair, flexible, and workable means of utilizing the state ceiling available under the Internal Revenue Code, and it shall be liberally construed and implemented to achieve those purposes.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 943, Sec. 2.5.)
