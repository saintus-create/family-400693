## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that, prior to the appropriation of any moneys for purposes of Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 15820), Chapter 3.6 (commencing with Section 15820.15), Chapter 3.7 (commencing with Section 15820.30), or Chapter 3.8 (commencing with Section 15820.50), consideration be given to funding those purposes from general obligation bond proceeds or from the Capital Outlay Fund for Public Higher Education.
