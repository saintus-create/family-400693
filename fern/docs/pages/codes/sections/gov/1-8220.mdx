## Text

The Secretary of State may adopt rules and regulations to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

The regulations shall be adopted in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3).

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 183, Sec. 137. Effective January 1, 2005.)
