## Text

A local agency may retain engineers, architects, accountants, financial consultants, bond counsel, or other services as may be necessary in the judgment of the legislative body for the construction or financing of construction of rental housing for employees.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1092, Sec. 1.)
