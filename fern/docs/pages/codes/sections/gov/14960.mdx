## Text

Without at the time furnishing vouchers and itemized statements, the Department of General Services may withdraw from the Architecture Revolving Fund not to exceed at any one time the sum of five hundred thousand dollars ($500,000). The sums withdrawn under this section shall be used as a revolving fund where payments of compensation earned or cash advances are necessary with respect to the construction, alteration, repair, or improvement of state buildings.
