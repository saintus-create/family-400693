## Text

On or before January 30 of each year, each city or county in which any agricultural preserve is located shall provide the Department with geographical information system (GIS) data files of all agricultural preserves and Williamson Act contracted land in existence at the end of the preceding year.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 644, Sec. 3. (SB 574) Effective January 1, 2022.)
