## Text

Notwithstanding Section 13340, there is hereby continuously appropriated, without regard to fiscal years, from the retirement fund to the board, the amount necessary to pay for any insurance obtained pursuant to Section 7511, which payments shall be made upon warrants drawn by the Controller upon demands made by the board.
