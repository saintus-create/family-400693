## Text

The Director of General Services shall appoint an architect to be a professional adviser to the department for purposes of this chapter. The professional adviser, after consultation with the State Architect and the Director of Finance, shall formulate the architectural program of the department under this chapter together with such limitations on the program as are deemed advisable.
