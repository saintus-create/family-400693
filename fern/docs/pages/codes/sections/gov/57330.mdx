## Text

Any territory annexed to a city or district shall be subject to the levying or fixing and collection of any previously authorized taxes, benefit assessments, fees, or charges of the city or district.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1195, Sec. 14.7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
