## Text

The contribution of a contracting agency described in Section 20834 in respect to miscellaneous members is increased by a sum equal to 0.08 percent of the compensation paid to those members by the employer.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
