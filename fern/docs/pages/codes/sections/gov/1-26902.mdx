## Text

“Money payable into the county treasury,” as used in Sections 26900 and 26901, includes money belonging to estates of deceased persons and required by law to be paid to the county treasurer, taxes on inheritances and transfers, any money deposited by order of court, and any other money deposited with the county treasurer by virtue of any official authority whatever.
