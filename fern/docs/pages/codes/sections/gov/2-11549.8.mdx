## Text

As used in this article, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Director” means the Director of the Office of Privacy Protection.

(b) “Office” means the Office of Privacy Protection.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 404, Sec. 34. (AB 2408) Effective January 1, 2011.)
