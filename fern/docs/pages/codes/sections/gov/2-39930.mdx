## Text

As used in this article:

(a) “Navigable water” includes a harbor, bay, inlet, and estuary.

(b) “Tidelands” includes submerged lands.

(c) “Owns tidelands” means by grant from the State, or otherwise, owns, holds, or possesses tidelands or the title to such lands.
