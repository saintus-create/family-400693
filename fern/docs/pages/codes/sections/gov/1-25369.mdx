## Text

By a four-fifths vote of the board of supervisors, any county in which a local hospital district including the entire county has been organized may grant any money accumulated in a capital outlay fund pursuant to Article 4 (commencing with Section 53730) of Chapter 4, Part 1, Division 2, Title 5 of the Government Code to the local hospital district.
