## Text

As used in this chapter, “legislative body” means the city council or board of trustees in the case of a city, the board of supervisors in the case of a county or a city and county, or the board of directors, trustees, or governors or other governing body in the case of a special district or any other municipal or public corporation or district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 487, Sec. 3. Effective July 10, 1982.)
