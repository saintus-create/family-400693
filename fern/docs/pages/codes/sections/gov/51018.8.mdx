## Text

The State Fire Marshal may issue orders directing compliance with this chapter or any regulations adopted pursuant thereto. The State Fire Marshal shall specify in the order the particular action which is required of the person issued the order.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1222, Sec. 18. Effective September 30, 1983.)
