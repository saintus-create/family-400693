## Text

The nonparticipant spouse shall have the right to a lump sum distribution of the amount awarded to the nonparticipant spouse by the judgment or court order.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
