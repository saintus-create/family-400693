## Text

(a) Pursuant to Section 14670, the Director of General Services, with the consent of the agency concerned, may let for a period not to exceed five years state land for apiary purposes.

(b) The director may lease state land pursuant to this section for less than fair market rent if the lease for apiary purposes does not require exclusive use of the land.
