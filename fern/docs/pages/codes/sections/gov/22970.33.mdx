## Text

The board may require a third-party administrator, recordkeeper, custodian, or investment manager that is contracted with, or appointed by, the system to be subject to the duties set forth in Section 24032.

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
