## Text

The general design and the details of the Seal of the Governor of the State of California, excluding colors, shall correspond substantially with the following representation thereof:

* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * *

NOTICE OF INCOMPLETE TEXT: The Governor’s Seal appears in the hard-copy publication of the chaptered bill. See Chapter 963, page 2203, Statutes of 1957.

* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * *

The roman numerals shall designate the sequence in office of the Governor.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 963. Note: The Governor's Seal appears in Ch. 963 on page 2203 in Vol. 1 of the 1957 Statutes publication, but current roman numerals are XL.)
