## Text

Appointments by the speaker; confirmation by Assembly Committee on Rules

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, all statutory appointments delegated to the Speaker of the Assembly are subject to confirmation by the Assembly Committee on Rules, two thirds of the membership thereof concurring.
