## Text

The director shall transmit any declaration made under this article to the Department of Finance with a recommendation and request that money be allocated from any available money appropriated for that purpose or to meet State emergencies within the meaning of that term as employed in this article, in order to carry out the work and remedial measures required to meet the emergency.
