## Text

No notice, agenda, announcement, or report required under this article need identify any victim or alleged victim of crime, tortious sexual conduct, or child abuse unless the identity of the person has been publicly disclosed.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 949, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1998.)
