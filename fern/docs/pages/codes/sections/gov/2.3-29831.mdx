## Text

As used in this chapter, “committee” means the chief administrative officer, auditor, and treasurer, or any other county employee appointed by the board of supervisors.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1191, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1992.)
