## Text

The Controller may establish procedures whereby some or all of the payments between funds and appropriations within a state agency and between funds and appropriations of different state agencies may be made by transfers upon his accounts in lieu of making such payments by claims and warrants. Corresponding entries shall be made on the accounts of the agency or agencies concerned.
