## Text

Under conditions established by the board, the system may periodically bill the Military Department for reimbursement of the administrative and program costs of administering the membership and service credit of National Guard members.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 355, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2008.)
