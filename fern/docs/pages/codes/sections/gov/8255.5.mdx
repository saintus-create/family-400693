## Text

The following definitions govern this chapter:

(a) “Commission” means the Commission on Asian and Pacific Islander American Affairs.

(b) “APIA” means Asian and Pacific Islander American.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 716, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
