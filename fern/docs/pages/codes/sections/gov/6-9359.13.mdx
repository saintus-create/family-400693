## Text

Notwithstanding any contrary provision of Section 9359.1 the retirement allowance of a member who is an elective officer of the state whose office is provided for by the Constitution, other than a judge or a Member of the Senate or Assembly, first elected to any such office after the effective date of this section and any allowance payable to a survivor of such member on death before or after retirement shall not be based on compensation in excess of the highest compensation received by the member as an incumbent of such office. Allowances payable to such members or survivors shall be adjusted under Section 9360.10 rather than 9360.9.
