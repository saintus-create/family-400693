## Text

(a) Nothing in this chapter limits, restricts, or prohibits a county from complying with Article 8 (commencing with Section 54220) of Chapter 5 of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5, and Section 65402.

(b) This section does not constitute a change in, but is declaratory of, existing law.
