## Text

The Department of General Services shall offer copies of the California State Contracts Register for sale, and the “price” thereof shall not exceed the amount necessary to pay all expenses incurred in its preparation and distribution.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 823, Sec. 3.5.)
