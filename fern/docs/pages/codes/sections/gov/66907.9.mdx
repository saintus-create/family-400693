## Text

The conservancy shall take whatever actions are reasonably necessary and incidental to the management of lands and facilities under its ownership or control. In order to carry out the purposes of this title, the conservancy may do all of the following:

(a) Adopt and enforce regulations governing the use of those lands and facilities.

(b) Initiate, negotiate, and participate in agreements for the management of those lands and facilities with a public agency, a corporate entity, an individual, a partnership, or other entity.

(c) Enter into any other agreement authorized by state or federal law.
