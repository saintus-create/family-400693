## Text

The California State Auditor, in connection with any audit or investigation conducted pursuant to this chapter, shall be deemed to be a department head for the purposes of Section 11189.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 281, Sec. 31. (SB 1395) Effective January 1, 2013.)
