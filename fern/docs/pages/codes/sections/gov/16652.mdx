## Text

Whenever it appears to the Treasurer, upon competent proof, that any bonds, coupons, or other evidences of state indebtedness, except warrants, have been lost or destroyed, he shall endeavor to stop payment on them, and notify the State’s fiscal agents to endeavor to stop payment thereon.
