## Text

The Department of General Services shall have a seal, bearing the following inscription: “Department of General Services.” The seal shall be fixed to all writs and authentications of copies of records and to other instruments that the department directs.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 135. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
