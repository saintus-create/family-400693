## Text

At the hearing any person interested, including persons owning property within the area, may appear and present any matters material to the questions set forth in the resolution declaring the necessity for incurring the bonded indebtedness.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1451, Sec. 1.)
