## Text

“Reduced worktime for partial service retirement” means any arrangement of worktime agreeable to both the public agency employer and the employee that aggregates no less, on a monthly basis, than a 20 percent reduction nor more than a 60 percent reduction from what would in that position be considered full-time employment, combined with the concurrent payment of proportionally reduced compensation and proportionally reduced retirement benefits.
