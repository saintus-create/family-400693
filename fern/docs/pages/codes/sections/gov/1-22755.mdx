## Text

The provisions of this part shall become operative with respect to employees and annuitants of the University of California upon filing with the board a resolution adopted by the Regents of the University of California electing to be subject to the provisions of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
