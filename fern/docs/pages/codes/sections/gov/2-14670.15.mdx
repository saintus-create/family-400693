## Text

Notwithstanding Section 14670, the Director of General Services, with the consent of the State Department of State Hospitals, may lease to Napa County for a period not to exceed 60 years, 32,632 square feet, on the east of Residence 09, building 225, facing Imola Street between Shurleft and Tejas Street, within the boundaries of Napa State Hospital for establishment of an independent living facility for persons who are mentally ill, persons who are handicapped, or persons who have low income. The lease shall be under terms and conditions determined by the director to be in the best interests of the state.
