## Text

All meetings conducted by the board pertaining to salaries of county employees shall be open and public, except as provided in the Ralph M. Brown Act, Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2, including Section 54957.6.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 939, Sec. 3. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative January 1, 1995, by Sec. 29 of Ch. 939.)
