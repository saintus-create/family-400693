## Text

If at any time between each decennial federal census, a city annexes or detaches territory or consolidates with another city, the city council of the city annexing or detaching the territory or the city council of the successor city, may reexamine the boundaries of its council districts pursuant to Section 21603 or 21623 of the Elections Code, as applicable.
