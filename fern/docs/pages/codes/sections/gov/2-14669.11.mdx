## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Director of General Services may sell or exchange, based on current market value and upon any terms and conditions, and with any reservations and exceptions, deemed by the director to be in the state’s best interests, the state-owned property consisting of one city block, bounded by Jackson, Alice, 11th, and 12th streets in the City of Oakland. The net proceeds, if any, from the sale or exchange shall be deposited in the General Fund and be available for appropriation in accordance with Section 15863 of the Government Code.
