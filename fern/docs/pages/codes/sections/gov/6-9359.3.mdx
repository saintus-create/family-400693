## Text

The right of a person to any benefit or other right under this chapter and the money in the Legislators’ Retirement Fund are not subject to execution or any other process whatsoever except to the extent permitted by Section 704.110 of the Code of Civil Procedure, and are unassignable except as specifically provided in this chapter.
