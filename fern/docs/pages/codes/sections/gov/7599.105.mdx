## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2040, and as of that date is repealed.

## History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 7599.104 by Stats. 2025, Ch. 213, Sec. 4. (SB 227) Effective October 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2040, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 34, commencing with Section 7599.100.)
