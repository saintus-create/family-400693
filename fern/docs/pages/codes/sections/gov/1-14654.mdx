## Text

All public property, real or personal, of the Department of Finance or the Department of Transportation used principally or primarily in carrying out of any function, or acquired in connection with the exercise of any function, which function is transferred to the Department of General Services, is transferred to the Department of General Services.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 120.)
