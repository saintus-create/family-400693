## Text

The Attorney General, in consultation with the administrator, shall undertake actions to recover all costs to the funds from any responsible party for an oil spill into waters of the state for which expenditures are made from the fund. The recovery of costs pursuant to this section shall not foreclose the Attorney General from any other actions allowed by law.
