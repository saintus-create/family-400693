## Text

The Advisory Committee shall provide the board advice and assistance in determining the health insurance coverage needs of the participating public agencies and in the adoption of rules and regulations.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 199, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
