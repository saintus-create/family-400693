## Text

(a) Any contributions made by the participant to the plan shall be credited to the participant’s account.

(b) Contributions made by the employer on behalf of the participant shall be credited to the participant’s account.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
