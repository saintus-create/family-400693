## Text

“Divestiture of power” means the termination of the power and authority to provide particular functions or classes of services within all or part of the jurisdictional boundaries of a special district.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 47, Sec. 1. (AB 2795) Effective January 1, 2011.)
