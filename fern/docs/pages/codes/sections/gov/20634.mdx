## Text

For a university member appointed under a health sciences compensation plan of the university, “compensation” shall not include past or future supplemental payments made pursuant to any health sciences compensation plan.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
