## Text

Candidates for board seats described in subdivision (g) of Section 20090, including incumbent board members running for reelection, shall file campaign statements with the Secretary of State and the board pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 84200) of Chapter 4 of Title 9.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 440, Sec. 1. (AB 1028) Effective January 1, 2012.)
