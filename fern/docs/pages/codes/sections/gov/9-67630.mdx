## Text

The proceeds from the sale of bonds shall be paid to any bank or trust company designated as the fiscal agent, trustee, or depositary of the commission, as provided in this chapter. If a part of the cash proceeds of the sale or exchange of bonds is to be used for operating funds of the regional transit terminal, such proceeds or part thereof shall be placed in any fund designated in the indenture pursuant to which the bonds are issued.
