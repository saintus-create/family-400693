## Text

If the transferee has received notice pursuant to this chapter and has not brought the building or structure into compliance within three years of that date, the owner shall not receive payment from any state assistance program for earthquake repairs resulting from damage during an earthquake until all other applicants have been paid.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 859, Sec. 3.)
