## Text

The employer shall adopt reasonable rules for all of the following:

(a) Registering employee organizations, as defined by subdivision (c) of Section 3599.52.

(b) Determining the status of organizations as employee organizations.

(c) Identifying the officers and representatives who officially represent employee organizations.

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 313, Sec. 1. (AB 1) Effective January 1, 2024. Operative July 1, 2026, pursuant to Sec. 3599.84.)
