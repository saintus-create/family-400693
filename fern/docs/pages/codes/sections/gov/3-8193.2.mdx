## Text

The requirements of Section 8193.1 shall apply solely to the portion of the project area described in paragraph (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 8180 (the R Street Area). The requirements of Section 8193 shall apply solely to the balance of the project area, exclusive of the R Street Area.
