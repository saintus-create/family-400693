## Text

The commission shall report to the Legislature on January 15, 1986, and each January 15 thereafter, on the number of claims it denied during the preceding calendar year and the basis on which the particular claims were denied.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1459, Sec. 1.)
