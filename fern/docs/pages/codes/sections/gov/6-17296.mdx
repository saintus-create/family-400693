## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature, in enacting this article, to provide assurances to the persons who invest in the state’s registered reimbursement warrants that funds will be set aside in the Warrant Payment Fund and will be used to retire the registered reimbursement warrants at maturity.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 136, Sec. 2. Effective July 5, 1994. Repealed on or after July 1, 1996, as prescribed by Section 17296.5.)
