## Text

The administrator may, for purposes of efficiency, safety, or implementation consistency, provide for services to vessels, ports, and port users which are necessary to achieve requirements mandated pursuant to this article. The administrator may establish or authorize reimbursement for those services which do not exceed the reasonable costs incurred in implementing and administering the service.
