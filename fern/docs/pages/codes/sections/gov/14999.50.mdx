## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to help develop California’s media production industry and thereby further strengthen the state’s economy by providing surplus government-owned property to media production companies at a low cost.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 302, Sec. 21. (AB 3256) Effective January 1, 2019.)
