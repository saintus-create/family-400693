## Text

(a) The authority shall be governed by a board of directors consisting of seven members appointed as follows:

(1) Two members of the board of supervisors of the County of Placer.

(2) One member of the governing body of the North Tahoe Public Utility District.

(3) One member of the governing body of the Squaw Valley Public Services District.

(4) One member of the governing body of the Tahoe City Public Utility District.

(5) One member of the governing body of the Truckee Tahoe Airport District.

(6) One member of the governing body of the Northstar Community Services District.

(b) If any of the districts cease providing services within the boundaries of the authority, the district shall lose its membership on the board of directors and the number of members of the board of directors shall be adjusted accordingly.

(c) The initial terms of office of each member of the board of directors shall be established by the appointing authority of the member.
