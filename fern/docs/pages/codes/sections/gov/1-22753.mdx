## Text

The provisions of this part shall be controlling over any memorandum of understanding reached pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 3500) of Division 4 of Title 1, except as otherwise provided by this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
