## Text

The chairperson of the authority on its behalf shall appoint an executive director, who shall not be a member of the authority and who shall serve at the pleasure of the authority. The executive director shall receive compensation that shall be fixed by the authority.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 478, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
