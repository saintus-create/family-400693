## Text

The determination of a legislatively determined mandate pursuant to Section 17573 shall not be binding on the commission when making its determination pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 17551.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 329, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2008.)
