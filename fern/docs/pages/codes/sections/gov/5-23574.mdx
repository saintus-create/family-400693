## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law no special district which is organized within any affected county and governed by the board of supervisors thereof, shall have its territory divided or in any other way have its organization changed as a result of the county consolidation except as to proceedings taken pursuant to law subsequent to the consolidation becoming effective.
