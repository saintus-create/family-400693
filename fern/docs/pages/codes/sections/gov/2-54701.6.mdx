## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the legislative body may award contracts for the construction of housing for employees by negotiation or following the receipt of competitive bids after notice inviting such bids.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1092, Sec. 1.)
