## Text

(a) The commission is authorized to acquire by deed, purchase, lease, contract, gift, devise, or otherwise, any real or personal property, structures, rights, rights-of-way, franchises, easements, and other interests in lands located within this state necessary or convenient for the construction or operation of a project, upon terms and conditions it deems advisable, and to lease, sell, or dispose of the property or interest therein in the manner that may be necessary or desirable to carry out the objects and purposes of this title.

(b) Nothing in this division shall authorize the commission to exercise the power of eminent domain.
