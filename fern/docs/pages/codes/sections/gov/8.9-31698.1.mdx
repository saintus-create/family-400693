## Text

A member who retires from a county retirement system covered by this chapter may enroll in a vision care program offered pursuant to this article subject to meeting the eligibility requirements established for the program.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 331, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
