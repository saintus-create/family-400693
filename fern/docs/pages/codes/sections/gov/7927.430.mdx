## Text

A Judicial Council form provided to request service pursuant to Section 26666.10, and the information contained therein, is confidential and shall not be disclosed pursuant to this division.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 417, Sec. 1. (AB 2791) Effective January 1, 2023.)
