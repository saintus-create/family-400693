## Text

The bank board shall do the following:

(a) Adopt bylaws for the regulation of its affairs and the conduct of its business.

(b) Adopt an official seal.

## History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 94) by Stats. 1994, Ch. 749, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1995.)
