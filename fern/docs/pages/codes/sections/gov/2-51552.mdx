## Text

Upon delivery of the certified statement from the city legislative body, the county auditor shall compute and enter in the assessment book the municipal taxes on the property enumerated and assessed as being in the city, using the rate fixed by the city legislative body and the assessed value in the assessment book, at the time and in the manner that he computes and enters county taxes.
