## Text

(a) The state’s liability under this chapter shall be limited to the amounts appropriated for this purpose.

(b) Moneys distributed to counties pursuant to this chapter may be used for any lawful purposes, and shall not be considered in the determination of eligible counties in succeeding years.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1286, Sec. 3. Effective September 28, 1987.)
