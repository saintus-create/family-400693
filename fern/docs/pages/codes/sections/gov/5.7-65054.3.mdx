## Text

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 352, Sec. 2. (AB 1511) Effective January 1, 2025. Inoperative July 1, 2028. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Repeal affects Article 5.7, commencing with Section 65054.)
