## Text

The board of supervisors shall provide for public hearings on the proposed comprehensive water resources management plan prior to the adoption thereof. After such hearings have occurred, the board of supervisors may make any changes in the comprehensive water resources management plan which may be appropriate and shall adopt such plan by resolution. The plan shall be adopted not later than January 1, 1978.
