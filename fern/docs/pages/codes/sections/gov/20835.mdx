## Text

A public agency which becomes a contracting agency on or after January 1, 1977, or which amends its contract to include the benefits provided in Sections 21624 and 21626, shall not be an employer for purpose of Section 20834, and all contributions of the contracting agency shall be held exclusively for the benefit of its employees, its retired employees, and beneficiaries of those employees and retired employees.
