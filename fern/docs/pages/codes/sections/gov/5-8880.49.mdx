## Text

A contract with a lottery game retailer shall not be assignable or transferable.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 500, Sec. 9. Effective August 17, 1992. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
