## Text

Nothing in this article shall amend, alter, or modify the duties of a county to comply with the provisions of a community redevelopment plan or an agreement with a community redevelopment agency with respect to property owned by a county within the boundaries of a community redevelopment project.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1136, Sec. 2.)
