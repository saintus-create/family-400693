## Text

“Fireman” as used in this part includes persons employed as “campus firefighter” and other persons employed in positions described in Section 20412 for the purposes of Section 218(d)(5)(A) of the Social Security Act.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 678, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 1999.)
