## Text

Neither the enactment of this chapter nor any amendment of its predecessor shortens the term of any member of the board who was lawfully elected or appointed, even if he would not be eligible for election or appointment under this chapter or such amendment, but he shall continue to be a member of the board until the expiration of the term for which he was appointed or elected.
