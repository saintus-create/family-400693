## Text

The work of the office shall be organized within the following offices or programs:

(a) CaliforniaVolunteers.

(b) The Office of Community Partnerships and Strategic Communications.

(c) The California Youth Empowerment Commission established by the California Youth Empowerment Act (Chapter 3.4 (commencing with Section 8261)).
