## Text

(a) Propositions relating to the levy of a special tax, the incurring of bonded indebtedness, or to establish or change an appropriations limit, or any combination thereof, under this chapter, may be combined into one ballot proposition as determined by the legislative body. The qualified electors for all of these purposes shall be determined and the election shall be conducted in the same manner as for a special tax election pursuant to Section 53326.

(b) The amendments of this section enacted by the Statutes of 1984 and 1991 do not constitute a change in, but are declaratory of and a clarification of, the existing law.
