## Text

The authority conferred by this chapter includes the authority to enter into any and all contracts incident to the exercise of the authority conferred by this chapter, including, without limitation, contracts for the performance of professional services.

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1389, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1986.)
