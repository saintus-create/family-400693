## Text

“Contracting agency” means an entity that meets the eligibility criteria set forth in Section 22920 that has elected to be subject to this part pursuant to Section 22922.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
