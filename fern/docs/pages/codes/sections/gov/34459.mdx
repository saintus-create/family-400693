## Text

If the voters vote in favor of the charter proposal, amendment, or repeal, it shall be deemed to be ratified, but shall not take effect until accepted and filed by the Secretary of State pursuant to Section 34460.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 357, Sec. 10.)
