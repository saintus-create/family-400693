## Text

The Attorney General shall lease facilities and equipment to connect the State Teletype System with the police department of each of the following cities:

(a) Paso Robles.

(b) Bishop.

(c) Santa Maria.

(d) Oceanside.

(e) Alameda.

(f) San Jose.
