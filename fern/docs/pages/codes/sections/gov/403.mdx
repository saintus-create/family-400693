## Text

All grants and commissions shall be kept in the name and by the authority of the people of the State of California, sealed with the Great Seal of the state, signed by the Governor, and countersigned by the Secretary of State.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 161.)
