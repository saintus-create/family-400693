## Text

The prior service pension for local members is a pension derived from the contributions of the employing contracting agency if and as provided for in the contract between the board and the contracting agency.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
