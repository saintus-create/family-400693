## Text

A member may request a meeting, to be conducted by the member’s employer, at which the employer shall explain to the member the elements of the member’s past or current compensation that have been or will be reported to the board as compensation earnable. The information provided to the member at the meeting shall be provided orally and in writing and a copy of the writing shall be provided to the member for his or her records.
