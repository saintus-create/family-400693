## Text

The Director of General Services may acquire any easements or rights-of-way which the director determines to be necessary for the proper utilization of real property owned or being acquired by the state.

This section does not apply to land, easements, or rights-of-way to be acquired by the Department of Transportation or the High-Speed Rail Authority.
