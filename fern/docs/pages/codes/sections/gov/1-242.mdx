## Text

Persons in the state who are not its citizens are either:

(a) Citizens of other states; or

(b) Persons who are not citizens of the United States.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 296, Sec. 26. (AB 1096) Effective January 1, 2022.)
