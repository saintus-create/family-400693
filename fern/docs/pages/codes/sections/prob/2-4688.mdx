## Text

Where this division does not provide a rule governing agents under powers of attorney, the law of agency applies.

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
