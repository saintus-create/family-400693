## Text

A devisee of a devise for maintenance is entitled to interest on the amount of any unpaid accumulations of the payments held by the personal representative on each anniversary of the testator’s death, computed from the date of the anniversary.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
