## Text

“Conservator” means a court-appointed conservator having authority to make a health care decision for a patient.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
