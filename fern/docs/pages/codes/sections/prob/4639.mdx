## Text

“Skilled nursing facility” means a “skilled nursing facility” as defined in Section 1250 of the Health and Safety Code.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
