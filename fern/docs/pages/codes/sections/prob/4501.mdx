## Text

The remedies provided in this part are cumulative and not exclusive of any other remedies provided by law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 37. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
