## Text

This chapter does not affect the ability of a design professional to obtain a lien for a work of improvement under Section 8400.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
