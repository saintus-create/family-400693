## Text

One who wrongfully detains a thing is an involuntary trustee thereof, for the benefit of the owner.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 820, Sec. 8. Operative July 1, 1987, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 820.)
