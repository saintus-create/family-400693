## Text

No contract shall provide for a finance charge which is determined in part by the precomputed basis and in part by the simple-interest basis except for any finance charge permitted by subdivisions (a) and (c) of Section 2982.8.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1380, Sec. 20. Effective October 1, 1980.)
