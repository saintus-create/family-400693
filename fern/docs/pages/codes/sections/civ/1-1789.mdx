## Text

The Legislature hereby finds and declares that it is in the public interest that consumers have comprehensive knowledge of services available through electronic commerce and to that end hereby enacts the Electronic Commerce Act of 1984.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 638, Sec. 1.)
