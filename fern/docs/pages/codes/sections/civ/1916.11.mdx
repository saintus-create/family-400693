## Text

Notwithstanding any other remedy a borrower may have based on an alleged failure to comply with Sections 1916.5 through 1916.9, the lien of the mortgage or deed of trust shall be valid.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 274, Sec. 2. Effective August 27, 1981.)
