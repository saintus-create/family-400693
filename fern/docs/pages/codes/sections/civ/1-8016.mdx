## Text

“Direct contract” means a contract between an owner and a direct contractor that provides for all or part of a work of improvement.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 697, Sec. 20. (SB 189) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 105 of Ch. 697 and by Section 8052.)
