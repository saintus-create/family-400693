## Text

Each pension fund or retirement system which is subject to the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (P.L. 93-406), shall not be required to obtain any license or certificate in order to provide funds for any type of loan transaction permitted by law.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 557, Sec. 2.)
