## Text

(a) The fee for a cemetery manager examination shall be eight hundred dollars ($800).

(b) The license fee to obtain a cemetery manager license shall be one hundred thirty dollars ($130).

(c) The renewal fee for a cemetery manager license shall be one hundred fifty dollars ($150).

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 625, Sec. 64. (SB 1443) Effective January 1, 2023.)
