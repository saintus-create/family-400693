## Text

The board may establish a qualifications committee of its own certified public accountant members or other certified public accountants of the state in good standing, to perform the following advisory duties:

(a) To examine the qualifications of all applicants for the license of certified public accountant.

(b) To recommend to the board applicants for the certified public accountant license who fulfill the requirements of this chapter.
