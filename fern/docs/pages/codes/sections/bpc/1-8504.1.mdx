## Text

“Pesticide” includes any of the following:

(a) Any spray adjuvant.

(b) Any substance, or mixture of substances, which is intended to be used for preventing, destroying, repelling, or mitigating any pest or organism.

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 560, Sec. 1. (SB 1244) Effective January 1, 2015.)
