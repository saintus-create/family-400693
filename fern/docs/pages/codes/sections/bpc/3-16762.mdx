## Text

Unless otherwise expressly provided, the remedies or penalties provided by this chapter are cumulative to each other and to the remedies or penalties available under other state law.

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 426, Sec. 3. (SB 763) Effective January 1, 2026.)
