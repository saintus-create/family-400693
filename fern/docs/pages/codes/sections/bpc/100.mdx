## Text

(a) There is in the state government, in the Business and Consumer Services Agency, a Department of Consumer Affairs.

(b) The amendments made to this section by the act adding this subdivision shall become operative on July 1, 2026.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 28, Sec. 1. (SB 170) Effective June 29, 2026. Operative July 1, 2026, by its own provisions.)
