## Text

The board shall keep records that will reasonably ensure that funds expended in the administration of each licensing or registration category shall bear a reasonable relation to the revenue derived from each category.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 758, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 1998.)
