## Text

Any amendment to a city or county ordinance relating to gambling establishments, or the Gambling Control Act, shall be submitted to the department for review and comment, before the ordinance is adopted by the city or county.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 176, Sec. 44. Effective August 24, 2007.)
