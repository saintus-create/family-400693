## Text

No person shall engage in the business of a proprietary private security officer unless registered with the department pursuant to this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 361, Sec. 2. (SB 741) Effective January 1, 2010. Section operative January 1, 2011, pursuant to Section 7574.16.)
