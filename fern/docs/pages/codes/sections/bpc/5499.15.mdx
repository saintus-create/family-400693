## Text

If the legislative body finds that property damage was caused by the negligence of a city or county officer or employee in connection with the abatement of a nuisance pursuant to this chapter, a claim for those damages may be paid from the city or county general fund.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1281, Sec. 6.)
