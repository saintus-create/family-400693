## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Medical Practice Act. Whenever a reference is made to the Medical Practice Act by the provisions of any statute, it is to be construed as referring to the provisions of this chapter.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
