## Text

When any license has been revoked or suspended following a hearing under the provisions of this article, any additional license issued under Article 6.5 (commencing with Section 9888.5) in the name of the licensee may be likewise revoked or suspended by the director.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 592, Sec. 26. (SB 861) Effective January 1, 2026.)
