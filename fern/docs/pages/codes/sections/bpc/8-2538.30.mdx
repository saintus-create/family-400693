## Text

(a) A temporary or trainee licensee shall not be the sole proprietor of, manage, or independently operate a business that engages in the fitting or sale of hearing aids.

(b) A temporary or trainee licensee shall not advertise or otherwise represent that they hold a license as a hearing aid dispenser.
