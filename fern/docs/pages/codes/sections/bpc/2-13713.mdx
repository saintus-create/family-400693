## Text

Any product referred to in this chapter is adulterated if its characteristics fall below the specifications for that product established by the department as minimum standards.

## History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 13706 by Stats. 1992, Ch. 322, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1993.)
