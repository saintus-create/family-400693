## Text

This chapter does not apply to a social media platform with fewer than 1,000,000 discrete monthly users.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 985, Sec. 1. (SB 918) Effective January 1, 2025. Operative July 1, 2025, pursuant to Section 22946.3.)
