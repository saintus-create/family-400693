## Text

A person whose license has been suspended or revoked shall not, until the reinstatement of his or her license, engage in any activity to which the license relates or any other activity or conduct in violation of the order or judgment by which the license was suspended.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 659, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007.)
