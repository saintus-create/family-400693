## Text

The commission shall charge a twenty-dollar ($20) fee to issue federal identification cards. Cards shall be reissued once every four years.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 370, Sec. 16. (SB 309) Effective January 1, 2014.)
