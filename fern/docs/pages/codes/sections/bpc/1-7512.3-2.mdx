## Text

(a) As used in this chapter, “person” includes any individual, firm, company, association, organization, partnership, and corporation.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2030.

## History

(History: Amended (as amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 625, Sec. 19) by Stats. 2024, Ch. 484, Sec. 8. (SB 1454) Effective January 1, 2025. Operative January 1, 2030, by its own provisions.)
