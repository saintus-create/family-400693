## Text

A violation of this article is an infraction punishable by a fine not to exceed one hundred dollars ($100).

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 46. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
