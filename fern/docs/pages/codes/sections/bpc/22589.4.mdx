## Text

This chapter shall not apply to either of the following:

(a) A social media platform that is controlled by a business entity that generated less than one hundred million dollars ($100,000,000) in gross revenue during the preceding calendar year.

(b) A social media platform whose primary function is to allow users to play video games.
