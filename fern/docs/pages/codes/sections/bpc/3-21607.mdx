## Text

Every junk dealer and recycler shall preserve the written record required by this article for at least two years after making the final entry of any purchase or sale of junk or scrap metals and alloys as defined in Section 21600.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1288, Sec. 7.)
