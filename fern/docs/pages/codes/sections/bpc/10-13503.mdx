## Text

It is unlawful to deliver aviation gasolines to the fuel tank of any aircraft through the same meter used for deliveries of gasolines not specifically refined or manufactured for aviation use.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 698, Sec. 4.)
