## Text

The Division of Medical Quality and the California Board of Podiatric Medicine shall adopt rules, pursuant to Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, to govern the conduct of oral argument following nonadoption of a proposed decision. These rules shall preclude oral argument that exceeds the scope of the record of duly admitted evidence.
