## Text

The board shall revoke the license of a licensee, other than one who is also licensed as a physician and surgeon, who uses or offers to use drugs in the course of his or her practice as an educational psychologist.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 659, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007.)
