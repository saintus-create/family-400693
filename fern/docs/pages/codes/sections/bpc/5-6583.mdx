## Text

The bureau shall establish a system of administrative citations and fines under Section 125.9 for violations of this chapter, the Professional Fiduciaries Code of Ethics, or any regulation adopted under this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 491, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
