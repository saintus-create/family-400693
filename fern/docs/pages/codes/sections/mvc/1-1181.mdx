## Text

For the purposes of this chapter the board of supervisors and the county elections official, respectively, shall have all the powers and duties that boards of trustees and city clerks have under Section 1180 and the terms “city,” “municipal election,” “board of trustees,” and “city clerk,” shall mean “proposed memorial district,” “proposed memorial district election,” “board of supervisors,” and “county clerk.”
