## Text

A person shall not advertise, offer, or enter into an agreement with a pension beneficiary that would involve an assignment of pension benefits that is prohibited by state or federal law.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 246, Sec. 2. (SB 386) Effective January 1, 2016.)
