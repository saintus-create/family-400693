## Text

In implementing this article, the awarding department shall utilize existing resources such as the Office of Small Business and Disabled Veteran Business Enterprise Services, the Department of Veterans Affairs, the federal Department of Veterans Affairs, and the Small Business Administration.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 623, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2004.)
