## Text

A resolution of issuance may include provisions requiring the department to hold or cause to be held in trust the revenues, or any part of the revenues, pledged to the payment of revenue bonds and the interest thereon, and to apply or cause to be applied revenues or such part of revenues, only as provided in the resolution of issuance.
