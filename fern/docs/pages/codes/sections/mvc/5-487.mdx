## Text

The department, subject to the approval of the National Guard Members’ Finance Committee, may provide for the issuance, sale, or exchange of refunding bonds for the purpose of redeeming or retiring any bonds issued under this chapter. All provisions of this chapter applicable to the issuance of revenue bonds are applicable to the funding or refunding bonds and to the issuance, sale, or exchange thereof. The department, with the approval of the National Guard Members’ Finance Committee, may adopt a resolution or resolutions of issuance or supplemental resolutions authorizing the issuance of such refunding bonds and fixing the terms and conditions thereof.
