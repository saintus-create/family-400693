## Text

As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise indicates:

(a) “District” means a memorial district organized under the provisions of this chapter.

(b) “Board” means the board of directors of a memorial district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 69. Effective January 1, 2003.)
