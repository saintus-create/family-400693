## Text

A resolution of issuance may include any and all covenants and agreements on the part of the department, and for such other acts or matters which the department, subject to the approval of the California National Guard Finance Committee, deems necessary or convenient or advisable for the better security of the revenue bonds issued thereunder or to make the revenue bonds more marketable.
