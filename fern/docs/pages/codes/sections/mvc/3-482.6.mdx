## Text

A resolution of issuance may provide for the issuance of temporary or interim revenue bonds, certificates, or receipts pending the preparation and delivery of definitive revenue bonds. Such temporary or interim revenue bonds, certificates, or receipts may be of any denomination and with or without coupons.
