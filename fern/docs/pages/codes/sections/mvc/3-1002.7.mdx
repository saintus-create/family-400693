## Text

A resolution of issuance may provide for the issuance of temporary or interim debentures, certificates or receipts pending the preparation and delivery of definitive debentures. Such temporary or interim debentures, certificates or receipts may be of any denomination and with or without coupons.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1488.)
