## Text

For the purpose of creating a fund to provide farm and home aid for veterans in accordance with the provisions of the Veterans’ Farm and Home Purchase Act of 1974 and of all acts amendatory thereof and supplemental thereto, the Veterans’ Finance Committee of 1943, created by Section 991, shall be and hereby is authorized and empowered to create a debt or debts, liability or liabilities, of the State of California, in the aggregate amount of five hundred million dollars ($500,000,000), in the manner provided herein, but not otherwise, nor in excess thereof.
