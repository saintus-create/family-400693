## Text

The director may make the services of the department under this article available, upon those terms and conditions as he or she may deem satisfactory, to any tax-supported public agency in the state, including a school district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 402, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
