## Text

All bids shall be upon a schedule prepared by the clerk, showing all articles needed by the district and its several offices and stating separately the price of each article to be furnished. Any person may bid upon any article separately.

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 83.)
