## Text

The bidder to whom any contract is awarded shall, within 20 days after the date of the award by the board of directors, execute a contract to perform the work. The contract shall be in a form approved by an attorney employed by the board of directors.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
