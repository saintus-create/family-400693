## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed as preventing the parties to the contract, after the claim has arisen, from mutually agreeing in writing to waive the provisions of this article and to have the claim litigated in a court of competent jurisdiction.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 90.)
