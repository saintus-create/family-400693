## Text

To meet an emergency, goods of a perishable nature, in an amount not exceeding one hundred dollars ($100) in value, may be acquired by a state agency without the permission of the department.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 776, Sec. 25. Effective September 27, 2000.)
