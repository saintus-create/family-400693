## Text

A department shall, upon request from a contractor plan room service, provide an electronic copy of a project’s contract documents at no charge to the contractor plan room.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 371, Sec. 1. (AB 2036) Effective January 1, 2011.)
