## Text

The notice shall be published once a week for at least two successive weeks in a newspaper circulated in the county and shall refer to precise plans and specifications covering the work to be done and materials, if any, to be furnished. If the material to be purchased costs over one thousand dollars ($1,000), and there is no purchasing agent, the material shall be purchased from the lowest responsible bidder.
