## Text

When the certificate of completion has been recorded the contractor shall be entitled to payment for the full amount of the contract price, and the recording of the certificate shall be sufficient notice to the owner of such track or tracks that such amount is due and payable.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 100.)
