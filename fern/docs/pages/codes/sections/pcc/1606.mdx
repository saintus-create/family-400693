## Text

Bid openings may be conducted electronically. The platform shall ensure that no bid is viewable before the deadline and that a verifiable digital record of the opening is maintained and made publicly accessible.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 84, Sec. 28. (SB 172) Effective July 13, 2026.)
