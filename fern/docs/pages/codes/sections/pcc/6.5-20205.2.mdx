## Text

Nothing in this article prohibits the board from doing or causing to be done directly by the district, and without any contract, any or all work necessary or proper in or about the making of all current and ordinary repairs or in or about current and ordinary upkeep or maintenance.

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 83.)
