## Text

Upon the request of the department, every state agency that is authorized by law to acquire goods shall designate some person in the agency whose duty it shall be to make reports to the department at times and in a manner as it may require.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 776, Sec. 18. Effective September 27, 2000.)
