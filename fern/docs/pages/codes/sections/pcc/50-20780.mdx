## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by sanitation districts as provided for in the County Sanitation District Act pursuant to Division 5 (commencing with Section 4700) of the Health and Safety Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 93.)
