## Text

When work is to be done, the board shall give notice calling for bids by publication in the county in which the principal office of a district is kept once a week for four consecutive weeks.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 551. Effective January 1, 2007.)
