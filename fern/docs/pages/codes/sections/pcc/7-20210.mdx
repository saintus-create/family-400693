## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by transit districts in the counties of Alameda or Contra Costa, as provided for in Part 1 (commencing with Section 24501) of Division 10 of the Public Utilities Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
