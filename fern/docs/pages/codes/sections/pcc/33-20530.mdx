## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by a joint highway district, as provided in the Joint Highway District Act, Part 1 (commencing with Section 25000) of Division 16 of the Streets and Highways Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
