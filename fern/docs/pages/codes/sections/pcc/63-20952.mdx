## Text

The district may enter into contracts or agreements with the State of California or other public districts for the joint construction of and for the joint use and maintenance of evaporation ponds or other works for the disposal of saline drainage waters and for the disposal of salts and other residues developed from the operation of such works.

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
