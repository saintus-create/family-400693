## Text

The department may retain the services of a design professional or construction project manager, or both, throughout the course of the project in order to ensure compliance with this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 752, Sec. 1. (AB 2498) Effective January 1, 2013.)
