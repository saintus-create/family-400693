## Text

Before making any contract for the construction or improvement of works in carrying out any project totaling twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000) or more, the board shall advertise for bids.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 142, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999.)
