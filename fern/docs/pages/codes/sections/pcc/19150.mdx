## Text

Any contract related to the 2020 United States Census and entered into by the Government Operations Agency or the Office of Planning and Research is exempt from this part.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 455, Sec. 4. (SB 877) Effective September 17, 2018.)
