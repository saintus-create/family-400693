## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by recreation and park districts as provided in the Recreation and Park District Law, Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 5780) of Division 5 of the Public Resources Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
