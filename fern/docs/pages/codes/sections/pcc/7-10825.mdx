## Text

No payment shall be made upon the contract to the contractor or any assignee of the contractor until an order by the trustees that a sufficient surety be substituted on the bond, or that a new, additional, or supplemental bond be given, is satisfied.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
