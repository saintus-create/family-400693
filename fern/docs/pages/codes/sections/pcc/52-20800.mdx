## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by sanitary districts as provided in the Sanitary District Act of 1923 pursuant to Division 6 (commencing with Section 6400) of the Health and Safety Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 95.)
