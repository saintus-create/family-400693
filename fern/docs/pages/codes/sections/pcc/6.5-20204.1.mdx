## Text

All bidders shall be afforded an opportunity to examine such plans and specifications, and the board shall award the contract to the lowest responsible bidder. The plans and specifications adopted shall be attached to and become part of the contract.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 83.)
