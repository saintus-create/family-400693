## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by a city council, as provided in the Municipal Lighting Maintenance District Act of 1927, Part 3 (commencing with Section 18600) of Division 14 of the Streets and Highways Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
