## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, whenever a district participates in any federal or state assistance program, the district shall comply with the procurement requirements of that program to the extent that the requirements are more restrictive than those set forth in this article.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1042, Sec. 1.)
