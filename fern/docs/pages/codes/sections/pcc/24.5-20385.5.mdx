## Text

As used in this article, “Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority” or “authority” means the Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority created by Section 130050.2 of the Public Utilities Code.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 87, Sec. 1. (AB 499) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 20389.)
