## Text

Contracts in excess of two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500) for the construction or repair of any levees or associated works, unless the construction or repair is done under its own superintendence, shall be made pursuant to this chapter.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 142, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
