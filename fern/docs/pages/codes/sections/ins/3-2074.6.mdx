## Text

In lieu of showing the attestation clause and official signatures in the form as set forth in Section 2071, the standard form policy may show, immediately following the policy provisions, the following:

“In witness whereof, this company has executed and attested these presents; but this policy shall not be valid unless countersigned by the duly authorized agent of this company at the agency hereinbefore mentioned.SecretaryPresident”
