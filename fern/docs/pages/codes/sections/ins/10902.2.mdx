## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to require a health benefit plan to offer a contract to an individual if the carrier does not otherwise offer contracts to individuals.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 810, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
