## Text

An insurer transacting such compensation or liability insurance shall include the following schedules in its annual statement required by law:

(a) A schedule showing distribution of unallocated liability loss expense payments.

(b) A schedule showing distribution of unallocated compensation loss expense payments.

(c) A schedule of its experience under policies of such insurance, in such form as the commissioner prescribes.
