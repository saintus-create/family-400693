## Text

This chapter shall not affect the negotiation through a licensed broker or agent for, nor the execution or delivery of an undertaking of bail, executed by an insurer for its insured under a policy of automobile insurance or of liability insurance upon the automobile of the insured, nor shall this chapter affect the negotiation for, or the execution or delivery of an undertaking of bail or bail bond which is authorized by Part 5, Division 2 of this code.
