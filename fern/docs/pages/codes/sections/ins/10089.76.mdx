## Text

Upon referral of a complaint to mediation, the department shall immediately transfer all necessary information concerning the claim, including the name and address of the insured and the insurer, to a mediator.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 848, Sec. 1. Effective October 13, 1995.)
