## Text

A classification shall take no account of any physical impairment of employees or the extent to which employees may have persons dependent upon them for support.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 228, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994. Operative January 1, 1995, by Sec. 7 of Ch. 228, as amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1242.)
