## Text

(a) The commissioner shall post to the department’s Internet Web site a report on wildfire risk compiled from the data collected pursuant to Section 929.

(b) The report shall be updated every two years to reflect new data submitted by insurers.

(c) The report shall separately provide information each calendar year.
