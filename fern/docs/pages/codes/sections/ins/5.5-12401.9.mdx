## Text

The schedules of rates which are required to be filed with the commissioner under the provisions of Section 12401.1 shall be printed or typed in type not smaller than 10-point and, so long as they are effective, full copies thereof, showing their effective date or dates, shall be kept at all times available to the public and prominently displayed in a public place in each office of a title insurer, an underwritten title company and a controlled escrow company in the county to which such rates apply. On request, copies of such schedules or adequate summaries of the pertinent part or parts thereof shall be furnished to the public.
