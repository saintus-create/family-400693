## Text

No mortgage guaranty insurer shall pay any compensation to any person for transacting insurance for or with it based in whole or in part upon a commission basis unless such person is licensed pursuant to and subject to all the provisions of and restrictions in Chapter 5, Part 2, Division 1 of this code. This section shall not be construed so as to affect the meaning of any other provisions of this article.
