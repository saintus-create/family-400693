## Text

The secretary shall prepare an annual statement, showing the condition of such reinsurer on December thirty-first and a supplemental report of the business of the reinsurer to January thirty-first and present both reports at the ensuing annual meeting.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
