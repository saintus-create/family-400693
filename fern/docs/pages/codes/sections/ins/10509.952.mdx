## Text

This chapter shall apply to all group and individual life insurance policies and certificates except as follows:

(a) Variable life insurance.

(b) Individual and group annuity contracts.

(c) Credit life insurance.

(d) Life insurance policies with no illustrated death benefits on any individual exceeding ten thousand dollars ($10,000).
