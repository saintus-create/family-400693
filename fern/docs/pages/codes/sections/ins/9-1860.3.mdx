## Text

The provisions of the following sections of this code shall be applicable to the administration, enforcement and interpretation of this chapter:

Sections 1 to 41, both inclusive, 100 to 121, both inclusive, 620, 621, 700, 701, 704, 730 to 737, both inclusive, 12903, 12904, 12919, 12921, 12921.5, 12924 to 12926, both inclusive, 12928, 12930, and 12974 to 12977, both inclusive.
