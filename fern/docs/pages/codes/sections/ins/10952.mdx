## Text

This chapter shall not apply to health benefit plans for coverage of Medicare services pursuant to contracts with the United States government, Medicare supplement policies, Medi-Cal contracts with the State Department of Health Care Services, policies offered under the Healthy Families Program, long-term care coverage, or specialized health benefit plans.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 656, Sec. 6. (AB 2244) Effective January 1, 2011. Inoperative, pursuant to Section 10960.5, on January 1, 2014, subject to condition for resuming operation.)
