## Text

If a check in payment of a tax, fee, or penalty is not paid by the bank on which it is drawn on its first presentation, the commissioner shall charge and collect an additional fee of fourteen dollars ($14).

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 534, Sec. 89. (AB 1699) Effective January 1, 2018.)
