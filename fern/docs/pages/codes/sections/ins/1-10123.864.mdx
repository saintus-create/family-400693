## Text

The provision of medically necessary pasteurized donor human milk obtained from a tissue bank licensed pursuant to Chapter 4.1 (commencing with Section 1635) of Division 2 of the Health and Safety Code is a basic health care service, as described in Sections 10112.27 and 10112.281 and any regulations adopted thereunder.
