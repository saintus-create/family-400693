## Text

The following annexation procedures may be used for annexation of land to any water conservation district as an alternative to the procedures set forth in Articles 2 (commencing with Section 75860) to 5 (commencing with Section 75920), inclusive, of Chapter 2 of Part 10 of Division 21.
