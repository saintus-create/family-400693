## Text

Where the district has acquired fee title to, or any interest in, land through condemnation proceedings and the board, pursuant to Section 43507, determines that said property or any part thereof or interest therein is no longer necessary for the uses and purposes of said district and that said property or any part thereof or interest therein shall be disposed of by sale, such sale shall be made as hereinafter provided.
