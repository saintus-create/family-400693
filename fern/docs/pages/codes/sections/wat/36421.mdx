## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, special bond elections within an improvement district shall be called, noticed, held, conducted and the results thereof canvassed in substantially the same manner as is provided in this division for bond elections held in the entire district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 6, Sec. 17. Effective February 4, 1980.)
