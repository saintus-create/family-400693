## Text

An application for a reservoir site loan pursuant to Section 12885.2 shall include:

(a) Evidence that the proposed site is zoned as a reservoir site.

(b) Geologic data as to the suitability of the site for reservoir construction.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 301.)
