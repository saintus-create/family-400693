## Text

In making the determination required by Section 81325, the board of supervisors shall include all resolutions received by the county at least 10 days before the date of the public meeting at which the determination is made.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 844, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
