## Text

After the filing of the plans, specifications, estimate, and statement and assessment, the board shall give notice of a hearing upon the formation petition and whether a special assessment shall be levied upon the lands within the improvement district for the purpose of raising money to pay for the improvements, as set forth in the petition, plans, specifications, and statement and assessment.
