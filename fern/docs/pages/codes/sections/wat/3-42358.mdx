## Text

Notice of the order requiring presentation of claims stating the time and place shall be published by the board in the county in which the office of the district is located once a week for four successive weeks, commencing within 10 days after the making of the order.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 59.7. Effective January 1, 1993.)
