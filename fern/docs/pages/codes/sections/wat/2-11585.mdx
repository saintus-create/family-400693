## Text

Where any water or property mentioned in the next preceding section has, prior to July 1, 1933, been filed on, appropriated, or dedicated to public use by any State agency, the use thereof by the State agency shall be deemed a more necessary public use.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
