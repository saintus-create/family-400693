## Text

A proposed annexation petition shall contain:

(a) A request addressed to the annexing district, petitioning the annexing district to annex the petitioning district.

(b) A statement setting forth the desirability or necessity of the annexation.

(c) Any terms subject to which the annexation shall be conditioned.
