## Text

The board may issue a regulation or informational order requiring a wholesale water supplier, an urban retail water supplier, or a distributor of a public water supply, as that term is used in Section 350, to provide a monthly report relating to water production, water use, or water conservation.

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 14, Sec. 12. (SB 606) Effective January 1, 2019.)
