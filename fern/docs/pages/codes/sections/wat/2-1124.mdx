## Text

The board at any time may amend or modify a decision or order to correct any obvious typographical or clerical error or oversight without the necessity of notice and a hearing thereon.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 659, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
