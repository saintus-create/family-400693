## Text

In determining the amount of the assessments to be levied and bonds to be issued pursuant to this chapter, the board of directors may include bond interest for facilities for delivery primarily of agricultural water supplies estimated to accrue during the period of construction, but not exceeding three years from the date of issuance of said bonds.
