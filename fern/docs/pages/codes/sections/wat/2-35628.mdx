## Text

A district shall not exercise the right of eminent domain under this article for the condemnation of property outside the boundaries of the principal county in which the district is situated unless it first obtains the consent thereto of the board of supervisors of the county in which such property is located.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 899.)
