## Text

When the United States is authorized to construct works for the benefit of any district and the district is obligated to repay the construction cost in whole or in part to the United States, the district may by resolution of its board enter into a contract with the United States for the construction of the works or may submit bids for the construction of the works the same as any other person may submit bids for the construction of the works.
