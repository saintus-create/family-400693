## Text

All of the cost of administration of a service area and the distribution of water therein shall be paid by the owners of the rights to divert or store water within the service area as provided in this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 230, Sec. 24. Effective August 16, 2004.)
