## Text

Not later than 10 days after the date on which any new member is admitted, the agency shall notify in writing the Secretary of State with regard to the name of the new member and the date of its admission.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 844, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
