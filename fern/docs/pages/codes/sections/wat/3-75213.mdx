## Text

The district shall issue warrants for the amount of the improvement district assessment, which warrants shall be made payable in amounts and at the times corresponding substantially to the payment of the installments of the improvement district assessment and shall bear interest at such rate of interest as may have been fixed on the levy of the improvement district assessment. Such warrants shall be payable only out of funds derived from the levy and collection of the improvement district assessment.
