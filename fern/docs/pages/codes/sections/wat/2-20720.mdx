## Text

A petition for the formation of a district shall contain:

(a) Generally, a description of the land.

(b) Generally, the sources, which may be in the alternative, from which the land is proposed to be irrigated.

(c) The name of a person to whom notice of the final hearing on the petition may be sent.

(d) A prayer that the land described be formed into a district.

(e) Signatures of the petitioners.
