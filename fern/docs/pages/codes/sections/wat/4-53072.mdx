## Text

A demand warrant unpaid for want of funds at the time of presentation shall be registered by the county treasurer separately from other warrants of the district and shall bear interest from date of registration at the rate which the board has adopted pursuant to Section 53300.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1109, Sec. 5. Effective September 28, 1983.)
