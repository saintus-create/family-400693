## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not limit the department in the financing and construction of any of the facilities of the State Water Resources Development System pursuant to the provisions of Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 12930) of Part 6, nor shall they constitute a limitation on or modification of the responsibility of the department to make allocations of costs provided for in water supply contracts executed pursuant thereto.
