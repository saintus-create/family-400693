## Text

It is the intention of the Legislature, in enacting this part, to permit levels of water management planning commensurate with the numbers of customers served and the volume of water supplied, while accounting for impacts from climate change.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 14, Sec. 26. (SB 606) Effective January 1, 2019.)
