## Text

Under such regulations and upon such terms, limitations, and conditions as it prescribes, the department may:

(a) Fix and establish the prices, rates, and charges at which the water supply made available to the people of the State of California by the Washoe Project, or by any division or unit thereof, shall be sold and disposed of; and

(b) Enter into contracts and agreements with public agencies or with portions thereof as described in subdivision (e) of Section 12056, for the sale and disposal of water supplies acquired by it pursuant to Section 12052.
