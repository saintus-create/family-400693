## Text

A district may enter into any forward contract, or futures contract, or put, call, or swap agreement, or similar procurement method for electricity, natural gas, or coal, or any weather, fuel, or energy risk management contract determined to be in the best interests of the district by the board of directors of the district for any of the uses or purposes of the district and by those contracts or agreements to bind the district for the payment of the consideration specified therein.
