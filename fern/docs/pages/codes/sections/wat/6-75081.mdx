## Text

The board shall have the same power, authority, and jurisdiction over and with reference to an improvement district formed pursuant to this chapter that it possesses over and with reference to the district and improvements made within and chargeable to the entire district; provided, that any obligation created in behalf of such an improvement district shall be a charge against only the property situated within the improvement district.
