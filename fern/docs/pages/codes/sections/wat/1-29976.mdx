## Text

The following acts are hereby repealed: Yr. Ch. Pg. 1917: 606: 936 1933: 994: 2557 1935: 833: 2250

Notwithstanding the repeal of said acts the provisions thereof as codified in this division shall continue to apply to the Palo Verde Irrigation District.
