## Text

The copy of the resolution published or posted shall be accompanied by a notice subscribed by the secretary, with the seal of the district attached, that:

(a) The hearing referred to in the resolution will be had at the time and place specified in the resolution.

(b) At that time and place any person interested, including all persons owning property in the district, will be heard upon the questions stated in the resolution.
