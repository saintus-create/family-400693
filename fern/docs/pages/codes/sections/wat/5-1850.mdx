## Text

Any factual or legal determinations made pursuant to a cease and desist order shall be conclusive and shall preclude any party to the order from raising those issues in any subsequent administrative proceeding.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 652, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2003.)
