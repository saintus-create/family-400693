## Text

The notice of the election at which general obligation bonds are proposed to be destroyed shall specify, in addition to the requirements provided in Section 35002:

(a) The amount of the bonded indebtedness authorized.

(b) The amount of the bonds remaining unsold.

(c) The amount of bonds proposed to be destroyed.
