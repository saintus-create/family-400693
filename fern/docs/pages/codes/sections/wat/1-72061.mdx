## Text

Notwithstanding any other provisions of law, within the Pomona Valley Municipal Water District, bonds may be issued by such district and the debt service thereon paid in and for the purpose and in the manner provided in this chapter. Except as provided otherwise in this chapter, such bonds shall be issued and the debt service thereon paid in and for the purpose and in the manner otherwise provided by this division.
