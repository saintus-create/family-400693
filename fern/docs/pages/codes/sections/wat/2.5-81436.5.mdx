## Text

The agency may prepay all or a portion of any unpaid principal balance relating to the existing capital assets as of June 30, 2009, on behalf of BAWSCA members, as provided in the July 2009 Water Supply Agreement.

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 251, Sec. 1. (AB 2167) Effective January 1, 2013.)
