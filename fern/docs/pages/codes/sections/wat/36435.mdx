## Text

After adopting the resolution ordering the improvement district formed, the board may provide for the issuance of warrants of the district for the improvement district in an amount not exceeding the estimate stated in the resolution ordering the improvement district formed, such warrants to be payable from assessments to be levied and collected upon the lands within said improvement district.
