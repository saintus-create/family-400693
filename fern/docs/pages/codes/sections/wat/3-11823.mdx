## Text

Notwithstanding anything contained in this part, the department may provide in the proceedings authorizing the issuance of bonds that revenues received during the period of construction and for a period of one year after completion thereof may be used for the payment during such periods of costs and expenses of the department, including interest due and payable, incurred in performing the duties prescribed in this part.
