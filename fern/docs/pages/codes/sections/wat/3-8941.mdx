## Text

Any owner or holder of lands within the boundaries of the drainage district whose lands are subject to the lien of assessment number six or supplemental assessment number six levied in connection with project number six may present to any officer authorized by law to collect such assessments, or any part thereof, warrants of the district now, or hereafter to be, issued in connection with project number six in payment of his assessment, or any part thereof.
