## Text

At the time set for hearing protests, the board shall hear and pass upon all protests made as provided for in Section 35546, and its decision shall be final and conclusive. The board may adjourn the hearing from time to time, and any written protest may be withdrawn by the holder of title making the same in writing at any time prior to the conclusion of the hearing or any such adjournment thereof.
