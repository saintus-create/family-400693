## Text

If any error should occur in the publication of the notice of the sale of any delinquent property or the list of delinquencies which might invalidate a sale and the error is discovered prior to sale, the collector shall at once republish the notice of the sale and the items of the list of delinquency as to the property affected by the error, making the republication conform to this article.
