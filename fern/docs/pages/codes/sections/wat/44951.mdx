## Text

Revenue warrants shall be secured by all or part of the revenues received by the district from tolls or charges levied pursuant to Section 43006 or from any other source other than assessments, and any such revenues may be pledged to the payment of revenue warrants. In the resolution authorizing the issuance of revenue warrants, the board may subject such revenues as it specifies to a lien or charge to secure the payment of the principal of and interest on the revenue warrants, but such warrants shall not be secured by any lien or charge upon any assessment.
