## Text

Any reclamation district, levee district or drainage district in which there are lands within the boundaries of the drainage district, which lands are subject to the lien of assessment number 6, or supplemental assessment number 6, levied in connection with project number 6, may present to any officer authorized by law to collect such assessments, warrants of the drainage district owned by it, and issued in connection with project number 6, in payment of the assessment on the lands.
