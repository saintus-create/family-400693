## Text

As an alternative procedure for the detachment of land from an improvement district, lands may be detached from any improvement district by resolution of the board declaring the territory detached after receipt by the board of written consent of the owners of all of the land to be detached therefrom, which consent shall include the terms and conditions of annexation, if any.
