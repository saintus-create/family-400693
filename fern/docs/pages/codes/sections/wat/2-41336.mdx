## Text

The names shall be arranged in groups under the designation of the office for which each person named is a candidate, in order determined by random drawing following the procedures provided by the Elections Code.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1593, Sec. 11. Effective September 30, 1990.)
