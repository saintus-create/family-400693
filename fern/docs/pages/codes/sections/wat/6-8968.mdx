## Text

The Controller upon demand by the board shall cancel the warrants and credit the face value thereof, plus accrued interest thereon to the date of the tender of the warrants to the board, department or officer receiving them, to the fund upon which the warrants were originally drawn and charge the fund with the accrued interest.
