## Text

In a district containing an improvement district the board and all of the officers of the district each respectively has all the rights, powers, and privileges as to the improvement district, its land, and the proceedings in relation to the improvement district that each respectively has for the district of which the improvement district is a part, including the right of the district to acquire, own, and hold property.
