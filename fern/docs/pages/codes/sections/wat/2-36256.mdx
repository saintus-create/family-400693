## Text

At the time set for hearing protests, the board shall hear and pass upon all protests made as provided for in Section 36254 and its decision shall be final and conclusive. The board may adjourn the hearings from time to time and any written protest may be withdrawn by an owner making the same in writing at any time prior to the conclusion of the hearing or any such adjournment thereof.
