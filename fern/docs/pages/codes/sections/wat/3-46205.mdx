## Text

The commissioners shall transmit two copies of the rolls to the board. The board shall file one copy in its records and transmit to the county treasurer of each affected county that portion of the roll relating to land within the county.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 117. Effective January 1, 1993.)
