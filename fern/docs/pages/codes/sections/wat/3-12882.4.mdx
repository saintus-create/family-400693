## Text

A copy of each application, except applications for loans for the preparation of proposed feasibility reports, shall be transmitted by the department to the Department of Parks and Recreation and to the Department of Fish and Game.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 711, Sec. 129. Effective September 15, 1992.)
