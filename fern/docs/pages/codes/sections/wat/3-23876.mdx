## Text

The inclusion petition shall describe the land in the improvement district as enlarged by the proposed inclusions and give the names and addresses of the owners of the additional land in substantially the same manner as in the original petition for forming an improvement district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
