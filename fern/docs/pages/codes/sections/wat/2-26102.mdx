## Text

The published list of delinquencies shall contain all of the following information relating to each parcel of land separately assessed on which the assessment is delinquent:

(a) The name of the assessee.

(b) The description of the land.

(c) The total amount due, which shall be the aggregate of the assessments, penalties, and costs due thereon.
