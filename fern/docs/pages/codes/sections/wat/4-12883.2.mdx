## Text

In the case of a grant for recreation or fish and wildlife enhancement, or both, the determination of economic justification of the proposed project may, in the department’s discretion, be limited to a determination of the economic justification of the recreation and fish and wildlife enhancement functions of the project.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 27.)
