## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, the provisions of this division applicable to bonds and warrants of the district and assessments therefor shall, so far as consistent with this chapter, apply to bonds and warrants of the district for improvement districts therein.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 6, Sec. 21. Effective February 4, 1980.)
