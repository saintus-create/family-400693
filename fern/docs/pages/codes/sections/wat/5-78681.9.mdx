## Text

Only the programs or projects that are not the obligation of the federal Central Valley Project or the State Water Project may be funded under this article.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
