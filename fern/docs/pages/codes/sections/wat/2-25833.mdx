## Text

A certified copy of an order determining that any land is entitled to a special rate of assessment shall be delivered to the assessor, and thereafter, until notified of a change in the determination, he shall enter the land described in the order separately in the assessment book and designate it so that it may readily be distinguished from other land not entitled to a special rate.
