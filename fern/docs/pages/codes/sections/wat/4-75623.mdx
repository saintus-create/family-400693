## Text

A notice of the hearing shall be mailed to the protestant at least 10 days before the date fixed for the hearing.

Notice of the determination by the board at the hearing shall be mailed to each protestant, who shall have 10 days from the date of mailing to pay the ground water charge, and the interest and penalties thereon.
