## Text

Upon making the order the secretary shall enter in the minutes of the board a notice in substantially the following form:

(Name of reclamation district, location or principal place of business.) Notice is hereby given that at a meeting of the board of trustees held on the (date), an installment of (amount) was ordered paid within 60 days from date thereof to ____ at ____. Any installment which shall remain unpaid on the (day fixed) will be delinquent together with the accrued interest thereon.
