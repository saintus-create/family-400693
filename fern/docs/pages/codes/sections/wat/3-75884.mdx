## Text

The order changing the boundaries shall describe the boundaries of the land included within the district, and if such land adjoins any portion of the district, the order shall also describe that portion of the boundary of the district which coincides with the boundary of the land included. For the purposes of the order, the board may cause a survey to be made of such portions of such boundaries as it deems necessary.
