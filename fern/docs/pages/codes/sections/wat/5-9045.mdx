## Text

If the assessment, or any part of it has been paid in warrants by the district on any lands in the district by funds other than the funds of the district, the district shall deliver to the owner of such lands a warrant of the drainage district, levied in connection with project number 6, which in amount shall be equivalent to the same pro rata of the assessment on the lands as the district paid on other lands in the district.
