## Text

Receipts from assessments levied and charges fixed in connection with distribution districts shall be deposited in a separate fund for the benefit of the distribution district to be used for meeting any payments which may be required in carrying out the requirements of a contract between a distribution district and the United States, executed under the provisions of the federal reclamation laws.
