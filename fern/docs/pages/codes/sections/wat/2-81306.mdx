## Text

“Project” means a work and all of the activities related to, or necessary for, the acquisition, construction, operation and maintenance of a work, including, but not limited to, planning, design, financing, contracting, project management, and administration.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 844, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
