## Text

Notice as required in Sections 75620 and 75623 shall be given by deposit thereof in any postal facility regularly maintained by the government of the United States in a sealed envelope with postage paid, addressed to the person on whom it is served at his name and address as disclosed by the records of the district. The service is complete at the time of deposit.
