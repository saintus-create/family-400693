## Text

For the purposes of formation or for any other of its purposes a district may, before the levying of the first assessment, incur indebtedness in a total amount not to exceed two thousand dollars, ($2,000) or, if the district contains more than 4,000 acres, one-half as many dollars as there are acres of land in the district.
