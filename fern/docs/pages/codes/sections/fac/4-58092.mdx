## Text

The director may establish such facilities and publish such bulletins and reports as are necessary and proper in keeping producers and handlers informed with regard to the supply, demand, and other economic conditions which affect the sale of any farm product which is produced in this state.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
