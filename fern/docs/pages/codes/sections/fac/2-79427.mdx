## Text

“Marketing season” or “fiscal year” are synonymous terms and mean the period beginning May 1 of any year and extending through April 30 of the following year.

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 605, Sec. 1. (AB 2695) Effective January 1, 2011.)
