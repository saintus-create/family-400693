## Text

Counties may, subject to the approval of the director, contract, one with the other, for the maintenance by one, or some of them, of the approved milk inspection service within the limits of the other as will best carry out this division and avoid duplication.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 445, Sec. 15.)
