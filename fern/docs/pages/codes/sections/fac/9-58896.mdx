## Text

A marketing order may contain provisions for the gathering and dissemination of weather data to producers of any commodity, and to any persons providing services relating to production of a commodity, when the data is necessary to aid in any manner in the production or harvesting of that commodity.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 939, Sec. 1. Effective September 7, 1984.)
