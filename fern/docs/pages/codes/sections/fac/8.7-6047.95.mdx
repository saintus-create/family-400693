## Text

Whenever acreage within the district is planted with table grape plants in a fashion so as to qualify as table grape acreage, the acreage is subject to assessment as provided in this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 244, Sec. 1. Effective August 29, 2003.)
