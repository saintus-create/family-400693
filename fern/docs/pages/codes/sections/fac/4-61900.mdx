## Text

The director may confer, enter into agreements, or otherwise arrange with the constituted authorities of this state, other states, or agencies of the United States with respect to plans which relate to the stabilization and distribution of market milk within this state or as between this state and other states or the United States, and may exercise his powers pursuant to this chapter to effectuate and enforce such plans.
