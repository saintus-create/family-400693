## Text

The members of the district board shall not receive any compensation for their services, but may be reimbursed for their actual and necessary expenses, when claims for those expenses have been approved by the district board.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 442, Sec. 1. Effective October 3, 2001.)
