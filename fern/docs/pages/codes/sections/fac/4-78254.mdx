## Text

The commission may present facts to, and negotiate with, state, federal, and foreign agencies on matters which affect this chapter, to the extent permitted by federal law.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1058, Sec. 1. Effective September 19, 1990.)
