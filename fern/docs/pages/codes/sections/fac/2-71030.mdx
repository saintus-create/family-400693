## Text

“Marketing season” or “fiscal year” are synonymous terms and mean the period beginning September 1 of any year and extending through the last day of August of the following year.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 567, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
