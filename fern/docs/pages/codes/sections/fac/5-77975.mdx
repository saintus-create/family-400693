## Text

If the director finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in Section 77971, the director shall certify and declare this chapter inoperative. The director may conduct another implementation referendum vote one or more years after the previous vote has been taken.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. Effective August 10, 1990.)
