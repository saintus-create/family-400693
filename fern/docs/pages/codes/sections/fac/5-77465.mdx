## Text

If the director finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 77462, he or she shall so certify and give notice of the vote to all producers, processors, and shippers whose names and addresses are on file with the director.

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 805, Sec. 1. Effective October 4, 1993.)
