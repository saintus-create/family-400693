## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 77602, the secretary shall certify and give notice of the favorable vote to all affected producers whose names and addresses are on file with the secretary.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 131. Effective January 1, 1996.)
