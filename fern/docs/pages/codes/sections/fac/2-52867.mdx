## Text

“Cotton growers” or “growers” means any person in the district who produces, or causes to be produced, cotton for market.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 189, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2009. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
