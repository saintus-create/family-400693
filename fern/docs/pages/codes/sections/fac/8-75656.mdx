## Text

Upon suspension of this chapter, the commission shall mail a copy of the notice of suspension to all producers and handlers whose names and addresses are on file with the commission.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 69, Sec. 60. Effective May 9, 1994.)
