## Text

Regulations adopted pursuant to this article or pursuant to Section 24681 or Section 24991 shall conform, so far as practicable, to the rules, regulations and standards of the United States Department of Agriculture which govern the inspection, preparation, and processing of poultry meat and poultry meat food products.
