## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 68092, the secretary shall certify and give notice of the favorable vote to all producers and handlers whose names and addresses may be on file with the secretary.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1996.)
