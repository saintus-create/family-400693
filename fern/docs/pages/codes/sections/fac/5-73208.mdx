## Text

To be eligible for election to the commission, a producer nominee shall submit to the director a nomination petition with the signatures of at least 10 eligible producers from the district from which the nominee is seeking election.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
