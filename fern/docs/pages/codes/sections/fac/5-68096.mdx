## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in Section 68092, the secretary shall so certify and declare all provisions of this chapter inoperative.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1996.)
