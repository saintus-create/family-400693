## Text

To address catastrophic events, a marketing order may contain provisions for the establishment and operation of an indemnity trust fund to cover livestock and livestock product losses due to disease, natural disaster, or accidents.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 609, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2000.)
