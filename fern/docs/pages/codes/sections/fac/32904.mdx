## Text

If reacting animals are found they shall be removed from the herd immediately by the owner and kept separate and apart from other cattle or goats and disposed of in accordance with the provisions of Part 2 (commencing with Section 9801) of Division 5.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 771.)
