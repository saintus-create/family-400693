## Text

“Wholesaler” means any person who is licensed or who works for a person who is licensed pursuant to Section 8035 of the Fish and Game Code and engaged in the California commercial fisheries.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1151, Sec. 1.)
