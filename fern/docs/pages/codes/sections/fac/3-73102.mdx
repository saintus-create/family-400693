## Text

Six producers, one from each district shall be nominated and elected by and from independent producers. Six producers, one from each district shall be nominated and elected by and from cooperative producers.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
