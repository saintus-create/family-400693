## Text

Following a favorable referendum conducted prior to August 31, 1986, a referendum shall be conducted by the commission every fifth year thereafter between October 1st and January 31st, following procedures provided by this section, unless a referendum is conducted as the result of a petition pursuant to Section 69103. In such case the referendum shall be every fifth year following the industry petitioned referendum.
