## Text

The term of office of all members and alternates on the commission, except ex officio members, shall be two years from the beginning of the marketing season in the year of their election and until qualified successors are elected.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 210, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
