## Text

In addition to any late charge, a seed-certifying agency, pursuant to procedures adopted by the seed-certifying agency, may suspend certification services to any person who fails to timely pay any fee or late charge required by this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 715, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
