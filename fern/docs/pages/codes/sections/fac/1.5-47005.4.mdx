## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be interpreted to preclude the creation and keeping of additional information that a certified farmers’ market operator may endeavor itself to create and keep, or to contractually require a vendor to provide additional information as a condition to selling in the operator’s farmers’ market.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 384, Sec. 3. (AB 2324) Effective January 1, 2017.)
