## Text

(a) It is unlawful for any person to produce or handle rice sold as certified unless duly registered pursuant to Section 55080.

(b) It is unlawful for any person to willfully make a false statement or representation, or knowingly fail to disclose a fact required to be disclosed, during registration pursuant to Section 55080.
