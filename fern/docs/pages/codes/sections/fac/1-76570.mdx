## Text

“Salmon” means the following species of fish:

(a) King or chinook salmon (Oncorhynchus tshawytscha).

(b) Silver or coho salmon (Oncorhynchus kisutch).

(c) Pink or humpback salmon (Oncorhynchus gorhuscha).
