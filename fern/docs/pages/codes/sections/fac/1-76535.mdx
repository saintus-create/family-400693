## Text

“Handler” means any of the following:

(a) A processor.

(b) A receiver.

(c) A wholesaler.

(d) An exporter.

(e) An individual licensed or working for a partnership, corporation, or any other business unit or organization licensed pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 8032 of the Fish and Game Code and engaged in the California commercial salmon fishery.
