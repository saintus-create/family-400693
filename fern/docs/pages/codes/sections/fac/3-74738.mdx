## Text

The term of office of all board members and alternate board members of the commission, except ex officio members, is three years from the beginning of the marketing season in the year of their election and until qualified successors are elected.

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 595, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
