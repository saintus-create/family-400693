## Text

Notwithstanding Section 74738, the initial term of members and alternates shall be divided by lot into three terms of one year, three terms of two years, and three terms of three years. At the end of each of these initial terms, the terms of office of all board members and alternates shall be three years. Terms of office of each board member and alternate board member of the commission shall be limited to three consecutive terms. However, members and alternates who serve initial one-year and two-year terms shall be eligible for three additional three-year terms.
