## Text

“Sea urchin fishery” means any activity, including economic activity, involved in the harvesting, receiving, processing, manufacturing, or distributing of sea urchin, parts of sea urchin, or products therefrom, for commercial purposes.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 973, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
