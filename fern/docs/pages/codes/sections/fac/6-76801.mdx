## Text

No program or activity which is conducted or sponsored pursuant to this chapter shall make false or unwarranted claims, or disparage the quality, value, use, or sale of any commodity authorized by law to be marketed in this state, nor shall any program be conducted with reference to a private brand or trade name which refers to salmon or salmon products.

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1500, Sec. 1.)
