## Text

The commission or secretary may bring an action for judicial relief from the secretary’s written notice, or from noncompliance by the commission with the written notice, as the case may be, in a court of competent jurisdiction, that may issue a temporary restraining order, permanent injunction, or other applicable relief.

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 597, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
