## Text

No apportionment shall be made for more than one fair in any one year in any county or district. The fair may be operated in one or more seasonal divisions with the approval of the department. The department may approve such method of operation if it finds that savings will accrue from not providing additional facilities or that an increase in revenue will result or both.
