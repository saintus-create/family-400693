## Text

It is a misdemeanor for any person licensed as a cash buying processor to buy or otherwise take title to, or possession of, any farm product from a producer of the product, except by cash payment of the full agreed price to the producer at the time of obtaining possession or control.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 942, Sec. 9.5.)
