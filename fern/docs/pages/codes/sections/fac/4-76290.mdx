## Text

The commission may promote the sale of lamb, wool, and the products derived therefrom by advertising and other promotional means, including cost-sharing advertising, for the purpose of maintaining and expanding present markets and creating new and larger intrastate, interstate, and foreign markets for lamb and wool, and to educate and instruct the public with respect to the many and varied uses of lamb, wool, and the products derived therefrom.
