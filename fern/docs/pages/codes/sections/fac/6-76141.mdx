## Text

(a) The commission shall, not later than April 20 of each year, establish the assessment for the following marketing season, which shall not exceed fifteen cents ($0.15) per 18-pound container or its equivalent in volume in fresh shipping cherries, or three-tenths of one cent ($0.003) per pound for processing cherries.

(b) A fee greater than that specified in subdivision (a) may be charged only if approved by producers pursuant to Section 76112.
