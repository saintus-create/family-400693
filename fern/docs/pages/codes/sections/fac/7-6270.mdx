## Text

Any two or more districts organized or existing under this chapter may be consolidated, whether or not the boundaries are coterminous, and whether or not the districts are located in the same county.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1078, Sec. 1. Effective October 11, 1993.)
