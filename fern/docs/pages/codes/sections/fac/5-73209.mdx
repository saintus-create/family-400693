## Text

The director shall establish a list of handlers and shall, by mail ballot or by calling a meeting of all handlers, conduct the nomination and election of handlers to the commission as provided for in Sections 73103 and 73104.

Handlers shall be given written notice of the meeting at least 15 days prior to the meeting date.
