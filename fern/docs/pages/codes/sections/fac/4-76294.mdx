## Text

The commission may collect information, including, but not limited to, industry statistics, and may publish and distribute, without charge, a bulletin or other communication for dissemination of information to producers and handlers.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 397, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2002.)
