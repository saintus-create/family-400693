## Text

A county shall not, through an approved milk inspection service or otherwise, levy or collect any fee or tax for the purpose of inspecting any milk or dairy product on any truck or other vehicle or during transportation of that milk or dairy product.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 445, Sec. 31.)
