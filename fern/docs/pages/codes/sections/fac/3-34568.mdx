## Text

Any distributor in possession of a milk case having another distributor’s registration shall notify the registered owner, and make the case available to the registered owner for pickup, upon receipt of payment for any case deposit which the distributor in possession may have paid for the case.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 620, Sec. 4.)
