## Text

This chapter, except as necessary to conduct an implementation referendum, shall not become operative until the director finds, in a referendum conducted by him or her, or a person designated by the director, that a favorable vote has been given pursuant to this article.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1286, Sec. 1.)
