## Text

The promotional, educational, economic, and marketing research, and any similar activities authorized by this chapter will serve to expand existing markets, create new markets for California cherries, and prevent economic waste.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1176, Sec. 1.)
