## Text

The commission may collect information, including, but not limited to, industry crop statistics, and publish and distribute without charge, a bulletin or other communication to handlers and producers of rice.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 567, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
