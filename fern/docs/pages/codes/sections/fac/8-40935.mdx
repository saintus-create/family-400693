## Text

If the director determines, as to any money heretofore or hereafter collected as inspection and certification fees, that the return of the money with respect to any processing season is impracticable because of the smallness of the amounts returnable, or for any other reason, the money so collected shall not be so returned but shall remain in the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund and be available for expenditure for the purposes of this chapter in any subsequent year.
