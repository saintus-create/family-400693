## Text

Between May 1, 1987, and April 30, 1988, the commission shall cause a referendum to be conducted among producers in the manner prescribed in Section 72131.5 to determine whether the operation of this chapter shall be reapproved and continued effective. If the director finds that a favorable vote has been given, he or she shall so certify and this chapter shall remain effective. If the director finds that a favorable vote has not been given, he or she shall so certify and declare the operation of this chapter and the commission suspended upon the expiration of the current marketing season ending April 30, 1988. Thereupon, the operations of the commission shall be concluded and funds distributed in the manner provided in Section 72134. No bond or security shall be required for any such referendum.
