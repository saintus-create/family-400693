## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in this article, the secretary shall certify and declare this chapter inoperative. The secretary may conduct another implementation referendum vote one or more years after the previous vote was taken.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 65. Effective July 18, 1995.)
