## Text

The commission may conduct, and contract with others to conduct, research, including the study, analysis, accumulation, and dissemination of information obtained from the research or elsewhere, regarding this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 550, Sec. 1. Effective August 27, 1990.)
