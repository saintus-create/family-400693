## Text

“Marketing season” or “fiscal year” are synonymous terms and mean the period beginning July 1 of any year and extending through June 30 of the following year.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 344, Sec. 1. (SB 250) Effective January 1, 2014.)
