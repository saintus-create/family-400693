## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 67122, he or she shall certify and give notice of that favorable vote to all producers and handlers whose names and addresses may be on file with the secretary. This chapter shall become operative on the 15th day after certification by the secretary and publication to producers and handlers.
