## Text

The director, in addition to other remedies which are provided by law, may direct suit in the name of the people of this state as plaintiff, to be brought for the recovery of fees as provided in this chapter. In such case, the director may make the necessary affidavit for and a writ of attachment may issue. All fees collected in this manner shall be handled as provided in this article.
