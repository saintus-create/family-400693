## Text

The packaging of dry curd cottage cheese or cottage cheese, and the addition of milk products to dry curd cottage cheese to make cottage cheese shall take place only in a licensed milk products plant.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 5. Effective August 21, 1997.)
