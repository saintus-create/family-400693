## Text

The maintenance of the asparagus industry of California is necessary to assure the consuming public of a continuous supply of this product and the maintenance of needed levels of income for those engaged in the industry.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1058, Sec. 1. Effective September 19, 1990.)
