## Text

When the director is required to concur in a decision of the commission, the director shall concur, refuse to concur, or request additional information from the commission within 15 working days from the date the director receives notification of the decision.

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 595, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
