## Text

When the director is required to determine whether or not to concur with a decision of the commission, he shall indicate his response to the commission within 15 working days after notification of the decision. The response may be a request that additional information be provided.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 891, Sec. 1.)
