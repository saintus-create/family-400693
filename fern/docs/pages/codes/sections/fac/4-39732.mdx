## Text

UHT nonfat, skim, or fat-free milk shall contain not more than twenty hundredths of 1 percent of milk fat and not less than 8.25 percent milk solids-not-fat.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 46. Effective August 21, 1997.)
