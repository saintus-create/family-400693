## Text

“Handler” means any person engaged in marketing of asparagus which the person has produced, purchased, or acquired from a producer, or is marketing on behalf of a producer whether as owner, agent, employee, broker, processor, or otherwise.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1058, Sec. 1. Effective September 19, 1990.)
