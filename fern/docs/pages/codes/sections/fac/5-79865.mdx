## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in this article, he or she shall certify the vote and give notice of the vote to producers whose names and addresses are on file with the secretary.

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 344, Sec. 1. (SB 250) Effective January 1, 2014.)
