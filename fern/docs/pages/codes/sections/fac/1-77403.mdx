## Text

The maintenance of the strawberry industry of California is necessary to assure the public of a continuous supply of this vital product and the maintenance of needed levels of income for those engaged in the strawberry industry of this state.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 805, Sec. 1. Effective October 4, 1993.)
