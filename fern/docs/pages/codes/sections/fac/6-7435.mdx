## Text

The secretary may establish regulations and enforcement provisions for purposes of this chapter consistent with the intent expressed in this division and any action shall be liberally construed to effectuate the intent of this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 651, Sec. 2. (AB 402) Effective January 1, 2024. Inoperative July 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 7440. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 7440.)
