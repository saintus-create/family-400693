## Text

If a passing score of 70 percent is not achieved, the applicant may not retake the examination for seven calendar days.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 705, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996. Section operative January 1, 1997, pursuant to Section 14099.5.)
