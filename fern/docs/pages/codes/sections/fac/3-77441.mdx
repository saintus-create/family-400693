## Text

The commission shall be and is hereby declared and created a corporate body. It may sue and be sued, enter into contracts, adopt a corporate seal, and has all of the powers of a corporation. Copies of its proceedings, records, and acts, when authenticated, shall be admissible in evidence in all courts of the state, and shall be prima facie evidence of the truth of all statements therein.
