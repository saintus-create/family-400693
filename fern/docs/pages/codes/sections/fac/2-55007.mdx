## Text

“Records” means books, records, contracts, documents, memoranda, papers, correspondence, or other data, whether in written, magnetic, or electronic form, that pertain to matters relating to this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 414, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2009.)
