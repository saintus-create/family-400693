## Text

If the director finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 72092, he shall certify and give notice of the favorable vote to all producers and handlers whose names and addresses may be on file with the director.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 891, Sec. 1.)
