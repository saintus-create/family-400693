## Text

(a) There is in the state government the California Salmon Council. The council shall be composed of nine voting members and their alternates, and three nonvoting members.

(b) If handlers have become subject to this chapter pursuant to Article 9.5 (commencing with Section 76961), the council shall be composed of 11 voting members and their alternates, and three nonvoting members.
