## Text

“Dairy farm” means any place or premises upon which milk is produced for sale or other distribution and where more than two cows or water buffalo, or six goats, sheep, or other hooved mammals, are in lactation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 460, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2005.)
