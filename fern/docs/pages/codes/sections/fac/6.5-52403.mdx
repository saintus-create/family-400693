## Text

Each seed-certifying agency shall establish a schedule of fees, in its discretion with input from the seed industry, for certification services provided and research conducted pursuant to this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 715, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
