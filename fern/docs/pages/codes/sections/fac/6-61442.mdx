## Text

The director shall have access to, and may enter at all reasonable hours, any place where any dairy product is being processed, bottled, stored, kept, or sold, or where the books, papers, records, or documents pertaining to any transaction which relates to any dairy product is kept. He may inspect and copy such books, papers, records, or documents in any place within the state.
