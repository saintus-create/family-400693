## Text

Between October 1, 1987, and September 30, 1988, the commission shall conduct a referendum among producers in the manner prescribed in Section 73202 to determine whether the operation of this chapter shall be reapproved and continued in effect.

If the director finds that a favorable vote has been given, he or she shall so certify and this chapter shall remain operative. If the director finds that a favorable vote has not been given, he or she shall so certify and declare the operation of this chapter and the commission suspended upon the expiration of the marketing season ending September 30, 1988. Thereupon, the operations of the commission shall be concluded and funds distributed in the manner provided in Section 73355.

No bond or security is required for the referendum.
