## Text

Any assessment which is levied as provided for in this chapter is a personal debt of every producer so assessed. Failure of a handler to collect the assessment does not relieve the producer of the obligation to pay the assessment.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Effective April 30, 1987.)
