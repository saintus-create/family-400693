## Text

(a) Each producer shall have one vote and the vote shall be individually cast in order to prevent block voting. The secretary shall prepare a ballot. The ballot form shall be substantially as follows:

Ballot

Shall Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 62750) of Part 3 of Division 21 of the Food and Agricultural Code be continued in effect? YesNo (b) In addition, the ballot shall include a statement of the voter’s total production during the calendar month next preceding the month of the commencement of the referendum period, where and to whom that production was sold or otherwise disposed, and the producer’s name and address and pooling numbers.
