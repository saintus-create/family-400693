## Text

If the expense of removal or destruction of any abandoned plant or crop becomes a lien on property which has been deeded to the state for nonpayment of taxes, as provided in Sections 5635 to 5637, inclusive, and the property is redeemed or sold to a private person at a tax sale, the action to foreclose the lien shall be commenced within 120 days after the redemption or sale of the property.
