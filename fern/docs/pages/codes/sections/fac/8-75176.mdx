## Text

Upon suspension of the operation of this chapter and of the commission, the commission shall mail a copy of the notice of suspension to all handlers affected by the suspension whose names and addresses are on file.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1093, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1995.)
