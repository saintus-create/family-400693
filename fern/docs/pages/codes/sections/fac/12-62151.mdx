## Text

The director may revoke or suspend, as the case may require, any license which is issued pursuant to this chapter, if he or she is satisfied that any licensee or any person who is connected with the licensee has violated any of the following:

(a) This chapter or any stabilization and marketing plan which is formulated pursuant to this chapter.

(b) Any regulation which is adopted by the director pursuant to Section 61891.

(c) Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 61301).

(d) Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 62700) or any pooling plan adopted thereunder.

The proceedings shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, and the director shall have all the powers which are granted in that chapter.
