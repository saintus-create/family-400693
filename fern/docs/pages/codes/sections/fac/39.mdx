## Text

“Qualified representative of the commissioner” means a deputy commissioner or inspector who holds an appropriate certificate of qualification issued by the director as provided in Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 2101) of Division 2 of this code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 26.)
