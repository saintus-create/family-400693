## Text

Seasonal marketing regulations shall be applicable to a particular marketing order or agreement for the purpose of carrying into effect by administrative order, the marketing regulatory authorizations and provisions of such marketing order or agreement as such authorizations or provisions may be applicable to, or required by, changing economic or marketing conditions and requirements, from time to time, during each marketing season in which such marketing order or agreement may operate.
