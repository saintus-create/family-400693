## Text

“Market milk” has the meaning of that term as defined in Section 32510 and has the same meaning as “restricted use market milk” as defined in Section 32516.5. Unless the context otherwise indicates, “market milk” includes market cream, the components and derivatives of market milk, and dairy products manufactured from market milk or its components and derivatives.
