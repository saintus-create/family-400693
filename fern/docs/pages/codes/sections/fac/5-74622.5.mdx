## Text

For the purpose of voting in the referendum required in Section 74621, only a person who would be required to pay the assessment pursuant to Section 74652.5 shall be considered a vintner.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 8, Sec. 2. Effective March 27, 1987.)
