## Text

Every dairy produce exchange, and any person that desires to operate a dairy produce exchange, before engaging in such activity, shall file with the director a written application for a license, accompanied by the license fee which is prescribed by Section 57134. The application shall state all of the following information:

(a) The name under which the dairy produce exchange proposes to operate.

(b) The location of its place of business.

(c) The names of its officers, directors, and the persons, firms, corporations, or associations which constitute its membership.

(d) A certified copy of its articles of incorporation, its constitution and bylaws, and the rules and regulations and bases of trade under which it proposes to operate.
