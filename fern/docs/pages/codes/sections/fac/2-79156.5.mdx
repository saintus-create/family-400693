## Text

“Fiscal year” and “marketing season” have the same meaning as the period of February 1 of any year and extending through January 31 of the following year.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 503, Sec. 1. (AB 944) Effective January 1, 2018. Provisions operative or inoperative as prescribed in Sections 79180.5, 79181.5, and 79195 et. seq. Repealed conditionally pursuant to Section 79182.5.)
