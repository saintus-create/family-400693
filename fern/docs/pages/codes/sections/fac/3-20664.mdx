## Text

The application shall contain all of the following:

(a) A facsimile of the brand which is sought to be recorded.

(b) A statement of the location on the animal where the brand is to be applied.

(c) The name and address of the applicant.

(d) The signature of a parent or guardian if the applicant is under 18 years of age.
