## Text

The maintenance and expansion of the wine industry of California and of its local, national, and foreign markets is necessary to assure the consuming public of a continuous supply of these products and the maintenance of needed levels of income for those engaged in the wine industry in this state.

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 3.)
