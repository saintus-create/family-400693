## Text

The board is authorized to annually assess cotton growers in the district in an amount not to exceed one-half of 1 percent of each grower’s gross dollar value from the previous year’s cotton crop.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 189, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 2009. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
