## Text

Each processor that receives deliveries of tomatoes for processing purposes is hereby designated as the authorized agent of the director to collect the inspection and certification fees which are chargeable to the processor and to each producer that delivers tomatoes.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 21.)
