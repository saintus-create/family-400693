## Text

A district agricultural association which did not receive an apportionment on the basis of the premiums which were paid at a fair that was held during the calendar year 1941 may receive an apportionment unless a county whose area lies in whole or in part within the boundaries of the agricultural district received an apportionment on the basis of the premiums which were paid at a fair that was held during the calendar year 1941.
