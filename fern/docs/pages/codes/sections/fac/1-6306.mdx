## Text

Unless otherwise permitted by law, any person who willfully and knowingly imports into, or who willfully and knowingly transports or ships within, this state, a Mediterranean fruit fly is guilty of a felony.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 167, Sec. 1. Effective June 22, 1990.)
