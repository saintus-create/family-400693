## Text

Upon certification of the commission, the producer and vintner members and their alternate members who are then serving on the California Wine and Winegrape Improvement Program made effective by the director on August 21, 1984, shall become the initial members and alternate members of the commission and shall serve in accordance with the requirements of Section 74071.
