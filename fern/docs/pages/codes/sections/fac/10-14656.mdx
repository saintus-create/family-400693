## Text

Upon demand of the person who has control of the seized fertilizing material, and within 10 days of sampling by the secretary, a subsample shall be returned from the state laboratory to the person in control of the fertilizing material.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 490, Sec. 31. Effective January 1, 2006.)
