## Text

Any lot of more than one container of seed which is transported to a dealer for resale, or any lot of more than five containers of seed which is sold to a consumer, is exempt from the requirements which are prescribed by Section 52452 or 52453 if both of the following requirements are complied with:

(a) Each container is plainly marked or identified with a lot number or other lot identification.

(b) The invoice and one or more of the containers bears the tag or label which is required by such sections.
