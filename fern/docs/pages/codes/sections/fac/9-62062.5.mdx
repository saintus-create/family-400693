## Text

The minimum prices otherwise established pursuant to this chapter shall be increased by any security charges that are imposed pursuant to Chapter 2.5 (commencing with Section 62500).

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 156, Sec. 24. Effective July 10, 1987.)
