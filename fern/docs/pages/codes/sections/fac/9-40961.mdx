## Text

It is unlawful for any person to deliver or to accept tomatoes for processing purposes, or to process any tomatoes, which have not been certified as meeting the requirements of this chapter or the regulations established pursuant to this chapter.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 26.)
