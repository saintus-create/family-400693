## Text

“Kiwifruit” means any variety of kiwifruit (“Kiwi,” “Chinese Gooseberry,” or species Actinidia deliciosa) produced in the state, including any kiwifruit delivered to a processor for processing into any kiwifruit product.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1127, Sec. 1. Effective October 11, 1993.)
