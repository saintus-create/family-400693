## Text

This chapter, except as necessary to conduct an implementation referendum, shall not become operative until the secretary finds, in a referendum conducted by him or her, or a person designated by the secretary, that a favorable vote has been given pursuant to this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 65. Effective January 1, 1996.)
