## Text

Federal nutritional labeling regulations apply to all frozen dairy products, except nutritional labeling provided for any frozen dairy product mix or any frozen dairy product dispensed by a soft serve establishment, shall state, on the basis of weight, the serving size upon which nutritional declarations are based.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 778, Sec. 1.)
