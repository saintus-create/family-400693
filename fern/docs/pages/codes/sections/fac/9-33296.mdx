## Text

The secretary shall make, and maintain, written findings upon which inspection fees are fixed pursuant to Sections 33294 and 33295. The written findings shall be readily available to any interested person for examination.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 584, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2007.)
