## Text

All proprietary information obtained by the commission or the secretary from producers or handlers is confidential and shall not be disclosed except when required by a court order after a hearing in a judicial proceeding involving this chapter.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 110. Effective January 1, 1996.)
