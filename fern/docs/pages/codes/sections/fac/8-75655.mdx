## Text

After the effective date of the suspension, the operation of the commission shall be concluded and any and all funds remaining, held by the commission, and not required to defray the expenses of concluding and terminating the operations of the commission, shall be returned upon a pro rata basis to all persons from whom assessments were collected in the immediately preceding marketing year. However, if the commission finds that the amounts so returnable are so small as to make impractical the computation and remitting of the pro rata refund to these persons, any funds remaining, after the payment of all expenses of winding up and terminating operations, shall be withdrawn from the approved depository and paid into an appropriate program conducted by the University of California or the California State University, or another state agency that deals with the purposes of this chapter. If no program exists, the funds shall be paid into the State Treasury as unclaimed trust funds.
