## Text

“Commercial feed” includes all materials which are intended for use as feed or for mixing in feed except preparations which are manufactured and distributed for feeding to domestic pets, such as dogs, cats, and birds.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1275.)
