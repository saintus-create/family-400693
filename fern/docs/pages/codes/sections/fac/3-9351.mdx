## Text

It is unlawful for any person to willfully sell, except for immediate slaughter, or expose in such a manner as may infect other livestock not so infected, any cattle which have or are infected with the Boophilus annulatus tick.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
