## Text

“Product promotion” means the generic promotion of forest products in domestic and foreign markets. “Product promotion” does not include reference to either unprocessed logs sold in foreign markets or to rough-sawn products sold in foreign markets for remanufacture.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 550, Sec. 1. Effective August 27, 1990.)
