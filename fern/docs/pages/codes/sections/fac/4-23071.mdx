## Text

Every person that violates any provision of this division is guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction, shall be punished by a fine of not less than one hundred dollars ($100) nor more than one thousand dollars ($1,000), or by imprisonment of not less than 10 days nor more than 6 months, or by both such fine and imprisonment. Each violation during any day constitutes a separate offense.
