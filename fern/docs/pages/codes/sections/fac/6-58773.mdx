## Text

The director shall also mail a copy of such notice of hearing and a copy of such proposed marketing order, or proposed amendments, to every producer or handler of such commodity whose name and address appears upon lists of such persons, on file in the department that may be directly affected by the provisions of such proposed marketing order or such proposed amendments.
