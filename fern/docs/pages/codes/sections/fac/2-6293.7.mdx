## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given by publication in a newspaper of general circulation published and circulated in the district. Notice of the hearing shall also be given to all winegrape growing acreage owners within the proposed district.

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 442, Sec. 1. Effective October 3, 2001.)
