## Text

UHT milk drink mix shall contain not less than 12 percent total milk solids, not less than 4 percent butterfat, and not more than one-half of 1 percent pure and harmless edible stabilizer by weight. It may contain sweetening and harmless coloring and flavoring.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 646, Sec. 17. Effective September 15, 1987.)
