## Text

(a) There is in state government the California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission. The commission shall be composed of nine grape rootstock nursery owners or representatives who shall be called members.

(b) The director and other appropriate individuals, as determined by the commission, shall be ex officio members of the commission.
