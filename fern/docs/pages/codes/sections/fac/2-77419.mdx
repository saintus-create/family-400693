## Text

“Marketing season” or “fiscal year” are synonymous terms and mean the period beginning February 1 of any year and extending through the last day of January of the following year.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 805, Sec. 1. Effective October 4, 1993.)
