## Text

If the secretary determines, as to any money collected as inspection and certification fees, that the return of the money with respect to any dehydration season is impracticable because of the smallness of the amounts returnable, or for any other reason, the money so collected shall not be returned but shall remain in the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund and be available for expenditure for the purposes of this chapter in any subsequent year.
