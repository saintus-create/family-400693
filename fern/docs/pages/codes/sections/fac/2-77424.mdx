## Text

“Ship” and “shipping” are synonymous terms and mean the operations of receiving, grading, cleaning, transporting, distributing, or otherwise preparing for market or marketing strawberries in other than processed form.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 113.5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
