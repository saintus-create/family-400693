## Text

The refusal of any officer who is authorized under this division to carry out the orders and directions of the director in the enforcement of this division is neglect of duty; provided that the failure of any such officer to carry out such orders and directions shall not be neglect of duty if funds necessary to carry out such orders and directions are not provided.
