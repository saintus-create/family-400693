## Text

“Olive oil” means the oil obtained solely from the fruit of the olive tree (Olea europea L.) which is produced in California for commercial purposes.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 344, Sec. 1. (SB 250) Effective January 1, 2014.)
