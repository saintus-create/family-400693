## Text

The director, citrus grower trade organizations primarily involved in issues affecting the orange industry, and other appropriate individuals and organizations, as determined by the commission, are ex officio members of the commission.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
