## Text

Regulations which are adopted by the director pursuant to this division shall not conflict with Agricultural Research Service of the United States Department of Agriculture orders or regulations which pertain to garbage that is derived from meats or meat products which originate in any country which is listed as a country in which there are animals which are infected with the disease known as rinderpest or with foot-and-mouth disease.
