## Text

Every handler is personally liable for the payment of the collected assessments, and failure of the handler to collect the assessment from any producer does not exempt the handler from this liability.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
