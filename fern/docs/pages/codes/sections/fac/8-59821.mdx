## Text

A marketing program may authorize the marketing program committee to provide for the adjustment of production of any commodity by means of tree or vine pulling. The marketing program committee may receive applications from producers for acreage adjustment payments. The marketing program committee shall, upon proper review and certification, make such acreage adjustment payments on an equitable basis from funds which are collected for that purpose under the marketing program on a uniform basis from all commercial producers of the commodity within the proration zone, or from funds which are received from any federal, state, or other agency for that purpose.
