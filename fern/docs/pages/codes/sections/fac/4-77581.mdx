## Text

The commission may enter into contracts to receive or render services in formulating and conducting plans and programs and any other contracts or agreements that the commission may deem necessary for carrying out this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 550, Sec. 1. Effective August 27, 1990.)
