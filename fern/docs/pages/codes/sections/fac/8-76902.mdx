## Text

Except as specified in Section 76905, the amount of fees due under this article shall be determined by multiplying the weight in the round of salmon by two cents ($0.02). For the purposes of establishing the weight in the round of salmon brought ashore, a conversion factor of 10 percent shall be applied to the weight of salmon brought ashore in a dressed condition.
