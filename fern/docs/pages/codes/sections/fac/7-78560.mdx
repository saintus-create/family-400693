## Text

The director, after consultation with the council, shall adopt an annual budget which sets forth the proposed expenditures which the director deems necessary for the performance by him or her and by the department of the duties which are imposed upon the director and the department by this chapter. The director shall also prepare and submit to the council an annual statement of the director’s expenditures pursuant to this chapter.
