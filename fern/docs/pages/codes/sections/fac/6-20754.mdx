## Text

Except as otherwise provided in Section 20755, the owner of a brand shall, on or before April 30 after its recordation, pay to the bureau a biennial period renewal fee of seventy dollars ($70) for the right to continue to use the brand.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 406, Sec. 2. (AB 732) Effective January 1, 2016.)
