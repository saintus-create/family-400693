## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative at the end of any marketing year in which targeted export assistance funds provided by the federal government have been permanently terminated, unless, pursuant to the procedures described in Section 77193, the producers approve the continuation of the commission. Funds are permanently terminated within the meaning of the previous sentence when the federal government does not provide targeted export assistance funds in three consecutive budget years.
