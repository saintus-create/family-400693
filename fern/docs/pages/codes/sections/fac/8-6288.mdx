## Text

The board of supervisors is ex officio the governing body of the dissolved district. It may levy such assessments and perform such other acts as may be necessary to wind up the affairs of the district and to raise money for the payment of any outstanding indebtedness.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1078, Sec. 1. Effective October 11, 1993.)
