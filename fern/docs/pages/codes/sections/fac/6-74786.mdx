## Text

Every nursery, including nurseries exempt from the payment of assessments, shall keep a complete and accurate record of its acreage of grape rootstock and its grape rootstock used or distributed for commercial purposes. The records shall be in simple form and contain information as the commission shall prescribe. The records shall be retained by the nursery for five years and shall be offered and submitted for inspection at any reasonable time upon written demand of the commission or its duly authorized agent.
