## Text

The promotional, educational, economic and marketing research, and any similar activities authorized by this chapter will serve to expand existing markets and create new markets for California iceberg lettuce and to prevent agricultural waste and maintain employment, and are, therefore, in the interests of the welfare, economy, and health of the people of California.
