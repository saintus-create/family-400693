## Text

“Public member” means the person appointed by the commission pursuant to Section 71050. The public member and his or her alternate shall not have any financial interest in the production or handling of rice.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 567, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
