## Text

(a) Prior to the beginning of each marketing season or as soon thereafter as possible, the commission shall establish an assessment rate for the marketing season.

(b) Unless approved by a two-thirds vote of the commission, the assessment rate shall not exceed 1 percent of the gross dollar value of winegrapes processed by vintners.
