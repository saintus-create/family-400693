## Text

Subsequent to the first election of board members pursuant to this chapter, persons to be elected to the commission shall be selected pursuant to nomination and election procedures which are established by the commission with the concurrence of the director.

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 595, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
