## Text

The secretary may, by regulation, adopt official final action methods of testing for the determination of the components of milk and products of milk as required to meet a legal compositional requirement of this division.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 371, Sec. 2. (AB 425) Effective January 1, 2022.)
