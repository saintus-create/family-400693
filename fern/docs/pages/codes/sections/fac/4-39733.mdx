## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, UHT nonfat, skim, or fat-free milk shall meet all the standards and requirements that are specified in this division for market milk.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 47. Effective August 21, 1997.)
