## Text

A quorum of the commission is five voting commissioners. Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, the commission may act by a vote of a majority of the members present at a meeting at which there is a quorum.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 531, Sec. 1.)
