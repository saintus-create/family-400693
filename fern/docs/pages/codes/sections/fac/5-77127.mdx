## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in Section 77123, the secretary shall so certify and declare this chapter inoperative. The secretary may conduct another implementation referendum vote one year or more after the previous vote has been taken.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 105. Effective January 1, 1996.)
