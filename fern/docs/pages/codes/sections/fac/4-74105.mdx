## Text

The commission may establish an annual budget according to accepted accounting practices. The budget shall be concurred in by the director prior to disbursement of funds, except for disbursements made pursuant to Section 74096.

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1286, Sec. 1.)
