## Text

The public member or his or her alternate shall have all the powers, rights, responsibilities, and privileges of any other member on the commission.

The public member shall not have any financial interest in the navel orange industry during the entire term of office.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
