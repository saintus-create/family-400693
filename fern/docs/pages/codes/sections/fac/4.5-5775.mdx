## Text

If the date of a pesticide application is changed, the notice required by this article shall be redistributed and contain the revised information. Additionally, the secretary shall transmit the revised information to the local broadcast and print media, including not less than two radio stations providing the broadest coverage in the eradication area. No pesticide shall be applied within 96 hours from the date of that change.
