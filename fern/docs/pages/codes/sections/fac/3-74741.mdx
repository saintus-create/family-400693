## Text

A quorum of the commission is seven voting members of the commission. The vote of a majority of the board members or alternate members present at a meeting at which there is a quorum shall constitute the act of the commission.

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 595, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
