## Text

It is unlawful for any person to operate as a wholesaler of fruits and vegetables under this division in any county with registration as established pursuant to this article unless that person is registered and has paid the required fee pursuant to this article.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 450, Sec. 1.)
