## Text

The California Horse Racing Board shall, at the request of the commission, certify the eligibility of any prospective lessee or user of the property to be licensed to conduct horseracing at the Del Mar Race Track.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 540, Sec. 2.)
