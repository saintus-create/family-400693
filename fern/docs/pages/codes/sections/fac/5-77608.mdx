## Text

Subsequent to the first election of members and alternate members of the commission pursuant to this chapter, persons to be elected to the commission shall be selected pursuant to nomination and election procedures established by the commission with the approval of the secretary.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 134. Effective January 1, 1996.)
