## Text

“Process” or “processed” means producing must, grape juice, grape concentrate, wine, or other winegrape products, including high proof and brandy from winegrapes.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 597, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
