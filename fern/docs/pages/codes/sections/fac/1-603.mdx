## Text

Any building or improvement that is constructed by the department pursuant to this chapter shall be primarily for the occupancy of the department and its commissions, divisions, bureaus, advisory boards, and services.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 198, Sec. 4. (SB 1499) Effective January 1, 2023.)
