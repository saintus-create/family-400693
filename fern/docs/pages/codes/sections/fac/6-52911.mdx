## Text

This chapter does not apply to the transportation of cottonseed through the district established by this chapter, from a point outside of the district to a destination outside of the district.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 592. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
