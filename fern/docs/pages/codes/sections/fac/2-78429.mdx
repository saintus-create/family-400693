## Text

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, that invalidity does not affect other provisions or applications of the act which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this act are severable.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1151, Sec. 1.)
