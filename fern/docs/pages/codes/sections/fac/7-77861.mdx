## Text

It is unlawful for any person to do any of the following:

(a) Fail to render or furnish a report, statement, or record required by the commission.

(b) Willfully render or furnish a false report, statement, or record required by the commission.

(c) Secrete, destroy, or alter records required to be kept under this chapter.
