## Text

Upon suspension of the operation of this chapter, the commission shall mail a copy of the notice of suspension to all producers whose names and addresses are on file with the commission and to the appropriate policy committees in both the Assembly and Senate.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. Effective August 10, 1990.)
