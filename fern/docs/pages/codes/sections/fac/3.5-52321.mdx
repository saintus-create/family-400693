## Text

All money that is received by the director pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund and shall be expended only for the purpose of this chapter.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 277, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
