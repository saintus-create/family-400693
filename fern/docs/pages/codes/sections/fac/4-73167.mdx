## Text

The commission shall prepare an annual budget according to accepted accounting practices.

Except for disbursements to pay the compensation of the employees of the commission, no disbursement of funds shall be made unless the director concurs in the budget.

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
