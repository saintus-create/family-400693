## Text

The rights, remedies, and penalties that are provided for in this chapter are in addition to any other rights, remedies, or penalties that are provided for by law, and supercede provisions of law in conflict therewith.

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 696, Sec. 50.6. Effective January 1, 1998.)
