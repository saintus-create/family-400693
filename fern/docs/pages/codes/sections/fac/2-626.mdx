## Text

Money shall not be transferred pursuant to Section 622 to the Department of Agriculture Building Fund if it will interfere with the administrative purposes for which the money was collected.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 198, Sec. 8. (SB 1499) Effective January 1, 2023.)
