## Text

The commission may administer and enforce this chapter and to do and perform all acts and exercise all powers incidental to, or in connection with, or deemed reasonably necessary for proper or advisable effectuation of this chapter. The commission’s authority includes, but is not limited to, entering into contracts for services with public and private agencies which are deemed necessary by the commission to carry out this chapter.
