## Text

UHT half-and-half is a product consisting of market milk, market cream, nonfat dry milk derived from market milk, and market skim milk to which edible stabilizers and stabilizing salts may be added. It shall be processed and packaged pursuant to Section 39701.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 646, Sec. 17. Effective September 15, 1987.)
