## Text

If an approved milk inspection service inspects a dairy farm, the dairy farm inspection fee, if levied, shall be collected from the producer of milk that is produced on the dairy farm.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 39, Sec. 15. (SB 1018) Effective June 27, 2012.)
