## Text

The director, at least once each month, shall report to the Controller the total amount of any money which is collected under Section 35221. He or she shall, at the same time, pay into the State Treasury the entire amount of the receipts which shall be credited to the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund and expended as provided in Section 32704.
