## Text

The owner or person in possession of the ungraded honey shall, on demand of any enforcing officer under this chapter, give to the officer a sworn statement in writing, which was made before a notary public, that specifies all of the following:

(a) That the honey will be disposed of or used pursuant to this article.

(b) The name and address of the person to whom such honey is to be delivered.

(c) An accurate identification of the honey.
