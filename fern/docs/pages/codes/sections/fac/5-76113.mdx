## Text

(a) The director shall establish a period in which to conduct the referendum which shall not be less than 10 days nor more than 60 days in duration, and may prescribe additional procedures as may be necessary to conduct the referendum.

(b) If the initial period established is less than 60 days, the director may extend the period. However, the total referendum period may not exceed 60 days.
