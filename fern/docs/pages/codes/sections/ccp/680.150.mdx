## Text

“Costs” means costs and disbursements, including but not limited to statutory fees, charges, commissions, and expenses.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
