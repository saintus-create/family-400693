## Text

(a) During the pendency of the consumer arbitration, a solicitation shall not be made of a party to the arbitration or of a lawyer for a party to the arbitration.

(b) For purposes of this section:

(1) “Lawyer for a party” has the same meaning as defined in Section 1281.9.

(2) “Solicitation” has the same meaning as defined in Section 1281.9.
