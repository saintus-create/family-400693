## Text

The county elections official’s certificate to nomination papers of a candidate shall be in substantially the following form:COUNTY ELECTION OFFICIAL’S CERTIFICATE TO NOMINATION PAPERS OF A CANDIDATETo the Secretary of State:I, County Elections Official of the County of _____________, hereby certify that I have examined the nomination papers, to which this certificate is attached, of the candidate for the ensuing presidential primary, and that the number of names which I have not marked “not sufficient” is ______________.The candidate named in the nomination papers is ______________.Dated this ____________ day of ____________, 20____.________________________ , County Elections OfficialBy____________________ , Deputy
