## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no positive test results obtained pursuant to this title shall be disclosed to any person unless the initial positive test result has been confirmed by appropriate confirmatory tests for positive reactors.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1579, Sec. 2. Effective September 30, 1988.)
