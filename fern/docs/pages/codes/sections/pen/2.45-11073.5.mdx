## Text

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 10, Sec. 21. (AB 134) Effective June 27, 2025. Conditionally operative pursuant to Section 11073.6. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 2.45, comprising Sections 11073 to 11073.6.)
