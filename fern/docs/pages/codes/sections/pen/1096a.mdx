## Text

In charging a jury, the court may read to the jury Section 1096, and no further instruction on the subject of the presumption of innocence or defining reasonable doubt need be given.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 46, Sec. 2. Effective July 3, 1995.)
