## Text

Two million three hundred fifty-seven thousand seven hundred seventy-eight dollars ($2,357,778) shall be set aside initially for the counties that did not have juvenile facilities on January 1, 1987. These funds shall be used to construct county juvenile facilities and are hereby allocated as follows:Amador $ 33,000Calaveras 80,000Colusa 218,928Glenn 213,850Inyo 846,000Lassen 350,000Mariposa 50,000Modoc 126,000Mono 18,000Plumas 45,000San Benito 243,000Sierra 10,000Trinity 30,000Tuolumne 94,000
