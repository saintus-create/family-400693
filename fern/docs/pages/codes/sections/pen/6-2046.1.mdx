## Text

The prison authorized to be established by Section 2046 shall be a medium security type institution. Its primary purpose shall be to provide custody, care, industrial, vocational, and other training to persons confined therein.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 828, Sec. 112.)
