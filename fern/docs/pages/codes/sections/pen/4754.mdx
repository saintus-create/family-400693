## Text

As used in this chapter, “prisoner” means any person committed to a state prison, including a person who has been transferred to any other facility, has escaped, or is otherwise absent, but does not include a person while on parole.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1310, Sec. 8.)
