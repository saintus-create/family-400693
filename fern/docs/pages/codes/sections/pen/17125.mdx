## Text

As used in this part, “Security Exemplar” has the same meaning as in Section 922 of Title 18 of the United States Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
