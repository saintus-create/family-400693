## Text

(a) The department shall submit to the Department of Justice fingerprint images and related information required by the Department of Justice from an applicant applying for an annual gate clearance, program provider identification card, or statewide gate clearance, as defined in Section 7460, in accordance with subdivision (u) of Section 11105.

(b) The Department of Justice shall provide a state or federal response to the department pursuant to subdivision (l) of Section 11105.
