## Text

As used in this part, “used firearm” means a firearm that has been sold previously at retail and is more than three years old.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
