## Text

The Board of Parole Hearings may conduct proceedings by videoconference. All references in this article and Article 4 of Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 2960) of this title to a participant’s statutory right to meet, be present, appear, or to represent the interests of the people or another participant at a proceeding shall be satisfied by the participant’s appearance by videoconference at the proceeding.
