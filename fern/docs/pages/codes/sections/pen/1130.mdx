## Text

If the prosecuting attorney fails to attend at the trial of a felony, the court must appoint an attorney at law to perform the duties of the prosecuting attorney on such trial.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 389. Effective September 28, 1998.)
