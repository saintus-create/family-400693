## Text

The Secretary of the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation shall not charge a fee for an inmate-initiated medical visit of an inmate confined in the state prison.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 570, Sec. 5. (AB 45) Effective January 1, 2020.)
