## Text

The provisions of Part 3 (commencing with Section 2000) apply to the California Correctional Institution at Tehachapi and to the persons confined therein insofar as those provisions may be applicable.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 828, Sec. 114.)
