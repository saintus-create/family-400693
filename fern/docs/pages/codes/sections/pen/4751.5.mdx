## Text

Costs incurred by a superior court include all of the following:

(a) Costs of any trial or hearing of any matter set forth in Section 4750, including costs for the preparation of the trial, pretrial hearing, and the actual trial or hearing.

(b) Any other costs reasonably incurred by a superior court in connection with any matter set forth in Section 4750.
