## Text

The Bureau of Livestock Identification shall compile a report on information received pursuant to Section 24104 of the Food and Agricultural Code. The bureau shall distribute the report to all county sheriffs’ departments in a timely manner.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 431, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
