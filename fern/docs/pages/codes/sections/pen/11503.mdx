## Text

There is hereby created in the State Treasury the Local Public Prosecutors and Public Defenders Training Fund for the support of the Prosecutors and Public Defenders Education and Training Program, established pursuant to this title.

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 40, Sec. 1. Effective March 31, 1986.)
