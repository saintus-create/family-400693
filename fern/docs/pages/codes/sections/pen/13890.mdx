## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to review the needs assessment report, as provided for in Section 13892, prior to providing additional funds for support of local forensic laboratory services or improvements.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 931, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
