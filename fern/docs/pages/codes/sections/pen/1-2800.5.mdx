## Text

The Prison Industry Authority shall be known as the California Correctional Training and Rehabilitation Authority. Any reference to the Prison Industry Authority in this or any other code shall be construed to mean the California Correctional Training and Rehabilitation Authority.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 241, Sec. 36. (SB 857) Effective January 1, 2026.)
