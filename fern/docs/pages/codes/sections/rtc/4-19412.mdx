## Text

The action shall be tried in the County of Sacramento unless the court with the consent of the prosecutor orders a change of place of trial.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
