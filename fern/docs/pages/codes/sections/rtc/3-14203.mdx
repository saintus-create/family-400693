## Text

The Controller may issue subpoenas for any of the following purposes:

(a) Compelling the attendance of any person before him or her or the production of books and papers.

(b) Determining the amount of any tax due.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
