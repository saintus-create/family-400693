## Text

If judgment is rendered for the plaintiff, the amount of the judgment shall first be credited on any fee due from the plaintiff, and the balance shall be refunded to the plaintiff.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
