## Text

If the Franchise Tax Board disallows interest on any claim for refund, it shall notify the taxpayer accordingly.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
