## Text

If the ordinance proposing the transactions and use tax is not approved as required by Section 7295, this chapter shall be repealed as of January 1, 2026.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 579, Sec. 1. (SB 705) Effective January 1, 2016. Repealed conditionally on January 1, 2026, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.9, commencing with Section 7295.)
