## Text

If, as of January 1, 2029, an ordinance or citizens’ initiative proposing a transactions and use tax is not approved pursuant to Section 7292.9.1, this chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 852, Sec. 2. (AB 3259) Effective September 28, 2024. Conditionally repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.77, commencing with Section 7292.9.1.)
