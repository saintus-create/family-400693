## Text

No injunction, writ of mandate, or other legal or equitable process shall ever issue in any suit, action, or proceeding in any court against this state or any officer of this state to prevent or enjoin the collection of any tax imposed by this part.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
