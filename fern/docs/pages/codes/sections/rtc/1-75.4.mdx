## Text

“Current tax rate” means the tax rate applicable to the current roll, including any rate in excess of the limitation prescribed by subdivision (a) of Section 1 of Article XIIIA of the California Constitution.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1102, Sec. 1.5. Effective September 27, 1983.)
