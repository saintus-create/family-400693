## Text

In each county that elects to sell tax certificates, there shall be created a Tax Certificate Redemption Fund. Moneys in the Tax Certificate Redemption Fund shall be used exclusively for the purposes described in Section 4527.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 189, Sec. 7. Effective July 24, 1995.)
