## Text

The fees, commissions, and expenses are obligations of the person required to pay any amount under this part and may be collected from him or her by virtue of the warrant or in any manner provided in this part for the collection of the tax.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
