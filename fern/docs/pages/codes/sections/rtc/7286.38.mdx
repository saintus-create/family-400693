## Text

The transactions and use tax authorized under this chapter constitutes a special tax that requires approval of two-thirds of the qualified electors pursuant to Section 4 of Article XIIIA of the California Constitution.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 259, Sec. 1. Effective July 20, 1992.)
