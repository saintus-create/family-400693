## Text

Every retailer engaged in business in a city or county, or city and county, which has an operative ordinance enacted pursuant to this chapter shall, at the time of making the sales of aerosol paint containers or felt tip markers, collect the tax from the consumer and give to the consumer a receipt therefor in the manner and form prescribed by the State Board of Equalization.
