## Text

The provisions of Section 72(u) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the treatment of annuity contracts not held by natural persons, shall be applicable.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1139, Sec. 58. Effective September 25, 1987. Applicable to income years beginning on or after January 1, 1987, by Sec. 241 of Ch. 1139.)
