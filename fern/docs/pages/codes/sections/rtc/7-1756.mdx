## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2028, and as of that date is repealed.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 132, Sec. 1. (SB 447) Effective January 1, 2018. Repealed on January 1, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 1.5, commencing with Section 1750.)
