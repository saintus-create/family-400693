## Text

In the event that a bill is amended on the floor of either house, whether by adoption of the report of a conference committee or otherwise, in such a manner as to require reimbursement pursuant to Section 2229 or 2230, the Legislative Counsel shall immediately inform, respectively, the Speaker of the Assembly and the President of the Senate of this fact. The notification from the Legislative Counsel shall be published in the journal of the respective houses of the Legislature.
