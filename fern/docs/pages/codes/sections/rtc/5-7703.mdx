## Text

Whenever any supplier who is required to pay tax in weekly installments as provided by Section 7701 fails to make a weekly return or to pay the full amount in accordance with the terms and conditions prescribed by the board, the supplier’s license may be revoked forthwith.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
