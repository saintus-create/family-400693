## Text

Unless otherwise provided, any provision of this part that relates to the assessment and collection of tax shall also apply to the assessment and collection of estimated tax.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
