## Text

Proceedings for the collection of any tax imposed by this part may be commenced at any time after the tax is due and within 10 years from and after the time a delinquency determination is issued pursuant to the provisions of this part.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
