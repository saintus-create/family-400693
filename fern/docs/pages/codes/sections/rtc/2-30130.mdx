## Text

This article may be amended only by vote of four-fifths of the membership of both houses of the Legislature. All amendments to this article must be consistent with its purposes.

## History

(History: Added November 8, 1988, by initiative Proposition 99. Operative January 1, 1989, by Section 30128.)
