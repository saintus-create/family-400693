## Text

For the purpose of this article, “research” shall include, but not be limited to, expenditures to develop and advance the understanding, techniques, and modalities effective in the prevention, cure, screening, and treatment of breast cancer.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31. Inoperative on or before January 1, 2032, as prescribed in Section 18796. Repealed on or before December 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 18796.)
