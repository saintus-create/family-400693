## Text

The remedies of the State provided for in this chapter are cumulative, and no action taken by the Controller or the Attorney General constitutes an election by the State to pursue any remedy to the exclusion of any other remedy for which provision is made in this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 37.)
