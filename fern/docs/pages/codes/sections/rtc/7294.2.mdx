## Text

If, as of January 1, 2029, an ordinance or citizens’ initiative proposing a transactions and use tax is not approved as required by subdivision (a) of Section 7294.1, this chapter shall be repealed as of that same date.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 852, Sec. 3. (AB 3259) Effective September 28, 2024. Conditionally repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.81, commencing with Section 7294.1.)
