## Text

The Franchise Tax Board may appoint and remove, in the manner provided by law, those officers, agents, branch office income tax deputies, and other employees as it deems necessary. They shall have the duties and powers as the Franchise Tax Board from time to time prescribes.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
