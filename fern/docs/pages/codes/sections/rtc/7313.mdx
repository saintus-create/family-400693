## Text

“Finished gasoline” means all products (including gasohol) that are commonly known or sold as gasoline.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
