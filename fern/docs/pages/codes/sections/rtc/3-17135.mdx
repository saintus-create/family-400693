## Text

The use of an automobile by a special agent of federal or state taxing agencies shall be treated in the manner provided for by Section 1567 of Public Law 99-514.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1138, Sec. 56. Effective September 25, 1987. Applicable to taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 1987, by Sec. 189 of Ch. 1138.)
