## Text

Every position holder shall pay the tax on the removal of diesel fuel from a terminal as defined under Section 60051.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
