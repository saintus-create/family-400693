## Text

A penalty shall be imposed for promoting abusive tax shelters and shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of Section 6700 of the Internal Revenue Code, except as otherwise provided.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 691, Sec. 47.5. Effective October 7, 2005.)
