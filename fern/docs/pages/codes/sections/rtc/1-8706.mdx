## Text

The board or its authorized representatives may impound the vehicles of a user whose permit has been revoked and who continues to use fuel after having been duly notified by the board of the revocation. The board or its representatives may retain possession of the vehicles until the user fully complies with the provisions of this part and all costs incurred in connection with the impounding or storage of the vehicles have been paid.
