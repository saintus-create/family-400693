## Text

Any person from whom a tax is collected by withholding under this article or under Section 13020 of the Unemployment Insurance Code is entitled to the remedies set forth in Articles 1 (commencing with Section 19301) and 3 (commencing with Section 19381) of Chapter 6. Any refund of the tax under Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 19301) shall be made to the withholding agent instead of directly to the taxpayer, if requested in writing by the withholding agent at the time the amounts refundable were transmitted to the Franchise Tax Board.
