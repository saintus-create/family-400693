## Text

The board shall adopt such rules and regulations as may be necessary to coordinate so far as permitted by the provisions of this part the system of beer and wine taxation imposed by this part with the system of beer and wine taxation imposed by the internal revenue laws of the United States.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
