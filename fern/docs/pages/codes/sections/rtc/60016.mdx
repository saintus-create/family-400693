## Text

“Highway” includes any way or place, of whatever nature, that is publicly maintained and open to the use of the public for purposes of vehicular travel.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
