## Text

Any action or proceeding commenced before this part takes effect, or any right accrued, is not affected by this part, but these actions or proceedings shall conform to this part as far as possible.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1442, Sec. 6. Effective October 2, 1989.)
