## Text

The tax amnesty program shall be conducted during a two-month period beginning February 1, 2005, and ending March 31, 2005, inclusive, or during a timeframe ending no later than June 30, 2005, pursuant to Section 19733. The program shall apply to tax liabilities for taxable years beginning before January 1, 2003.
