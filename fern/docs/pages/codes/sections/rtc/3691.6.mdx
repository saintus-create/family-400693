## Text

Upon request of the Controller, the tax collector shall report the disposition of all tax-defaulted parcels subject to tax collections power to sell in his or her county.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 183, Sec. 321. Effective January 1, 2005.)
