## Text

For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2021, a nonresident alien’s gross income does not include any payment made by an agent pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 18537.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 73, Sec. 1. (AB 1518) Effective January 1, 2026.)
