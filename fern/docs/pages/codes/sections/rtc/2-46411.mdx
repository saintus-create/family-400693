## Text

The board may bring any legal actions as are necessary to collect any deficiency in the fee required to be paid, and, upon the board’s request, the Attorney General shall bring the actions.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. Effective August 1, 1991.)
