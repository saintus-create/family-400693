## Text

Whenever the Controller determines that the tax due under this part has been over paid, the person making payment shall be entitled to a refund of the amount erroneously paid on presentation of proof satisfactory to the Controller that he or she is entitled to a refund.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
