## Text

Every position holder shall pay the tax on the removal of motor vehicle fuel from a terminal as provided in Section 7362.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
