## Text

Every person operating a qualified motor vehicle within and without this state or the United States shall apply to the board for a license on forms prescribed by the board. It is unlawful for any person to be an interstate user without first securing a license.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 620, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 1998.)
