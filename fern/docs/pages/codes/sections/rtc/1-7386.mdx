## Text

“Aircraft” means any powered contrivance designed for navigation in the air except a rocket or missile.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
