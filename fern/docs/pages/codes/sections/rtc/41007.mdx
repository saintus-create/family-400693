## Text

(a) “Service supplier” shall mean a person supplying an access line to a service user in this state.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2020.

## History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 3) and added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 54, Sec. 4. (SB 96) Effective July 1, 2019. Section operative January 1, 2020, by its own provisions.)
