## Text

The Franchise Tax Board may require employers to submit copies of income tax withholding exemption certificates. If the Franchise Tax Board determines that a certificate is invalid for state income tax purposes, the Franchise Tax Board shall notify the employer and the affected employee of its determination. An employee who disagrees with the Franchise Tax Board’s determination may request review of the determination by filing a written petition in the form and within the time prescribed by the Franchise Tax Board. After review, the Franchise Tax Board shall give written notification of its decision to both the employer and the employee.
