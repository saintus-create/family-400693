## Text

The board shall notify the Controller of any and all determinations made pursuant to this chapter as well as the amounts self-determined under this part, and the Controller shall keep an appropriate record of all such matters.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
