## Text

(a) The place of use of a digital product shall be the place where any right or power is exercised over the digital product. The right or power to remotely access a digital product is exercised at the place where the person accessing the digital product is located.

(b) It shall be presumed that a digital product that was purchased outside of this state, as determined pursuant to Section 6010.5, and used in this state within 90 days from the date of sale or purchase was purchased for storage, use, or other consumption in this state.

(c) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2027.
