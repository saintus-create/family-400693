## Text

The fee imposed by Section 9008 of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (Public Law 111-148), shall be considered a tax described in Section 275(a)(6) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 359, Sec. 13. (AB 154) Effective September 30, 2015. Applicable to taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2015, as provided in Sec. 41 of Stats. 2015, Ch. 359.)
