## Text

“Blender” includes any person that produces or converts blended diesel fuel outside the bulk transfer/terminal system.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 50. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
