## Text

“Fuel tank” means any receptacle on an aircraft from which fuel is supplied for the propulsion of the aircraft.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
