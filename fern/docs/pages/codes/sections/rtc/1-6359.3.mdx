## Text

Any nonprofit veterans’ organization is a consumer of, and shall not be considered a retailer within the provisions of this part with respect to flags of the United States of America which it sells, where the profits are used solely and exclusively in furtherance of the purposes of the nonprofit organization.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1741.)
