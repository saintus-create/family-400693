## Text

The Franchise Tax Board shall transmit promptly to the Treasurer all moneys and remittances received by it under this part. It shall at the same time furnish copies of the schedules covering the transmittals to the Controller.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
