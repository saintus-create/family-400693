## Text

No tax shall be included in the sales price or on the sales invoice by the ultimate vendor on diesel fuel sold to an ultimate purchaser.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
