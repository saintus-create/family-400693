## Text

For the purpose of refund claims filed pursuant to this article, diesel fuel containing a de minimis quantity of dye shall not be considered diesel fuel containing visible evidence of dye.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
