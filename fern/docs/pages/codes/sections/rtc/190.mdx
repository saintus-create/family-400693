## Text

It is the intent of this chapter to provide immediate tax relief where property was damaged during the February 1986 storms and floods and to ensure that local governments receive the property tax revenues necessary to carry out their operations in the 1985–86 fiscal year.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1110, Sec. 6. Effective September 24, 1986.)
