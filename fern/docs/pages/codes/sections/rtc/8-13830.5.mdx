## Text

The board shall hold hearings at such places as are deemed necessary, upon reasonable notice to the executors, ancillary administrators, all other interested persons, and to the taxing officials of the states involved, all of whom are entitled to be heard.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
