## Text

No injunction or writ of mandate or other legal or equitable process shall issue in any suit, action, or proceeding in any court against this State or against any officer of the State to prevent or enjoin the collection of any tax sought to be collected.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
