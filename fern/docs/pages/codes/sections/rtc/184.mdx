## Text

If the tax collector of an eligible county has certified to the Director of Finance the information specified in Section 182.5, the Director of Finance shall certify this amount to the Controller for allocation to the county. Upon receipt of certification by the Director of Finance, the Controller shall make the appropriate allocation to the county within 10 working days thereafter.
