## Text

For purposes of this part, the term “domestic building and loan association” has the same meaning given that term by Section 7701(a)(19) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1139, Sec. 12. Effective September 25, 1987. Applicable to income years beginning on or after January 1, 1987, by Sec. 241 of Ch. 1139.)
