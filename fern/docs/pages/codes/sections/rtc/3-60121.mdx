## Text

Before granting a license to an interstate user, the board may require the person to file with the board security pursuant to Section 60401. The license issued to any interstate user is not transferable and is valid until canceled or revoked.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 620, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 1998.)
