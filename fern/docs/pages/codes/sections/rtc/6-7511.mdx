## Text

It is unlawful for any person to operate in this state after a license has been revoked.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
