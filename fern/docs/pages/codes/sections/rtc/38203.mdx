## Text

On or before December 31, 1978, and on or before December 31 of each year thereafter, the board shall certify to the Director of Finance and to the Legislature the rate determined pursuant to Section 38202, and such rate shall be the yield tax rate applied under Section 38115 for the 12-month period beginning on the next succeeding January 1.
