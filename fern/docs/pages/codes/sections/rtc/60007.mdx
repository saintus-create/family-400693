## Text

“Removal” means any physical transfer of diesel fuel, and any use of diesel fuel other than as a material in the production of diesel fuel. However, diesel fuel is not removed when it evaporates or is otherwise lost or destroyed.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
