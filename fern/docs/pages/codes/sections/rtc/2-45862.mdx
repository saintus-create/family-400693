## Text

The board shall develop and implement a program that will evaluate an individual employee’s or officer’s performance with respect to his or her contact with fee payers. The development and implementation of the program shall be coordinated with the Taxpayers’ Rights Advocate.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 589, Sec. 170. Effective January 1, 1994.)
