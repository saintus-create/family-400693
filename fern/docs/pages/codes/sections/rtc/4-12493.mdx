## Text

(a) Every lien has the effect of an execution duly levied against all property of a delinquent insurer or surplus line broker.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2013.

## History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 84) and added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 33, Sec. 85. (SB 78) Effective June 27, 2013. Section operative July 1, 2013, by its own provisions.)
