## Text

The term “withholding agent” means any person required to deduct and withhold any tax under Section 18662.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 2. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
