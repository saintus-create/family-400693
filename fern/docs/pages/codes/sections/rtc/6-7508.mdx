## Text

The board may cancel any license issued under this part immediately upon surrender thereof but before revoking a license the board shall allow the person an opportunity to show cause as provided in Section 7507.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
