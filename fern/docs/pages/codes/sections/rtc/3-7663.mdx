## Text

Promptly after making its determination the board shall give to the delinquent supplier written notice of the estimate, tax, and penalty, the notice shall be given in the manner prescribed in Section 7671 for giving notice of a deficiency determination.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
