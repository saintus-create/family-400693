## Text

The Department of Finance shall establish guidelines in carrying out this chapter. These guidelines shall include a procedure for the review of claims submitted by an eligible county to the Department of Finance.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1507, Sec. 14.)
