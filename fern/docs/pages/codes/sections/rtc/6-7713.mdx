## Text

All determinations made by the board under this chapter are due and payable at the time they become final. If they are not paid when due and payable, a penalty of 10 percent of the amount of the determination, exclusive of interest and penalties, shall be added thereto. Payments shall be made in the form of a remittance payable to the Controller and shall be filed with the board together with a copy of the notice of determination which the board shall furnish to the supplier for that purpose.
