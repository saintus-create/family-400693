## Text

Taxable mortgage pools shall be defined and treated in accordance with Section 7701(i) of the Internal Revenue Code, except as otherwise provided.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1139, Sec. 13. Effective September 25, 1987. Applicable to income years beginning on or after January 1, 1987, by Sec. 241 of Ch. 1139.)
