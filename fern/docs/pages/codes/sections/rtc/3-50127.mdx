## Text

The fees, commissions, and expenses are the obligation of the person required to pay any amount under this part and may be collected from the person by the warrant or in any other manner provided in this part for the collection of the fee.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1442, Sec. 6. Effective October 2, 1989.)
