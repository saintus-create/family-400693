## Text

The notice shall be recorded with the county recorder. After recordation, the notice shall be forwarded to the tax collector. The recorder shall make no charge for the recording.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 497, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1999.)
