## Text

Allocations for specific projects on the state highway system only shall be deemed expenditures within the county in which the project is situated for the purpose of compliance by the department and the commission with Sections 188 and 188.8.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 777, Sec. 104.)
