## Text

Route 580 is from:

(a) Route 5 southwest of Vernalis to Route 80 in Oakland via the vicinity of Dublin and Hayward.

(b) Route 80 near Albany to Route 101 near San Rafael via the Richmond-San Rafael Bridge.
