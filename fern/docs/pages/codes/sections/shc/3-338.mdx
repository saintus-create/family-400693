## Text

Route 38 is from:

(a) Route 10 near Redlands to Route 18 near Baldwin Lake via Barton Flats.

(b) Route 18 near Baldwin Lake along the north side of Big Bear Lake to Route 18 near the west end of Big Bear Lake.
