## Text

For purposes of Section 164.3, the eligible interregional and intercounty routes include the following:

Route 203.

Route 205.

Route 207.

Route 215.

Route 239.

Route 243.

Route 267.

Route 299, between Route 101 and Route 89, and between Route 139 and Route 395.
