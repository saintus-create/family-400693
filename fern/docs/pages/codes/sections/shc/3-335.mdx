## Text

Route 35 is from:

(a) Route 17 at Summit Road to Route 92 via Skyline Boulevard.

(b) Route 92 to Route 280 at Bunker Hill Drive.

(c) Route 280 via Skyline Boulevard to Route 1 in San Francisco.
