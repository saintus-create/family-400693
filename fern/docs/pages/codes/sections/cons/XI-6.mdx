## Text

(a) A county and all cities within it may consolidate as a charter city and county as provided by statute.

(b) A charter city and county is a charter city and a charter county. Its charter city powers supersede conflicting charter county powers.

## History

(History: Sec. 6 added June 2, 1970, by Prop. 2. Res.Ch. 331, 1969.)
