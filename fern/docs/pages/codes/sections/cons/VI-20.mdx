## Text

The Legislature shall provide for retirement, with reasonable allowance, of judges of courts of record for age or disability.

## History

(History: Sec. 20 added Nov. 8, 1966, by Prop. 1-a. Res.Ch. 139, 1966 1st Ex. Sess.)
