## Text

This article shall become operative for injuries occurring on and after January 1, 1991.

## History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 892) by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1550, Sec. 28. Note: This section provides for delayed operation of Article 2, commencing with Section 4060.)
