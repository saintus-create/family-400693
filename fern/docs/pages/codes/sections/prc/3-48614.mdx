## Text

“Industrial generator” means an entity which buys and uses lubricating oil only for equipment owned or used by the entity. “Industrial generator” includes state or local governmental entities, as defined by Section 5902 of the Government Code. “Industrial generator” does not include motor carriers which have received oil for which a payment has not been made pursuant to Section 48650.
