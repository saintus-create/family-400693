## Text

“Agricultural activity” has the same meaning as “agricultural activity, operation, or facility, or appurtenances thereof” as defined in subdivision (e) of Section 3482.5 of the Civil Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 822, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
