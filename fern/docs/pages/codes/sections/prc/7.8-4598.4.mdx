## Text

Agreements may be entered into and grants may be made by the director pursuant to this article for the purpose of preparing PTEIRs for projects that demonstrate potential to increase carbon sequestration, decrease atmospheric carbon levels, and reduce the potential for large wildland fires.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 36, Sec. 11. (SB 862) Effective June 20, 2014.)
