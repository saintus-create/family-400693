## Text

The board, in consultation with the Department of General Services, shall perform a study to determine if the retreads, procured by the Department of General Services, have met all quality and performance criteria of a new tire.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1038, Sec. 21. Effective September 29, 1996.)
