## Text

When State lands, upon which the full purchase price has not been paid, have been sold to the State for delinquent taxes and the deed therefor to the State has been forwarded to and filed with the commission, such lands shall again become subject to entry and sale in the same manner, and subject to the same conditions, as apply to State lands of like character.
