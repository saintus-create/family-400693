## Text

A copy of the supervisor’s order shall be posted in a conspicuous place upon the property affected, and the order shall become final 10 days after posting, unless it is appealed from as provided in Section 3350.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 741, Sec. 15.)
