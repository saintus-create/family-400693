## Text

If the jurisdiction of the authority to order a proposed act is not affected, an omission of any officer or the authority in the proceedings under this division or any other defect in the proceedings shall not invalidate the proceedings for issuance of bonds pursuant to this division.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 908, Sec. 1.)
