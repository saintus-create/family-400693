## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, districts are subject to the Uniform District Election Law, Part 4 (commencing with Section 10500) of Division 10 of the Elections Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
