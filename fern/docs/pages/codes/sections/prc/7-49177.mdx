## Text

Bonds and interest thereon shall be paid by revenue derived from an annual tax upon the property in the district, and all the property in the district shall be and remain liable to be taxed for those payments.

The bonds and the interest thereon shall not be taxable in this state.

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
