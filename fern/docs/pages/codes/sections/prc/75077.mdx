## Text

Funds provided pursuant to this division, and any appropriation or transfer of those funds, shall not be deemed to be a transfer of funds for the purposes of Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 2780) of Division 3 of the Fish and Game Code.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 729, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008. Note: This section was added on Nov. 7, 2006, by initiative Prop. 84. Pursuant to Stats. 2008, Ch. 729, Sec. 5, the provisions of Division 43 added by initiative Prop. 84 prevail over any conflicting provision of Ch. 729 (including this amendment).)
