## Text

The local protection program, if it is otherwise consistent with the requirements of this division, may be submitted to the commission if both of the following requirements are met:

(a) It is submitted pursuant to a resolution adopted after at least one public hearing by each local government and district having jurisdiction in the marsh and the Solano County Local Agency Formation Commission stating the local protection program is intended to be carried out in a manner fully in conformity with this division.

(b) It contains materials sufficient for a thorough and complete review.
