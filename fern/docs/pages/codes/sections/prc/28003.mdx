## Text

Morro Bay and San Diego Bay are each hereby designated a State Estuary. Morro Bay and its watershed are hereby designated a State Estuary planning area.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 52, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
