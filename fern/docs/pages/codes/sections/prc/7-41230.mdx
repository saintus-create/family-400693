## Text

The city funding component shall identify and specifically describe projected costs, revenues, and revenue sources the city will use to implement all components of the city source reduction and recycling element.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 145, Sec. 23. Effective June 19, 1990.)
