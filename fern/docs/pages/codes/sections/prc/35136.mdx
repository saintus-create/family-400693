## Text

(a) Notice of the time and place of the public hearing for the adoption of the annual budget shall be published pursuant to Section 6061 of the Government Code, and shall be published not later than 30 days prior to the date of the hearing.

(b) The proposed annual budget shall be available for public inspection at least 30 days prior to the hearing.
