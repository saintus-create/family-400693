## Text

Any solid waste facility, located outside of any city, shall be maintained in compliance with the flammable clearance provisions of Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 4371) of Part 2 of Division 4.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1293, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1993.)
