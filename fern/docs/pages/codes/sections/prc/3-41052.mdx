## Text

The city source reduction component shall describe the methods the city will use to determine the categories of solid wastes to be diverted from disposal at a disposal facility through source reduction.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1292, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1993.)
