## Text

The Legislature recognizes that it is the intended purpose of the Land and Water Conservation Fund Act of 1965, as expressed in such act, that such federal financial assistance as may be provided to the state pursuant to the act shall be in addition to, rather than as a substitute for, state and local outdoor recreation funds.
