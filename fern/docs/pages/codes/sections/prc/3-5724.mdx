## Text

Any grant made pursuant to this chapter, and the performance of the applicant in expending the grant, may be audited at any time by the department.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 5, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 43 at the June 3, 1986, election. Operative July 1, 1986, by Stats. 1986, Ch. 5, Sec. 2.)
