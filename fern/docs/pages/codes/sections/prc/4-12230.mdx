## Text

The department shall carry out the California Forest Legacy Program. Nothing in this division alters the department’s responsibility for the administration of state, federal, or private funds that are allocated for the purpose of protecting private forest lands and all associated natural resources.

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 657, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
