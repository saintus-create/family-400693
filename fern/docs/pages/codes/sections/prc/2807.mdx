## Text

(a) There is hereby established a project for the implementation of a statewide program of earthquake safety education and preparedness entitled the California Earthquake Education Project (CALEEP). The Seismic Safety Commission may contract with the University of California to carry out the project. The project shall focus on identifying state and local leadership interested in using the CALEEP materials, disseminating those materials, and utilizing the materials.

(b) The project shall develop a relationship with the State Department of Education, interested teacher’s education computer centers statewide, and interested counties and school districts.
