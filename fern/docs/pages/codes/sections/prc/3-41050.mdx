## Text

The city source reduction component shall include a program and implementation schedule which shows the methods by which the city will, in combination with the recycling and composting components, reduce a sufficient amount of solid waste disposed of by the city to comply with the diversion requirements of Section 41780.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1292, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1993.)
