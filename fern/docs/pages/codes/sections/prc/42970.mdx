## Text

The purpose of this chapter is to increase the amount of postconsumer carpet that is diverted from landfills and recycled into secondary products or otherwise managed in a manner that is consistent with the state’s hierarchy for waste management practices pursuant to Section 40051.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 681, Sec. 2. (AB 2398) Effective January 1, 2011. Conditionally inoperative pursuant to Section 42983.5.)
