## Text

The office shall incur costs to implement this article only to the extent that funding adequate to cover those costs has been deposited in, and appropriated from, the California Main Street Program Fund.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 230, Sec. 13. Effective August 16, 2004.)
