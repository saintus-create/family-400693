## Text

Except as provided in Section 5098.1, the management and disbursement of moneys in the fund received pursuant to the “Land and Water Conservation Fund Act of 1965” (Public Law 88-578; 78 Stat. 897) are governed by the provisions of Chapter 1.9 (commencing with Section 5099) of this division.
