## Text

This article does not apply to a landowner who is a natural person and who personally performs services of a professional forester, when those services are personally performed on lands owned by that person.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 133, Sec. 41. (SB 272) Effective July 23, 2021.)
