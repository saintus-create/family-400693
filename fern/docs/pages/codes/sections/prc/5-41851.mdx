## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall infringe on the existing authority of counties and cities to control land use or to make land use decisions, and nothing in this chapter provides or transfers new authority over that land use to the board.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
