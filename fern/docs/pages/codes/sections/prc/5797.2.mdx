## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the disposition of property at Glen Helen Regional Park and the acquisition of replacement park property of equal or greater recreational value authorized pursuant to this chapter is for the purpose of increasing recreational opportunities in the county.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 259, Sec. 1. (AB 385) Effective January 1, 2026. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 5797.8.)
