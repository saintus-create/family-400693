## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative on September 30, 2026, and, as of January 1, 2027, is repealed.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 351, Sec. 2. (AB 2440) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Ch. 8.4, commencing with Section 42451.)
