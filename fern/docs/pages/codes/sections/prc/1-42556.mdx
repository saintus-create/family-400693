## Text

If the board determines that the policy goals established by Section 42554 are not being met by January 1, 1999, the board shall make recommendations to the Legislature, on or before January 1, 2000, on strategies for meeting the goals established in Section 42554.

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1066, Sec. 2.)
