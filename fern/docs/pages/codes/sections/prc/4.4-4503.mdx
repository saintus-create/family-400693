## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2028, and as of that date is repealed, unless a later enacted statute that is enacted before January 1, 2028, deletes or extends that date.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 606, Sec. 5. (SB 926) Effective September 27, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 4.4, commencing with Section 4500.)
