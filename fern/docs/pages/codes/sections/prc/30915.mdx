## Text

The purpose of this chapter is to provide authorization for projects that restore and protect the water quality and environment of coastal waters, estuaries, bays, and near shore waters.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 727, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
