## Text

(a) No such lease or agreement shall be effective unless prior to adopting the resolution provided for by Section 7058.5 the city shall have petitioned the State Lands Commission for approval of the proposed resolution, and the proposed resolution shall have been approved by the State Lands Commission.

(b) No city shall consent to the modification or amendment of any such lease or agreement without the advance consent of the State Lands Commission to such modification or amendment.
