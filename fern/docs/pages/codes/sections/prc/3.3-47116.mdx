## Text

(a) The manufacturer shall post and maintain a copy of the plans required pursuant to Section 47115 on its Internet Web site.

(b) The board, or its successor agency, shall post and maintain copies of the plans submitted by the manufacturers pursuant to Section 47115 on its Internet Web site.
