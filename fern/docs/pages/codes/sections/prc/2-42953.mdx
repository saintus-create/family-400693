## Text

Any person who gives, contracts, or arranges with another person to transport waste or used tires shall utilize only a person holding a valid waste and used tire hauler registration from the department, unless the hauler is exempt as specified in Section 42954.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 542, Sec. 3. (SB 1181) Effective January 1, 2023.)
