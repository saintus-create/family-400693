## Text

(a) On or before July 1, 2025, the department shall provide to the Legislature, and post on its internet website, a report as it relates to appropriations made pursuant to the Budget Act of 2022 for purposes of the California Beverage Container Recycling and Litter Reduction Act, including, but not limited to all of the following information:

(1) Whether and how recycling opportunities and rates in underserved areas improved.

(2) A list of funding recipients.

(3) Locations, including counties, in which funding was provided.

(b) The report shall be submitted pursuant to Section 9795 of the Government Code.
