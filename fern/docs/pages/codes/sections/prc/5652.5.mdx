## Text

A grant recipient shall encumber grant moneys within three years of the date of the approval of the grant and grant moneys shall be liquidated within eight years from the date of appropriation.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 623, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2009.)
