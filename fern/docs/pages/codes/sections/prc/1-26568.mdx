## Text

The procedures for initiation of proceedings, notice, and hearing and formation of a district under this chapter shall be alternative to the procedures in Articles 3 (commencing with Section 26550) and 4 (commencing with Section 26561) of Chapter 2. Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 26570) does not apply to districts formed under this chapter.
