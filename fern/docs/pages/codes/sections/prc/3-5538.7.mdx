## Text

If a district is created and established in Riverside County, officers and employees of the County of Riverside may act, ex officio, as officers and employees of the district and may discharge the authority and responsibility specified in this article.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 789, Sec. 20.)
