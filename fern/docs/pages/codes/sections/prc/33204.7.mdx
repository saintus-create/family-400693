## Text

On January 1, 1984, the Department of Parks and Recreation shall transfer Fryman Canyon, which was acquired pursuant to Section 4 of Chapter 809 of the Statutes of 1980, to the conservancy. Fryman Canyon shall be deemed to have been acquired by the conservancy pursuant to Section 33204, but may be transferred by the conservancy only to the National Park Service, without payment of any compensation, in accordance with Section 33205.
