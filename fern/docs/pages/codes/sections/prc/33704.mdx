## Text

The Legislature hereby acknowledges the role of the former Coachella Valley Mountains Conservancy, a California nonprofit corporation, and its successor, the Coachella Valley Mountains Trust, a California nonprofit corporation, in providing the leadership within the community that led to the creation of the conservancy pursuant to this division. Accordingly, the conservancy created by this division shall consult, and coordinate its program, with the trust, and any successor entity, for as long as it may exist.
