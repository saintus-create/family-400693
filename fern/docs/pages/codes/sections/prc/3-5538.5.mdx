## Text

If the Board of Supervisors of the County of Marin acts as the governing body of a district pursuant to Section 5506.5, the respective officers of that county shall act, ex officio, as officers and employees of the district and discharge the authority and responsibility specified in this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 872, Sec. 131. Effective January 1, 1997.)
