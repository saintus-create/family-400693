## Text

(a) A carpet stewardship organization shall include nonvoting board members with representation from the following types of entities:

(1) A circular economy environmental nongovernmental organization.

(2) A retailer that sells covered products.

(3) A labor representative from a floor covering joint apprenticeship program in northern California that is approved by the Division of Apprenticeship Standards.

(4) A labor representative from a floor covering joint apprenticeship program in southern California that is approved by the Division of Apprenticeship Standards.

(b) A carpet stewardship organization shall pay the travel costs and other expenses for the nonvoting members identified in subdivision (a) to participate in all board meetings.
