## Text

On or before June 1, 2022, all of the duties and responsibilities for the local assistance grant program established pursuant to this article shall be transferred to the Office of the State Fire Marshal.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 225, Sec. 12. (AB 9) Effective January 1, 2022.)
