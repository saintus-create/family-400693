## Text

Every project submitted for funding pursuant to Section 33208 shall have an adopted project plan developed by, or in coordination with, the applicable local government or other state agency. The conservancy shall by regulation specify the content of project plans. Maximum public participation shall be afforded in the development of project plans, including public hearings and findings.
