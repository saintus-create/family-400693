## Text

Any administrative civil penalty imposed by the board pursuant to Section 41813 or 48150 shall be deposited in the Local Government Assistance Account, which is hereby created in the Integrated Waste Management Fund. Any funds deposited in that account shall be used solely for the purposes of assisting local governments in complying with the diversion requirements established under Section 41780, and shall not be used by the board for administrative purposes.
