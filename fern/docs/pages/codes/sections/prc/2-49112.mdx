## Text

The notice shall state the fact that the board of supervisors has fixed the time and place, which shall be stated in the notice, for a hearing on the matter of the formation of a garbage and refuse disposal district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
