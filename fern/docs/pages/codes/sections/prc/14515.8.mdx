## Text

(a) “Pilot project recycler” means a recycling location established under Section 14571.9.

(b) This section shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2034, and as of that date is repealed.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 182, Sec. 1. (SB 1113) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2034, by its own provisions.)
