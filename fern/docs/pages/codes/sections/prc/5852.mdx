## Text

“Delta” means the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta, as defined in Section 12220 of the Water Code minus the area contained in Alameda County.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 839, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007.)
