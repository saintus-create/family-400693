## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall be inoperative as to a legislative body unless and until the legislative body adopts a resolution declaring that it is subject to its provisions and has forwarded a copy of such resolution to the State Controller.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
