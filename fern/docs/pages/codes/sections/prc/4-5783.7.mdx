## Text

In the case of a district that includes only incorporated territory within a single city, the board of directors may be elected or appointed by the city council which may appoint itself as the board of directors.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
