## Text

Except as otherwise determined by the board, only landfills authorized in this article shall be eligible for financial assistance in the shredding of tires pursuant to Article 3 (commencing with Section 42870).

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 25. Effective March 30, 1990.)
