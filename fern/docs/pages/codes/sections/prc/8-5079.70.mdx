## Text

There is hereby created within the office the California Main Street Program to provide technical assistance and training for small cities’ government, business organizations, merchants, and property owners to accomplish community and economic revitalization and development of older central and historic business districts and neighborhoods.

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 230, Sec. 13. Effective August 16, 2004.)
