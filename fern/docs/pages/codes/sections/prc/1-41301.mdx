## Text

The county source reduction and recycling element shall set forth a program for management of solid waste generated with the unincorporated area of the county, consistent with the waste management hierarchy provided in Section 40051.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
