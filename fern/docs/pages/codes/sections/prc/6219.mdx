## Text

The commission may, if it determines it is in the best interests of the state, accept on behalf of the state any gift, devise, grant, quitclaim, or other conveyance of title to, or interest in, real property. Such land, when acquired, shall be examined for significant environmental values as defined pursuant to Section 6370.1, and classified and administered according to the rules and regulations of the commission.
