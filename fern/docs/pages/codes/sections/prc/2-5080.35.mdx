## Text

Whenever the department intends to enter into an operating agreement pursuant to this article with respect to a unit of the state park system, the department shall notify each Member of the Legislature in whose district that unit is located of its intention.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1487, Sec. 7. Effective September 28, 1982.)
