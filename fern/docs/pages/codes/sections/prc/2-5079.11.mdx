## Text

Unless otherwise provided by law, all funds received by the office for the purposes of historical resource preservation shall be deposited in the fund. The office and the Controller may establish separate accounts in the fund for the purpose of separating deposits according to their origin or intended purpose.

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 749, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
