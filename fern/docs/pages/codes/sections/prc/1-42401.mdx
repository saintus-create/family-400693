## Text

“Retreaded tire” means any tire that utilizes an existing casing for the purpose of vulcanizing new tread to such casing which meets all performance and quality standards specified in the Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards determined by the United States Department of Transportation.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2.)
