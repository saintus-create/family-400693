## Text

If before January 1, 2033, a lead agency fails to approve an infrastructure project, then the certification is no longer valid.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 60, Sec. 6. (SB 149) Effective July 10, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2034, pursuant to Section 21189.91.)
