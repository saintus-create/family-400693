## Text

The commission shall transfer moneys from the Distributed Electricity Backup Assets Account to the state board to be used in the Climate Heat Impact Response Program established pursuant to the proclamation of a state of emergency issued by Governor Gavin Newsom on July 31, 2021.

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 61, Sec. 6. (AB 205) Effective June 30, 2022.)
