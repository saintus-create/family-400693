## Text

The county funding component shall identify and specifically describe projected costs, revenues, and revenue sources the county will use to implement all components of the county source reduction and recycling element.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 145, Sec. 39. Effective June 19, 1990.)
