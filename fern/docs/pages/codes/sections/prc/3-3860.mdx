## Text

The director may award grants to eligible jurisdictions for purposes of planning, equipment purchases, installation, and other measures related to the mitigation of methane gas hazards. Ongoing maintenance and monitoring activities shall not be financed by grants pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1322, Sec. 3.)
