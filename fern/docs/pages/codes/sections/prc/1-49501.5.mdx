## Text

“License” means a solid waste license issued by a local agency or a business license issued by a local agency if the local agency has not established any other form of authorization for the lawful provision of solid waste handling services.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 811, Sec. 3. Effective September 24, 1998.)
