## Text

It is not the intent of the Legislature to require cities and counties to revise their source reduction and recycling elements to comply with the requirements of this chapter.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 476, Sec. 8. (AB 341) Effective January 1, 2012.)
