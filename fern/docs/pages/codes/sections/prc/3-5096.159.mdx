## Text

Funds appropriated for local assistance grants pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 5096.151 shall be encumbered by the recipient within three years of the date the appropriation became effective, regardless of the date when each project was approved pursuant to subdivision (d) of Section 5096.157.

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 133, Sec. 1. Effective June 28, 1983.)
