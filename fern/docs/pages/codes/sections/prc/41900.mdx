## Text

Each city and county shall demonstrate a funding source, or sources, available to pay for preparing, adopting, and implementing the element or plan, as required by this part.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 145, Sec. 49. Effective June 19, 1990.)
