## Text

Any fees or charges imposed pursuant to this part by any enforcement agency shall bear a direct relationship to the reasonable and necessary cost, as determined by the enforcement agency, of providing the efficient operation of the activities or programs for which the fee is assessed.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 952, Sec. 12. Effective October 16, 1995.)
