## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Electric Program Investment Charge” means the surcharge instituted by the Public Utilities Commission pursuant to Decision 11-12-035 or any subsequent decisions to collect funds for renewable energy programs and research, development, and demonstration programs.

(b) “Fund” means the portion of the Electric Program Investment Charge Fund created by Section 25711.
