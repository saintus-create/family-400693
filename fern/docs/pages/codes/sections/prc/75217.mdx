## Text

The program administrators shall jointly report the progress on the implementation of the program, including each allocation, in an annual report required pursuant to subdivision (e) of Section 75125. The report shall include an assessment of the system-level impacts of program investments, including how those investments enable or accelerate climate-friendly housing, transportation, and land use outcomes, and the direct, indirect, and cumulative effects of those investments in supporting greenhouse gas emissions reductions.
