## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, upon receipt of a resolution of the Board of Education of the Las Virgenes Unified School District, the conservancy shall exchange for the district, under terms mutually agreed upon by the district and the conservancy, an acceptable substitute property for excess school lands within the zone now held by the district.
