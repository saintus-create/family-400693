## Text

The division shall identify all wells converted to or being operated as gravity-based energy storage wells on its internet website.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 419, Sec. 1. (SB 567) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2035, pursuant to Section 3190.9.)
