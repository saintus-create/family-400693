## Text

Upon presentation to the legislative body of a petition in the form prescribed by Sections 26551 and 26552, the legislative body shall adopt a resolution setting a public hearing on such petition and directing notice thereof to be mailed to all owners of real property to be included within the proposed district as shown on the assessment roll last equalized by the county.
