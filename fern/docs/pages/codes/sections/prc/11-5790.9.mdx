## Text

Any general obligation bonds issued by a district shall have the same force, value, and use as bonds issued by a city and the bonds and interest on the bonds are exempt from all taxation within the State of California.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
