## Text

Nothing in this division abrogates, limits, or otherwise affects the duties of the Department of Conservation under the California Beverage Container Recycling and Litter Reduction Act, Division 12.1 (commencing with Section 14500).

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
