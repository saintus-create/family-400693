## Text

A board of directors may require that the election of members to the board of directors shall be held on the same day as the statewide general election pursuant to Section 10404 of the Elections Code.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
