## Text

“Industrial oil” includes, but is not limited to, any compressor, turbine, or bearing oil, hydraulic oil, metal-working oil, or refrigeration oil. Industrial oil does not include dielectric fluids.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 817, Sec. 5.)
