## History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456. Note: The provisions of Section 1110, as added November 8, 1938, by initiative Proposition 5, were relocated to Section 7891 in the 1957 code reenactment. This Section 1110 serves as a cross-reference, and it is not part of Article 2, commencing with Section 1050.)
