## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed, unless a later enacted statute that is enacted before January 1, 2029, deletes or extends that date.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 985, Sec. 4. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2019. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 3.5, commencing with Section 7860.)
