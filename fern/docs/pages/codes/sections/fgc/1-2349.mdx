## Text

No bird, mammal, fish, reptile, or amphibian, except smoked, cured, or dried fish other than trout, may be shipped by parcel post.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 44. Effective January 1, 2008.)
