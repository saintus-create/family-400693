## Text

“Fish” means a wild fish, mollusk, crustacean, invertebrate, amphibian, or part, spawn, or ovum of any of those animals.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 5. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
