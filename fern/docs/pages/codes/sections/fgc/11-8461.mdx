## Text

The annual license fee for a live freshwater bait fish license is fifty-five dollars ($55) for each person.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 701, Sec. 33. Effective September 15, 1992. Operative January 1, 1993, by Sec. 72 of Ch. 701.)
