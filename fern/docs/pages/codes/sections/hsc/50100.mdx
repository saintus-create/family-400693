## Text

“Rules”, “regulations”, or “rules and regulations” mean regulations as defined in Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code. All regulations of the department and agency shall be subject to such provisions.
