## Text

Except for hot oil cooking or filtering equipment, “V” type threads shall not be used on food-contact surfaces.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
