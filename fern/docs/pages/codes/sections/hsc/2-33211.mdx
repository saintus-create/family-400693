## Text

If one agency is designated, it shall obtain the report and recommendation of the planning commission of each community on the redevelopment plan and its conformity to the general plan of each community before presenting the redevelopment plan to the respective legislative bodies for adoption.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 588.)
