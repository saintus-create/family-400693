## Text

Upon any subsequent conviction under the provisions of Section 120280, the court may order the person confined for a period not exceeding one year for the subsequent conviction, or other penalty as provided by that section.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
