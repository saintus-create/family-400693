## Text

The department may, under any program established pursuant to this article, investigate the feasibility of contracting with one or more banks in the area served by the program for the redemption of nutrition coupons.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
