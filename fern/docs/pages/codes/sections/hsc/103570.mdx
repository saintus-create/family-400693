## Text

A certification limited to a statement as to the date of birth of any child needed for admission to school or for the purpose of securing employment shall be issued without fee by the local registrar or county recorder upon request of any parents or guardian.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
