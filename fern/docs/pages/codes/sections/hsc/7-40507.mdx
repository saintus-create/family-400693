## Text

The south coast district board, in making any order granting a permit, may specify the time during which the order shall be effective and may require the payment of fees established by the south coast district board.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1166, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994.)
