## Text

For purposes of this article, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Account” means the High Polluter Repair or Removal Account created pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 44091.

(b) “High polluter” means a high-emission motor vehicle, including, but not limited to, a gross polluter.
