## Text

No person shall maintain or operate in or upon the navigable waters of any lake, reservoir, or fresh water impoundment of this state any vessel that is equipped with a toilet unless the toilet is sealed or otherwise rendered inoperable or designed so that no human excreta can be discharged into the waters.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
