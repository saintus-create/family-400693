## Text

Two or more agencies within two or more communities may jointly exercise the powers granted under this part. In such case the agencies, the planning commissions, and the legislative bodies may hold joint hearings and meetings, or the legislative bodies of the communities acting separately may each designate the agency of one of the communities to act as the agency for all of the interested communities.
