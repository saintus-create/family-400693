## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Department” means the State Department of Public Health.

(b) “Data collection pilot program” means the Childhood Drowning Data Collection Pilot Program established pursuant to this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 817, Sec. 2. (SB 855) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Sec. 131355.)
