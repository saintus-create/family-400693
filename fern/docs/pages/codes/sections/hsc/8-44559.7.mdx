## Text

The authority may enter into agreements with commercial banks or other financial institutions, or with other agencies of the state, to provide necessary assistance in carrying out the program authorized by this article, including origination and servicing of loans.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1164, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994.)
