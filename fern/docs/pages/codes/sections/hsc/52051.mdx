## Text

The provisions of Chapter 16 (commencing with Section 7260) of Division 7 of Title 1 of the Government Code shall not apply to a mortgagor of any home acquired by foreclosure, trust deed, sale or other proceeding resulting from default on a home mortgage made by a county or city or by a lending institution pursuant to this part.
