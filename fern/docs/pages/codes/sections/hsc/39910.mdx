## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that it is in the interests of the people of the State of California to ensure that the state board establish a unified, coordinated, and expedited process for districts to retrofit electrical generating facilities in a manner that protects public health and the environment and that complies fully with applicable federal and state statutes and regulations.
