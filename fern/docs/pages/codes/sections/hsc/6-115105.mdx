## Text

The department shall require each person who acquires, possesses or uses a source of ionizing radiation to maintain records relating to its receipt, storage, transfer or disposal, and other records as the department may require, subject to exemptions as may be provided by regulations.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
