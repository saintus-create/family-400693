## Text

Pursuant to the rules or criteria as the director may deem necessary, the Scientific Advisory Review Committee shall review and recommend approval of grant applications and monitor programs receiving grants under this article.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
