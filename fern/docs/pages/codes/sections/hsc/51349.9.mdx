## Text

On or before March 1, 2028, and each year thereafter, the agency shall submit a report to the Legislature pursuant to Section 9795 of the Government Code, describing program activity, including guarantees issued, geographic distribution, borrower demographics, claims paid, and recommendations for improvement.
