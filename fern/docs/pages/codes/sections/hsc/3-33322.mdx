## Text

The planning commission may select one or more project areas comprised of all or part of any survey area, on its own motion, or at the request of the agency. The planning commission shall select one or more project areas comprised of all or part of any survey area, at the direction of the legislative body, or upon the written petition of the owners in fee of the majority in area of a proposed project area, excluding publicly owned areas or areas dedicated to a public use.

The planning commission shall formulate a preliminary plan for the redevelopment of each selected project area.
