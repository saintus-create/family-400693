## Text

The committee is hereby authorized and empowered to create a debt or debts, liability or liabilities, of the State of California, in the aggregate of fifteen million dollars ($15,000,000), in the manner provided in this chapter. The debt or debts, liability or liabilities, shall be created for the purpose of providing the funds to be used for the purposes specified in Section 52505 and shall be deposited in the First-Time Home Buyers Fund created pursuant to Section 52504.
