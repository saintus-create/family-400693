## Text

All emissions reductions and credits achieved as a result of programs initiated under this chapter shall be used to fulfill local and regional commitments to air quality standards. Any additional reductions or credits that may exist after the local or regional commitment to air quality is fulfilled may be used to fulfill the state’s commitment to air quality standards and attainment.
