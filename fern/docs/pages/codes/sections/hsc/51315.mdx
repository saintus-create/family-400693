## Text

Subject only to the limitations prescribed in this chapter, the agency, in addition to any other power conferred by this part with respect to housing, may make or undertake commitments to make loans to finance the acquisition, construction, rehabilitation, refinancing, or development of special needs housing. For this purpose, the agency may enter into regulatory contracts and other agreements with the owners or operators of that housing to ensure compliance with this chapter.
