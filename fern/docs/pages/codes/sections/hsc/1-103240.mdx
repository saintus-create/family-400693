## Text

If the amendment relates to a certificate that has been transmitted to the State Registrar, the amendment shall be transmitted to the State Registrar who shall review it for acceptance for filing.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
