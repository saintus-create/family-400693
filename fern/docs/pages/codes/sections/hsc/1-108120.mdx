## Text

As used in this chapter, “federal act” means the Federal Hazardous Substances Act (74 Stats. 372; 15 U.S.C., Sec. 1261, et seq.).

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
