## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given by publishing a copy of the resolution pursuant to Section 6066 of the Government Code prior to the time fixed for the hearing in a newspaper having general circulation in the county sanitation district. Such notice shall also be given by posting a copy of the resolution in three public places within the proposed improvement district at least two weeks before the time fixed for the hearing.
