## Text

“Capitalized operating reserves” means capitalized funds for assisted units for the purpose of covering potential or projected operating deficits over time, including, but not limited to, operations, supportive services, and rent subsidies.

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 22, Sec. 44. (AB 130) Effective June 30, 2025.)
