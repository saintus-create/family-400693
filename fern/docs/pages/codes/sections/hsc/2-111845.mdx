## Text

The department is not required to institute proceedings under this part for minor violations of this part, if the department believes that the public interest will be adequately served in the circumstances by a suitable written notice or warning.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
