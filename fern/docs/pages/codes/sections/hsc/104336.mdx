## Text

The University of California may establish a spinal cord injury research fund, independent of the State Treasury, to accept public and private funds for the purpose of implementing this chapter.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 457, Sec. 1. (AB 1931) Effective January 1, 2011.)
