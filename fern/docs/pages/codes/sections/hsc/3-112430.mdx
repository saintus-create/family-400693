## Text

When requested by the department or an agent thereof, any licensee shall within a reasonable time submit a report setting forth in itemized particulars the quantity of food products held by him or her in cold storage.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
