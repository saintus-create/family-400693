## Text

Fees as applicable shall be submitted for permits:

(a) Fees for a permit to conduct any construction subject to this part as determined by the schedule of fees adopted by the department.

(b) Plan checking fees equal to one-half of the construction, plumbing, mechanical, and electrical permit fees, except that the minimum fee shall be ten dollars ($10).

(c) Except for a temporary recreational vehicle park, an annual operating permit fee of twenty-five dollars ($25) and an additional two dollars ($2) per lot or two dollars ($2) per camping party for the maximum number of camping parties to be accommodated at any one time in an incidental camping area.

(d) Temporary recreational vehicle park operating permit fee of twenty-five dollars ($25), with no additional fee for the lots.

(e) Change in name fee or transfer of ownership or possession fee of ten dollars ($10).

(f) Duplicate permit fee or amended permit fee of ten dollars ($10).
