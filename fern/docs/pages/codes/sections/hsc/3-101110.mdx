## Text

The board of supervisors in each county may employ one or more public health nurses, each of whom shall be a registered nurse possessing qualifications prescribed by the department on the date of employment.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
