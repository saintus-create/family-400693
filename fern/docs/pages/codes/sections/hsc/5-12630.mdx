## Text

The State Fire Marshal shall establish and collect the original and annual renewal fees for fireworks licenses required by this chapter. The fees shall not exceed the amount necessary to cover the costs incurred in the administration and enforcement of this part.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
