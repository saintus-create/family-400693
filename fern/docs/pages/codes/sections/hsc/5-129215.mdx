## Text

The Health Facility Construction Loan Insurance Fund, established pursuant to Section 129200, shall be a trust fund and neither the fund nor the interest or other earnings generated by the fund shall be used for any purpose other than those purposes authorized by this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
