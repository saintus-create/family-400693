## Text

Property seized pursuant to Section 11488 may, where appropriate, be held for evidence. The Attorney General or the district attorney for the jurisdiction involved shall institute and maintain the proceedings.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 314, Sec. 10. Effective August 19, 1994.)
