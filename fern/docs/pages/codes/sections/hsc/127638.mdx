## Text

In implementing the program, the department shall consult with interested parties, including the State Department of Health Care Services, the Department of Managed Health Care, the Department of Insurance, abortion and contraception providers, gender-affirming care providers, consumer advocates, and other stakeholders it deems appropriate.
