## Text

For the purposes of Sections 40918, 40919, 40920, and 40920.5, the designation of a district’s air pollution as “moderate,” “serious,” “severe,” or “extreme” for an area which is a receptor of transported air pollutants shall be based on violations of state ambient air quality standards which would occur without regard to the transport contribution.
