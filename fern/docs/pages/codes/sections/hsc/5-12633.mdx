## Text

The original and annual renewal application for registration of each model of emergency signaling devices shall be made to the State Fire Marshal. A registration fee established and collected by the State Fire Marshal for each model of signaling device shall accompany each application.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 233, Sec. 2.)
