## Text

The dentist or dental hygienist shall attend to dental conditions of the city as the governing body may assign. Compensation for the dentist or dental hygienist shall be determined by that body.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
