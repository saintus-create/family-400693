## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that it is in the interests of all Californians to protect the air quality from increasing volumes of cruise ship engine and oceangoing ship engine emissions.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 588, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2006.)
