## Text

The original and annual renewal license fee to manufacture, import, export, or wholesale, or any combination thereof, agricultural and wildlife fireworks shall be established and collected by the State Fire Marshal.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1313, Sec. 3.)
