## Text

The program shall discourage tampering and other forms of cheating, and effectively address “avoidance vehicles,” such as nonregistered vehicles and vehicles lacking a sufficient inspection and maintenance history.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 929, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
