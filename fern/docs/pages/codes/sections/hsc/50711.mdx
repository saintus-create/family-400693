## Text

The Director of Housing and Community Development shall have possession and control of all records and papers held previously by the Director of Employment Development relating to the purposes and activities of Section 7100 of the Government Code as it read prior to January 1, 1982.

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1165.)
