## Text

If and when a specific appropriation is made available, the State Department of Social Services shall develop new regulations regarding food preparation provisions for child day care facilities, community care facilities, and residential care facilities for the elderly that would carry out the intent of this part to ensure the health and safety of individuals and that would not adversely affect those facilities that are safely operated. In developing proposed food preparation provisions for child day care facilities, the State Department of Social Services shall consult with the department and the State Department of Education.
