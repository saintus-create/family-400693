## Text

It is a misdemeanor for a person to falsely represent or present himself or herself as a home care aide applicant or registered home care aide.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 790, Sec. 1. (AB 1217) Effective January 1, 2014. Provisions implemented as of January 1, 2016, pursuant to Section 1796.61.)
