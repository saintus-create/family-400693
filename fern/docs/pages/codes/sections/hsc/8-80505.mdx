## Text

The department shall not approve a loan pursuant to Article 7 (commencing with Section 80480) if the total debt against the eligible property subject to the release or threatened release of a hazardous material on which the response action will be taken exceeds 80 percent of the estimated value of the property after all necessary response actions are complete.
