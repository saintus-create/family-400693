## Text

A facility shall be licensed as a residential care facility for the elderly if it offers care and supervision, as defined, to its residents. Every residential care facility for the elderly in this state shall be licensed under this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1127, Sec. 3.)
