## Text

The Legislature hereby finds and declares that licensed physicians, experienced in the treatment of addiction, should be allowed and encouraged to treat addiction by all appropriate means.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 815, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
