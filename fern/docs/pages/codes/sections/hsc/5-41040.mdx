## Text

The Sacramento district may, pursuant to agreement with one or more local agencies within the district, establish the Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Coordinating Council to provide for coordinated air quality planning within the Sacramento district.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1541, Sec. 3.)
