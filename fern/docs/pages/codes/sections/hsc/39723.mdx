## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as resulting in any taxpayer paying a higher tax within the meaning of Section 3 of Article XIIIA of the California Constitution.

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 830, Sec. 6. (SB 535) Effective January 1, 2013.)
