## Text

“Mortgage” means a mortgage, deed of trust, or other instrument that may be a lien on real property. “Mortgage” includes the note secured by such an instrument.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 553, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
