## Text

Annexations may proceed under Article 2 (commencing with Section 2822), as though territory to be annexed were a new district, except that the pest or pests to be controlled shall be the same as those which the annexing district is authorized to control.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 911, Sec. 75. Effective September 7, 1984.)
