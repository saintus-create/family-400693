## Text

With the approval of the Department of Finance, and for use in the furtherance of the work of the authority, the director may accept all of the following:

(a) Grants of interest in real property.

(b) Gifts of money from public agencies or from organizations or associations organized for scientific, educational, or charitable purpose.
