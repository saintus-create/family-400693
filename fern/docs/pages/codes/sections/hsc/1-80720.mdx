## Text

“Pollution condition” means a release or threatened release of a hazardous material and any resulting impact upon the environment.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
