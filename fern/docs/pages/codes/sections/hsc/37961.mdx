## Text

If the jurisdiction of the legislative body to order the proposed act is not affected, an omission of any officer or the local agency in proceedings under this part or any other defect in the proceedings shall not invalidate the proceedings or bonds issued pursuant to this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1201.)
