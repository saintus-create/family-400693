## Text

The local enforcement agency may recover the reasonable regulatory costs of the administration, implementation, investigation, and enforcement of this chapter from a limited service charitable feeding operation.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 489, Sec. 3. (AB 2178) Effective January 1, 2019.)
