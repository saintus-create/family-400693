## Text

Low acid frozen food shall be packaged in a container of distinctive appearance so as to indicate to the purchaser that the package is not ordinary canned goods of a nonperishable nature.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
