## Text

The department shall adopt regulations to implement the amendments of this article in 1991. The adoption of the regulations shall be deemed to be an emergency, and necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, safety, and general welfare.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
