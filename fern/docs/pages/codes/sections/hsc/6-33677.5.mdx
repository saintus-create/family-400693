## Text

A county auditor shall only offset excess amounts of property tax revenues allocated to a redevelopment project against property tax revenues of that redevelopment project, and not against the property tax revenues of another redevelopment project governed by the same redevelopment agency.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 636, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
