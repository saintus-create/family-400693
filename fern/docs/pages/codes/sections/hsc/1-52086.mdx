## Text

Two or more cities in the same county, or a county and one or more cities within the county, or two or more counties, may enter into an agreement to join or cooperate with one another in the exercise jointly, or otherwise, of any or all of their powers for the purpose of financing multifamily rental housing development pursuant to this chapter.
