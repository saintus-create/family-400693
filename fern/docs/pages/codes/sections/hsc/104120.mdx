## Text

The department shall establish standards for applications for funding, review of proposals, funding awards, technical assistance, monitoring, and evaluation of local programs as it may deem necessary for the implementation of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
