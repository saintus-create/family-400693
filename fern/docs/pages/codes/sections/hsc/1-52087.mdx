## Text

The same notice requirements specified in Section 65863.10 of the Government Code shall apply to multifamily rental housing that receives financial assistance pursuant to this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Effective March 11, 2002.)
