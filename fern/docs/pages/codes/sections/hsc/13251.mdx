## Text

Every civil action brought under the provisions of this chapter at the request of the State Fire Marshal shall be brought by the district attorney or Attorney General in the name of the people of the State of California and any such actions relating to fire protection may be joined or consolidated.

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 345.)
