## Text

Notwithstanding Article 4 (commencing with Section 110423), the sale or distribution of any dietary supplement products containing ephedrine group alkaloids is prohibited.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 903, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
