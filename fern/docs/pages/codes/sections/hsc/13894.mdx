## Text

At the time and place specified for the meeting, any person may appear and be heard regarding any item in the budget or regarding the addition of other items. The hearing on the preliminary budget may be continued from time to time.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
