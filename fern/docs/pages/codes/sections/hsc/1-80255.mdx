## Text

“Project” means any response action, and the planned future development, included in an application for a loan pursuant to Article 6 (commencing with Section 80450).

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
