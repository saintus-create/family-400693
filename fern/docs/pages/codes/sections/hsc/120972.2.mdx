## Text

(a) The State Department of Public Health’s Office of AIDS may expend moneys from the AIDS Drug Assistance Program Rebate Fund to support prevention services for individuals most vulnerable to HIV, including, but not limited to, harm reduction services, internal and external condoms, or other preventative measures to limit individuals from contracting HIV.

(b) To the extent that funds are available for these purposes, the State Department of Public Health’s Office of AIDS may allocate funds to local health departments and community-based organizations to support HIV prevention.
