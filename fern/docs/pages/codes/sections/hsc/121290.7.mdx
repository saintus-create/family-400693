## Text

The Office of AIDS shall appoint an internal advisory committee composed of the office’s African-American HIV specialist, a section head from the office, and a designee to supervise the day-to-day activities of the initiative.

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 403, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
