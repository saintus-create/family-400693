## Text

The trustee acting on behalf of bondholders shall have and possess all the powers necessary or convenient for the exercise of any functions specifically set forth in this part or incident to the general representation of bondholders in the enforcement and protection of their rights.

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1602, Sec. 1.)
