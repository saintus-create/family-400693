## Text

Any person who restrains, hinders, obstructs, or threatens any officer or employee of a district in the performance of that person’s duties, or any person who interferes with any work done by, or under the direction of, the district is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 395, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003.)
