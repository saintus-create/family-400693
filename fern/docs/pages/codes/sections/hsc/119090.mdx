## Text

(a) Any person who violates Sections 119075 to 119085, inclusive, is guilty of a misdemeanor, and subject to a fine of not more than five hundred dollars ($500) or not more than six months’ imprisonment.

(b) For purposes of this section, “person” shall not include public agencies, officers or employees of public agencies, or public utilities.
