## Text

Notwithstanding any other law and except as provided in Section 1728.95, beginning on the effective date of the act that added this section and until no later than 90 days following the effective date of the revised regulations adopted pursuant to Section 1734, the department shall not issue a new license to operate a home health agency or add a branch office to an existing license pursuant to this chapter.
