## Text

The Legislative Analyst’s Office shall submit a report to the Legislature and the Governor no later than January 1, 2001, regarding the effectiveness of the programs established pursuant to this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 407, Sec. 26. Effective August 27, 1998. Operative November 4, 1998 by Sec. 31 of Ch. 407, pursuant to adoption of Prop. 1A. Inoperative January 1, 2002, as provided in Section 51455.)
