## Text

(a) Nothing in this division affects the authority of the Public Utilities Commission.

(b) Nothing in this division affects the obligation of an electrical corporation to provide customers with safe and reliable electric service.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 488, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2007.)
