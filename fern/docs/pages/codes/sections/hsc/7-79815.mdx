## Text

(a) Notwithstanding any other provision of this part, this article governs liability pursuant to this part for an owner of property.

(b) This article is applicable only to property that is acquired by the owner on or after January 1, 1995.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
