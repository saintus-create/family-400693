## Text

Floor slabs placed on earth shall be constructed of reinforced concrete designed by a licensed structural or civil engineer to include control joints at appropriate intervals to minimize cracks as well as appropriate vapor and moisture barriers as specified by a licensed geotechnical engineer.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 350, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1994.)
