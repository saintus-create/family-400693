## Text

At the next triennial building standards rulemaking cycle that commences on or after January 1, 2019, the Department of Housing and Community Development shall develop and submit for approval clarifications in the California Building Code and the California Residential Code pertaining to the requirements for the construction of live/work units.

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 573, Sec. 1. (AB 565) Effective January 1, 2019.)
