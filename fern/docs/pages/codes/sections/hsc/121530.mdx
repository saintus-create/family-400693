## Text

The examination shall consist of either an approved intradermal tuberculin test or any other test for tuberculosis infection that has been recommended by the CDC and licensed by the FDA, that, if positive, shall be followed by an X-ray of the lungs.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 179, Sec. 157. Effective January 1, 2009.)
