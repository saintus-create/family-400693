## Text

“Nonprofit charitable temporary food facilities” means either one of the following:

(a) A temporary food facility, as defined in Section 113930, that is conducted by a nonprofit charitable organization, as defined in Section 113841.

(b) An established club or organization of students that operates under the authorization of a school or other educational facility.
