## Text

The authority may charge reasonable application and project fees to reimburse the authority for costs incurred in administering applications for financing pursuant to this division and to support authority programs, including, but not limited to, the Capital Access Loan Program authorized by Article 8 (commencing with Section 44559), and grants and loans as authorized by subdivisions (h) and (g) of Section 44526.
