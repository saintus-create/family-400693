## Text

On or before January 1, 2004, the Trustees of the California State University shall submit a progress report to the Legislature on the status of the establishment and implementation of curricula in gerontology in accordance with this article.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 551, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
