## Text

The original and annual renewal license fee to manufacture, import, export, or wholesale, or any combination thereof, model rocket motors shall be established and collected by the State Fire Marshal.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 106, Sec. 5. (AB 467) Effective January 1, 2016.)
