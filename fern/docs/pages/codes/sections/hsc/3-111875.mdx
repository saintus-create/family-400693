## Text

When an authorized agent of the department finds, or has reasonable cause to believe, that the embargo will be violated, that agent may remove the embargoed food, drug, device, or cosmetic to a place of safekeeping.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
