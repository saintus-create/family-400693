## Text

The department, at the request of the county alcohol and drug program administrator, may assist local community organizations in initiating effective programs to prevent and treat alcohol and other drug use. The department may charge a fee for this assistance.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 17. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
