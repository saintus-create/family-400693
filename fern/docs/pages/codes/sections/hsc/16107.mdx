## Text

(a) The University of California, the California State University, the Department of Corrections, and public agencies are encouraged to develop and implement policies and performance standards according to lowest reasonable life cycle costs in a similar manner to Section 16101.

(b) No provision of this chapter shall apply to the Regents of the University of California unless the regents, by resolution, make the provision applicable.
