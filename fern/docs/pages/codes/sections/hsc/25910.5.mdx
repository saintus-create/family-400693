## Text

(a) The State Department of Health Services shall adopt regulations prohibiting or regulating the use of asbestos notwithstanding Section 25910 if the state department finds that such use is dangerous to the public health.

(b) The State Department of Health Services shall enforce the regulations adopted pursuant to this section, and may commence and maintain all proper actions to enjoin and abate violations of such regulations or to compel the performance of any act specifically required of any person, officer, or board by such regulations to protect and preserve the public health.
