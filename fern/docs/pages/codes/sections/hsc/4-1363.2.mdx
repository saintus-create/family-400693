## Text

On or before July 1, 1999, the disclosure form required pursuant to Section 1363 shall also contain a statement that enrollees are encouraged to use appropriately the “911” emergency response system, in areas where the system is established and operating, when they have an emergency medical condition that requires an emergency response.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 979, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
