## Text

The public health nurse shall attend to matters pertaining to the health and sanitary conditions of the county as the board of supervisors may assign. Compensation for the public health nurse shall be determined by that board.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
