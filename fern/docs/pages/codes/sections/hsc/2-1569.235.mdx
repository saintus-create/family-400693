## Text

As a requirement for licensure, the applicant shall attend an orientation given by the department which outlines the applicable rules and regulations, and the scope and responsibility for operation of a residential care facility for the elderly.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 848, Sec. 3.)
