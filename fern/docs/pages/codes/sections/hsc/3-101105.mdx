## Text

The public health nurse shall attend to matters pertaining to the health and sanitary conditions of the city as the governing body may assign. Compensation for the public health nurse shall be determined by that body.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
