## Text

(a) Nothing in this article shall affect the existing obligations of the parties or transferor to a real estate contract, or their agents, to disclose any facts materially affecting the value and desirability of the property, including, but not limited to, the physical conditions of the property and previously received reports of physical inspections noted on the disclosure form set forth in Section 1102.6 or 1102.6a of the Civil Code.

(b) Nothing in this article shall be construed to change the existing inspection and disclosure duties of a real estate broker or salesperson including, but not limited to, those duties imposed by Section 2079 of the Civil Code.
