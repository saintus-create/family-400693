## Text

Each enforcement agency shall follow procedures that are consistent with the Medical Waste Management Act and the regulations adopted pursuant to this chapter, when issuing medical waste permits.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 564, Sec. 66. (AB 333) Effective January 1, 2015.)
