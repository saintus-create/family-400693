## Text

(a) Commencing in 2002, and annually thereafter, the authority shall submit a report to the Legislature regarding the loan program described in subdivision (g) of Section 44526.

(b) This section shall not become operative if Assembly Bill 779 of the 1999–2000 Regular Session is enacted.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 915, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2001. This section is inoperative pursuant to failure of condition in subd. (b). See the fully operative Section 44525.7 (added by Sec. 7.5 of Ch. 915), as amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 643. Note: Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 325, was intended by the Legislature to repeal this inoperative version but incorrectly identified the version of this section amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 643, Sec. 11.)
