## Text

A room, area, or cabinet separated from any food preparation or storage area, or warewashing or storage area shall be provided for the storage of cleaning equipment and supplies.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
