## Text

It is unlawful for any person, with the intent to defraud, to falsely represent and provide for compensation a device, substance, method or treatment as effective to diagnose, arrest, prevent, or cure cancer. Nothing in this section shall abridge the existent rights of the press.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
