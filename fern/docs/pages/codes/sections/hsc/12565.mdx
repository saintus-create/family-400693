## Text

All fireworks or toy propellent devices containing pyrotechnic compositions examined by the State Fire Marshal and found by him or her to come within the definition of “model rocket” or “model rocket motor” in Section 12519 or 12520, respectively, shall be classified as model rocket motors.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 106, Sec. 3. (AB 467) Effective January 1, 2016.)
