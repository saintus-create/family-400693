## Text

The department and designated agencies shall maintain surveillance and supervision over the services provided handicapped children under authorization by the program to assure a high quality of service and shall cause a record to be kept showing the condition and improvement of these handicapped children.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
