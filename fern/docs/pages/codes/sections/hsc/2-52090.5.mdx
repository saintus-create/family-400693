## Text

The agency and any city or county may enter into an agreement which provides that the agency may sell bonds authorized pursuant to Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 52030) for the city or county and operate a program with the proceeds of the sale for the purpose of providing funds for construction loans and mortgage loans for multifamily rental housing developments within the city or county and for the provision of capital improvements in connection with and determined necessary to the multifamily rental housing.
