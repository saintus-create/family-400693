## Text

The State Department of Public Health shall apply the same financial eligibility requirements for the purposes of administering the HIV Care Program as those set forth for the ADAP in Section 120960.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 38, Sec. 24. (SB 78) Effective June 27, 2019. Section operative April 1, 2020, pursuant to Section 120974.)
