## Text

Any patient being seen or treated by a trainee shall be apprised of that fact and shall be given the opportunity to refuse treatment. Consent to the treatment shall not constitute assumption of the risk.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
