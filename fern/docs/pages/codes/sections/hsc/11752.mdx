## Text

As used in this division, “department” means the State Department of Health Care Services and “director” means the Director of Health Care Services.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 22, Sec. 27. (AB 75) Effective June 27, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 110 of Ch. 22.)
