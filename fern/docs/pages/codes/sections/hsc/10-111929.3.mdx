## Text

The department may enter into a memorandum of understanding or other interagency agreement with another state agency to administer and enforce provisions of this chapter as they relate to inhalable products, including, but not limited to, testing provisions, advertising and labeling provisions, and the provisions relating to the manufacture and sale of inhalable products.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 576, Sec. 10. (AB 45) Effective October 6, 2021. Conditionally operative as prescribed by Section 111929.4. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 111929.5.)
