## Text

“Health officer,” as used in the Communicable Disease Prevention and Control Act (Section 27) includes county, city, and district health officers, and city and district health boards, but does not include advisory health boards.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
