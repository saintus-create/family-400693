## Text

The requirements of the former California Expedited Remedial Action Reform Act of 1994 (former Chapter 6.85 (commencing with Section 25396) of Division 20) continue to apply to sites selected before the effective date of this chapter for participation in the pilot program established by that act.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 39, Sec. 78. (SB 1018) Effective June 27, 2012.)
