## Text

In addition to the purposes of the agency provided in Sections 50154 and 50950, a purpose and role of the agency is to make financing opportunities available to first-time home buyers in this state, in the manner provided in this part.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 320, Sec. 12. Effective June 29, 1982.)
