## Text

Except as provided in subdivision (b) of Section 11832.3, no person, firm, partnership, association, or local government entity shall establish, operate, manage, conduct, or maintain an alcohol or other drug program within this state without first obtaining a certification pursuant to this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 42, Sec. 32. (AB 118) Effective July 10, 2023.)
