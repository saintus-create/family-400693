## Text

Private mausoleums or columbariums may be constructed in conformance with the methods and standards set forth in this chapter or in conformance with the construction methods and standards as adopted by the Cemetery and Funeral Bureau.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 568, Sec. 260. Effective January 1, 2001.)
