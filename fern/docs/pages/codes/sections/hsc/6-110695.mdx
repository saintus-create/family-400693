## Text

Any food is misbranded if it is a confectionery and contains alcohol in excess of 12 of 1 percent by weight and that fact does not appear on the label for the food.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
