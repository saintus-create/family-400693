## Text

At the expiration of not less than 90 days after adoption of the resolution of intention to construct, maintain and operate a refuse transfer or disposal system, or both, the district board may, in its discretion, adopt a resolution declaring the system formed or may rescind its resolution of intention to form said system and declare all prior proceedings in connection therewith void.

Any existing refuse collection and disposal system of a district shall be dissolved without further action by the board upon the adoption by the same district board of a resolution declaring a refuse transfer or disposal system, or both, formed.
