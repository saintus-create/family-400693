## Text

“Mortgagor” means a person or persons who have received a home mortgage on a home and who are deemed by a city or county conducting a program under this part to be a person or family of low or moderate income and unable to pay the amounts at which unassisted private enterprise is providing suitable, decent, safe, and sanitary housing.
