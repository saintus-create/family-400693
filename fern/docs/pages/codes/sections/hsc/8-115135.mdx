## Text

The department and any other appropriate state agency may institute training programs for the purpose of qualifying personnel to carry out this chapter, and may make those personnel available for participation in any program or programs of the federal government, other states, or interstate agencies in furtherance of the purposes of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
