## Text

The Department of Justice may charge a fee sufficient to cover its cost in providing services in accordance with Section 1569.17 to comply with the 14-day requirement for provision to the department of the criminal record information, as contained in subdivision (c) of Section 1569.17.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 311, Sec. 38. Effective August 19, 1998.)
