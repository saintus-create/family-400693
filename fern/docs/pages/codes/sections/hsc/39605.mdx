## Text

To carry out the purposes of this division, the state board may:

(a) Provide any assistance to any district.

(b) Require any district to provide requested information utilized in the normal operation of the district or required by a state or federal statute or regulation.

(c) Hold public hearings.

(d) May accept assistance, financial and otherwise, from any public entity.
