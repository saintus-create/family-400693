## Text

A person shall not be appointed to, or be a member of, the board unless that person is a resident of the district and has resided within the district at least 30 days immediately prior to his or her appointment to the board.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 96, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
