## Text

The board may either itself operate the transit system, or any part thereof, or it may contract with any other public or private agency or corporation to operate all, or any part, of the transit system for the district, or it may contract with any other public or private agency or corporation for the improvement in transit services, facilities, equipment, or operations being operated and conducted by such agency or corporation, and all of the same either within the district, outside the district, or both.
