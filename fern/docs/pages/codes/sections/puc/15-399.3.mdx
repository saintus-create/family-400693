## Text

Nothing in Section 399.2 shall be construed to preclude any of California’s local publicly owned electric utilities from exercising authority to operate their electric distribution grid as provided under law.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1050, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
