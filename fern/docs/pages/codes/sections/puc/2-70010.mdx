## Text

It shall be the purpose of the Marin County Transit District to develop, finance, organize, and provide local Marin County transit service in a manner consistent with an overall San Francisco Bay Area regional transit system.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 83, Sec. 2.)
