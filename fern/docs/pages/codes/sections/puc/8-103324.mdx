## Text

The ballot for the election shall contain such instructions as are required by law to be printed thereon and in addition thereto, the following:Shall the San Mateo County Transit District be authorized to operate and levy a property tax at an annual rate which shall not exceed ______ cents ($0.___) on each one hundred dollars ($100) of assessed valuation within the district?YES◻NO ◻
