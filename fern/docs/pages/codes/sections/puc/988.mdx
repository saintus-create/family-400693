## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, requirements placed on a core transport agent shall not apply to gas services provided by a local publicly owned gas utility to customers within the jurisdiction or service territory of that local publicly owned gas utility.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 604, Sec. 4. (SB 656) Effective January 1, 2014.)
