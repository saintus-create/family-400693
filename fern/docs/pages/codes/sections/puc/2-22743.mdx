## Text

Each voter shall completely select the voting target next to either “Yes” or “No” respectively, to indicate whether the voter has voted for or against the issuance of the bonds.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 30. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
