## Text

All provisions of Article 5 (commencing with Section 132300) relating to allocation of revenues, tax rates, and bonds apply to the expanded, extended, or increased transactions and use tax, except that the revenues derived from the expanded or increased tax may be used for the additional purposes described in subdivision (a) of Section 132320.
