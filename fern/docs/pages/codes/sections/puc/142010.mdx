## Text

The authority is terminated two years from the last day on which the transactions and use tax authorized by this division is collected and as of that date this division is repealed.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 4. Effective July 14, 1986. Repealed on date prescribed by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Division 15, comprising Sections 142000 to 142277.)
