## Text

The ballots for the election shall contain substantially the instructions required to be printed on ballots for use at general state and county elections and in addition shall set forth the proposition of annexation substantially as follows: Shall the _______ (city) be annexed to _______ transit district in accordance with and subject to all of the terms and conditions of an agreement of annexation dated _______ now on file in the office of the clerk of _______ (city)?YESNO
