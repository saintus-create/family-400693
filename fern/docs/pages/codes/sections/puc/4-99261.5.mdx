## Text

The San Diego Metropolitan Transit Development Board shall adopt rules and regulations for its area of jurisdiction.

The transportation planning agency may include in its rules and regulations any rule and regulation of the transit development board.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1055, Sec. 6.)
