## Text

Any vacancy, including any caused by death or resignation of any member, shall be filled by appointment of the board of directors within 60 days of date of vacancy. Such appointment shall be made within the jurisdiction in which the vacancy occurred.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
