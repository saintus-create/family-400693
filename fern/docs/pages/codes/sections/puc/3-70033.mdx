## Text

The County Elections Official of the County of Marin shall compare the signatures to the petition with the affidavits of registration and certify to their sufficiency or insufficiency.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 109. Effective January 1, 2003.)
