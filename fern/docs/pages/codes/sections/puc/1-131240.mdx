## Text

Upon approval of a retail transactions and use tax at an election conducted pursuant to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 131100) in a county with an adopted county transportation expenditure plan that includes a provision for the creation of a county transportation authority, the authority shall be created at that election.

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
