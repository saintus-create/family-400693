## Text

For employees of the Los Angeles Metropolitan Transportation Authority covered under this chapter, this chapter shall supersede subdivisions (a) to (c), inclusive, of Section 30750 and Sections 30751 and 30755.

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 833, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
