## Text

The district is hereby designated a rapid transit district and the transit facilities and services the district is authorized to acquire, own, operate, control, or use are designated a rapid transit system, for the purposes of Part 15 (commencing with Section 35001) and Part 16 (commencing with Section 36000) of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.
