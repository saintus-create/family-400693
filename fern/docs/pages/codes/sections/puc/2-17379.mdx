## Text

The annexing board shall by resolution describe the territory ordered to be annexed, and set forth the boundaries of the district as they will exist after the annexation. The order shall also set forth in detail the terms and conditions, if any, of the annexation; and if the annexation is for limited purposes only, the land which is being annexed shall be described as one parcel and the land of the annexing district shall be described as a separate and distinct parcel.
