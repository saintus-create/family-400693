## Text

Any person, corporation, or association which is organized pursuant to any previously existing statute for the purpose of cooperatively selling gas is deemed to be organized and existing pursuant to this chapter, and this chapter applies to that person, corporation, or association.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
