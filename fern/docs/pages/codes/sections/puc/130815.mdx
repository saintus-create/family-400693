## Text

(a) The governing body of the authority shall consist of the following members:

(1) Five members of the San Bernardino County Board of Supervisors.

(2) One member from each County of San Bernardino incorporated city, who shall be a mayor or a city council member.

(3) One nonvoting member appointed by the Governor.

(b) Each County of San Bernardino incorporated city may appoint an alternate member to represent it at a meeting, but only if the regular member cannot attend the meeting. The alternate member shall either be a mayor or a city council member.
