## Text

The ballot shall contain the following:

(a) “Official ballot provided by the board of directors of the ‛____ airport district’ to be used in the bond election of said district in the county of ____, State of California, on the ____ day of ____ 20__.”

(b) Other matters required by law.

(c) To the right of or below the bond proposition on the ballot, the words “Yes” and “No” on separate lines, with voting targets.
