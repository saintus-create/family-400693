## Text

The commission shall not hire a staff, but shall use the staff of the San Diego Association of Governments and shall compensate the San Diego Association of Governments for the use of staff services as set forth in Section 132103.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1576, Sec. 23.)
