## Text

The district shall adopt and maintain a general transit plan for the district. The district may designate one of the transit plans it is otherwise required to prepare as the general transit plan.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 522, Sec. 9. (AB 709) Effective January 1, 2019.)
