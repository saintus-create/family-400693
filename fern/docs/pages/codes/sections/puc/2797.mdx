## Text

The commission shall permit the gas or electric corporation to recover in its revenue requirement and rates all costs to acquire, improve, upgrade, operate, and maintain transferred mobilehome park or manufactured housing community gas or electric systems.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 424, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
