## Text

The board shall supervise and regulate every transit facility owned and operated or controlled by the district, including the fixing of rates, rentals, charges and classifications, and the making and enforcement of regulations, contracts, practices, and schedules for, or in connection with, any transit facility owned or controlled by the district.
