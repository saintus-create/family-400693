## Text

The recommended county transportation expenditure plan shall be subject to a public hearing and then to a review by the commission, before submittal to the board of supervisors and local governments for adoption.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
