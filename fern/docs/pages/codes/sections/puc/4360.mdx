## Text

Nothing in this chapter affects the requirement that operators of liquefied petroleum gas (propane) master-meter systems supplying 10 or more customers from a single source comply with the applicable provisions of the federal law.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1630, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1991, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1630.)
