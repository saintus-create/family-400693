## Text

No person, corporation, or association, that is hereafter organized or doing business in this state, may use the word “cooperative” as part of its corporate name or other business name or title for gas producers’ cooperative marketing activities, unless it has complied with this chapter.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
