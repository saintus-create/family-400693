## Text

It shall be a condition of the operation of any transit facility owned or controlled by the board that Section 5333 of Title 49 of the United States Code shall be given effect to the extent required by law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 557, Sec. 29. Effective October 5, 2005.)
