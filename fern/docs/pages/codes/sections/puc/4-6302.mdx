## Text

The grantee of a franchise under this chapter shall be liable to the granting municipality for all damages proximately resulting from the failure of the grantee well and faithfully to observe and perform any provision of the franchise and any provision of this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
