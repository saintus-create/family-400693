## Text

The covenants, conditions, and provisions of the agreements, leases, and equipment trust certificates shall not conflict with any of the provisions of any trust agreement securing the payment of bonds, notes, or certificates of the board.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 599, Sec. 2.)
