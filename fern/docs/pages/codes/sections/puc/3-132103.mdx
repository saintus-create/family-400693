## Text

The commission shall expend only that amount of the funds generated pursuant to this chapter for staff support, audit, administrative expenses, and contract services that are necessary and reasonable to carry out its responsibilities pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1576, Sec. 23.)
