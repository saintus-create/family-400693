## Text

The covenants, conditions, and provisions of the agreements, leases and equipment trust certificates shall not conflict with any of the provisions of any trust agreement or similar document securing the payment of bonds, notes, or certificates of the consolidated agency.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 743, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
