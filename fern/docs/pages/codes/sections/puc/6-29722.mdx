## Text

The ballot for the election shall contain such instructions as are required by law to be printed thereon and in addition thereto the following: Shall territory in ____ (giving name of public agency or portion thereof) be annexed to San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit District, Special Service District No. ___ (here insert number)?YESNO
