## Text

The marketing contracts and agreements between an association and its members, and any agreements authorized in this chapter, are not illegal, in restraint of trade, or contrary to any statute which is enacted against pooling or combinations.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
