## Text

Payment of claims against the district and disbursement of district funds shall be in the manner provided for claims against a county and disbursements of funds of a county.

The board, however, may provide by resolution for the payment of claims against the district without the prior specific approval thereof by the board, subject to such conditions as prescribed by the board.
