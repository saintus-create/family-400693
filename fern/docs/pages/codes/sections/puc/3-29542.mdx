## Text

The ballots for the election shall contain substantially the instructions required to be printed on ballots for use at general state and county elections and in addition shall set forth the proposition of annexation substantially as follows: Shall the County of ________________ be annexed to San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit District in accordance with and subject to all the terms and conditions of an agreement of annexation dated _________ now on file in the office of the Clerk of _______________ County?YESNO
