## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative if a large electrical corporation elects not to participate pursuant to Section 3299 and is repealed on January 1 of the year following a notification by the commission pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 3299.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 119, Sec. 46. (SB 254) Effective September 19, 2025. Conditionally repealed on date prescribed by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 5, commencing with Section 3299; see additional repeal provisions in Section 3299.)
