## Text

Upon the hearing, the board shall determine whether or not the terms and conditions will be approved and shall hear and determine all objections thereto. Failure of any person interested in the district or in the matter of the proposed annexation on the terms and conditions set forth in the ordinance to show cause in writing pursuant to Section 90952 constitutes an assent on his part to a change in the boundaries of the district upon the terms and conditions set forth.
