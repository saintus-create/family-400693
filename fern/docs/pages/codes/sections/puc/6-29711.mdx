## Text

The board shall by resolution determine that the annexation of the territory will facilitate the acquisition or operation of transit facilities for the special service district, describe the territory, declare its intention to annex the territory to the special service district, set forth the terms and conditions upon which the territory shall be annexed, fix the time and place for hearing on the question of the annexation and provide for notice of the hearing.
