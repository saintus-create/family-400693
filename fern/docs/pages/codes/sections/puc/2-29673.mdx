## Text

At or before the hearing, any person interested may file with the secretary of the district written objections to the creation of the special service district or to the inclusion of his property therein. Upon the hearing the board shall hear and determine all protests and objections. The hearing may be continued from time to time by the board without further notice other than an order to be entered upon the minutes of its meeting fixing the time and place of the continued hearing.
