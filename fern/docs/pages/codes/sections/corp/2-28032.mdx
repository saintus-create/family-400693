## Text

“Affiliate” means any person or persons controlling, controlled by, or under common control with, other specified persons.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
