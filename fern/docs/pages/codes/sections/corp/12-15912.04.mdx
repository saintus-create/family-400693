## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 495, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date for Chapter 5.5 (later renumbered as Chapter 4.5), comprising Sections 15900 to 15912.07.)
