## Text

As used in this division “security” includes all of the things enumerated in Section 25019 and also includes shares, stock, and investment certificates as defined in the Savings Association Law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 458, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
