## Text

The commissioner may vacate or modify a stop order if he or she finds that the conditions which caused its entry have changed or that it is otherwise in the public interest to do so.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 64, Sec. 7. (AB 2742) Effective January 1, 2015.)
