## Text

The only interest of a partner which is transferable is the partner’s transferable interest. A transferable interest is personal property.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 495, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007. Section operative January 1, 2008, pursuant to Section 15912.04.)
