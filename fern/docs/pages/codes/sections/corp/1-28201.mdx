## Text

No licensee shall, except by prior written notice to the commissioner, transact business under any name other than its corporate name.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
