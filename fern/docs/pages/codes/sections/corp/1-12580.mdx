## Text

If any record subject to inspection pursuant to this chapter is not maintained in written form, a request for inspection is not complied with unless and until the corporation at its expense makes such record available in written form. For purposes of this chapter, “written” or “in writing” includes cathode ray tube and similar electronic communications methods.
