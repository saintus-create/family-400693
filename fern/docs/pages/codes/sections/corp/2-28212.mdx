## Text

The board of directors, executive committee, or other policy body of each licensee shall approve the contract to be entered into between the licensee and any person who will make recommendations with respect to the investment of funds of the licensee.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
