## Text

The department shall maintain a toll-free telephone number for deferred deposit transaction customers to make complaints and express concerns regarding the product or a specific licensee.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 777, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003. Section operative on December 31, 2004, or sooner, pursuant to Section 23104.)
