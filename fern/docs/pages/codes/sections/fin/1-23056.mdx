## Text

Every order, decision, license, or other official act of the commissioner is subject to judicial review in accordance with law.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 777, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003. Section operative on December 31, 2004, or sooner, pursuant to Section 23104.)
