## Text

Within 10 days of relocating a California branch office or California facility, a foreign (other state) credit union shall file a report with the commissioner which contains the information specified by the commissioner by regulation or order. The foreign (other state) credit union shall not conduct business at the new location of the California branch office or California facility without a license issued by the commissioner for the new location.
