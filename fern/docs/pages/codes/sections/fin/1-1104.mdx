## Text

No certificate of determination of a bank shall become effective unless such certificate of determination is filed with the Secretary of State with the commissioner’s approval endorsed thereon. Promptly after the certificate of determination becomes effective, such bank shall file with the commissioner a copy of the certificate of determination certified by the Secretary of State.
