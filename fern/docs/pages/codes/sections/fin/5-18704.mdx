## Text

No California industrial loan company may conduct any activity as an agent for an insured depository institution that the California industrial loan company would be prohibited from conducting as a principal.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 887, Sec. 71. Effective September 25, 1996.)
