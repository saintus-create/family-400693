## Text

(a) The exemptions and exclusions in this article are not applicable to a person engaged in business as a program administrator or a PACE solicitor.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2019.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 475, Sec. 19. (AB 1284) Effective October 4, 2017. Section operative January 1, 2019, by its own provisions.)
