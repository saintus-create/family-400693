## Text

(a) Any reference in a provision of any statute or regulation of this state to banks or commercial banks includes industrial banks.

(b) Subdivision (a) does not apply in any of the following cases:

(1) In case the provision or a related provision expressly provides otherwise.

(2) In the case of any provision of Division 1.6 (commencing with Section 4800) or this division.
