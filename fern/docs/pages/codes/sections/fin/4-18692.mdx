## Text

An application by a California industrial loan company for approval to have an insured depository institution engage in authorized agency activities as its agent shall be accompanied by a filing fee of two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 887, Sec. 61. Effective September 25, 1996.)
