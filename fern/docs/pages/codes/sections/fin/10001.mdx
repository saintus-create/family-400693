## Text

(a) No person, other than a California savings association or other person authorized by this division, shall do any business of an association.

(b) No foreign savings association may control a California savings association.

(c) No foreign holding company may control a California savings association.

(d) The commissioner shall obtain an injunction or take other action necessary to prevent any person from unlawfully doing any business of an association in this state.
