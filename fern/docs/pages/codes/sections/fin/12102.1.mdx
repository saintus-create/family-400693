## Text

A licensee under this division or any person engaged in the same type of business as licensed under this division whether said person is licensed or not shall be deemed to be an agent as defined under Sections 506 and 506a of the Penal Code and subject to the penalties under said sections.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 807.)
