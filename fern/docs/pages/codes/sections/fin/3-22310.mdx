## Text

(a) Except for a rebate or refund pursuant to any administrative, civil, or criminal action, or any act of the commissioner, a rebate or refund required to be made upon payment in full of a loan pursuant to this division need not be made if the aggregate of all rebates or refunds required in connection with a loan is less than one dollar ($1).

(b) No licensee shall contract for or receive any payment required in connection with a loan for the purpose of avoiding a rebate or refund of less than one dollar ($1).
