## Text

In no event shall the board employ through interagency agreement any investment personnel who would also serve during the term of the agreement as investment staff to the Board of Administration of the Public Employees’ Retirement System.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
