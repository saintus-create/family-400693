## Text

The Legislature recognizes that the amount of lottery revenues may fluctuate over time. Therefore, it is the intent of the Legislature that no additional funds be provided in order to offset any decline in lottery revenues.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1052, Sec. 1.)
