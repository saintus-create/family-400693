## Text

(a) Courses offered through a dual enrollment program offered by the local educational agency, with an agreement with an institution of higher education, shall be deemed dual credit courses.

(b) A dual credit course offered pursuant to subdivision (a) may be counted simultaneously toward both of the following:

(1) High school credit on an official transcript from the participating local educational agency.

(2) Postsecondary credit on an official transcript at the participating institution of higher education.
