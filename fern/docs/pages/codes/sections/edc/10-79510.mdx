## Text

As used in this article, unless the context requires otherwise, the following definitions apply:

(a) “AANHPI” means Asian American, Native Hawaiian, and Pacific Islander.

(b) “Community college” means a college of the California Community College system.

(c) “Program” means the AANHPI Student Achievement Program established pursuant to this article.
