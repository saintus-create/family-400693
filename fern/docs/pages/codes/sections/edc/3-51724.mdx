## Text

The attendance of a pupil in an evening high school program shall not result in the crediting of more than one unit of average daily attendance during the regular school year, even if the pupil also attends one or more classes in the regular high schools of the district.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
