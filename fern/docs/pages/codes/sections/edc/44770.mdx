## Text

In enacting this chapter, it is the intent of the Legislature to create and expand regional science resource centers to increase educational and training opportunities for the teachers and students of California. Towards that end, the Legislature intends to provide ongoing funding to support regional science resource centers, in partnership with nonprofit agencies which have demonstrated success in attaining private and governmental support for similar activities on a regional level.
