## Text

The agency shall award learning lab funding in compliance with both of the following:

(a) Learning lab funding shall be awarded to public postsecondary educational institutions geographically located in California.

(b) Recipients of learning lab funding shall include, but are not limited to, public postsecondary educational institutions in both northern and southern California.
