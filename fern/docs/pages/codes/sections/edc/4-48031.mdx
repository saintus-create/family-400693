## Text

Any person who is eligible to attend high school and who does not reside in a high school district or in a unified school district may attend high school in any high school district or unified school district in the county in which he or she resides or in another county.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1186, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1995.)
