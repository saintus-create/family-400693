## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that community college districts with noncredit programs recognize the importance of noncredit programs and that they will continue to support these offerings.

It is also the intent of the Legislature that community college districts with noncredit programs consider the need for both credit and noncredit allocations in allocating future growth pursuant to board of governors regulations.
