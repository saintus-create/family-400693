## Text

Funds allocated to any public educational entity from the California State Lottery Education Fund, pursuant to Section 8880.5 of the Government Code, may be used by that entity at its discretion in conformity with Section 8880.5.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1052, Sec. 1.)
