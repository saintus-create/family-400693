## Text

The proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the 1992 Higher Education Capital Outlay Bond Fund, which is hereby created.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 13, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 153 at the June 2, 1992, election.)
