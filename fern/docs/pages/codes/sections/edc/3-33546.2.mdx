## Text

(a) When the health curriculum framework is next revised, on or after January 1, 2025, the Instructional Quality Commission shall consider providing for inclusion, in that curriculum framework, content on sextortion.

(b) For the purposes of this section, “sextortion” means a threat to use sexual or intimate images or videos, however obtained, to compel another person to produce sexual or intimate images or videos, engage in sexual acts, or provide anything of value.
