## Text

The Zero-Textbook-Cost Degree Grant Program is hereby established to reduce the overall cost of education for students and decrease the time it takes students to complete degree programs offered by community colleges.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 24, Sec. 16. (AB 1602) Effective June 27, 2016.)
