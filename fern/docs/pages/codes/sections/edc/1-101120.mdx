## Text

The proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the 2016 State School Facilities Fund established in the State Treasury under Section 17070.41 and shall be allocated by the State Allocation Board pursuant to this chapter.

## History

(History: Added November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 51, Sec. 3.)
