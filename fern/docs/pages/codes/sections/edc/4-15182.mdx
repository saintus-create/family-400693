## Text

Any bonds executed in the manner provided by the board of supervisors shall be valid, notwithstanding any change in the officers who signed the bonds or the coupons, or in the seal of the board of supervisors, occurring after the execution.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
