## Text

This part shall be implemented only during those fiscal years for which funds are appropriated for purposes of this part in the annual Budget Act.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 361, Sec. 2. (SB 1402) Effective January 1, 2013. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 88651.)
