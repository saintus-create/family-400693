## Text

There is hereby established in the Neurology Department at the University of California, San Francisco, a research project on substance abuse. The major goal of this research is to identify new pharmaceutical agents to prevent or treat alcohol and drug addiction. It is the intent of the Legislature that dedicated state funding for this research will be provided for five years, and be appropriated in the annual Budget Act. It is further the intent of the Legislature that the augmentation of one million dollars ($1,000,000) per year appropriated in the Budget Act of 2000 for this program be used for permanent on-going support of the program.
