## Text

(a) A district that plans to operate a Concept 6 program after June 30, 2009, shall by July 1 of 2009, and by July 1 of any succeeding year in which it plans to operate a Concept 6 program, submit evidence in writing to establish to the satisfaction of the Superintendent of Public Instruction that it has developed a specific school building plan to provide adequate pupil capacity to eliminate the Concept 6 program by the earliest practicable date and no later than July 1, 2012.

(b) The superintendent shall evaluate the written submission to determine whether the district has developed a specific school building plan and shall submit a report to the state board.
