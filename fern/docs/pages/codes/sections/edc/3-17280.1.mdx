## Text

Written rules and regulations adopted pursuant to this article to clarify the application of the California Building Standards Code shall be made available to the public by the State Architect upon request.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 642, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
