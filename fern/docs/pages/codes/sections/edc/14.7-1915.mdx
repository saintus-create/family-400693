## Text

In addition to any educational opportunity available under Article 14.5 (commencing with Section 1900), any youth or adult offender in custody in Sacramento County shall be permitted, notwithstanding any other provision of law, to enroll in any appropriate educational program operated by a school district, community college district, or private school in that county, subject to the objectives, standards, and conditions set forth in Section 1203m of the Penal Code.
