## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this article that the governing board of each school district, each county superintendent of schools and each state special school or center employ qualified persons to serve as readers for the legally blind certified classroom teachers employed by each of these entities.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1288, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
