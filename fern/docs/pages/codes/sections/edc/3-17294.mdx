## Text

“Construction or alteration” as used in this article includes any construction, reconstruction, or alteration of, or addition to, any school building.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
