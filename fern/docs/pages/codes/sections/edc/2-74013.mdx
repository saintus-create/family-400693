## Text

“Former district” means a district which has been wholly included in another district. The boundaries of a former district are those of the district as it existed immediately prior to being wholly included in another district.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1214, Sec. 5. Effective September 22, 1982.)
