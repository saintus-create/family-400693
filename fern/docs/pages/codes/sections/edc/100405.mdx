## Text

For purposes of this part, “Chapter 12” means Chapter 12 (commencing with Section 17000) of Part 10 and “Chapter 12.5” means Chapter 12.5 (commencing with Section 17070.10) of Part 10.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 407, Sec. 16. Approved in Proposition 1A at the November 3, 1998, election.)
