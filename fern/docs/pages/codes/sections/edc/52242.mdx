## Text

A school district may help pay for all or part of the costs of one or more advanced placement examinations that are charged to economically disadvantaged pupils.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 538, Sec. 29. (SB 416) Effective January 1, 2016.)
