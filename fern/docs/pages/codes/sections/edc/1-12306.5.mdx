## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision in this article or Section 2795 of the Public Resources Code, the Controller shall apportion money received pursuant to Section 12304 for potash deposits located in the Trona Joint Unified School District and the Kern Community College District as follows:

(a) Kern Community College District shall receive 15 percent of the total apportionment.

(b) Trona Joint Unified School District shall receive 85 percent of the total apportionment.
