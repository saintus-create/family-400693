## Text

In its review of electronic learning assessment resources, the California Learning Resource Network shall explore the feasibility of creating, implementing, and maintaining an online customer feedback system that would allow purchasers of electronic learning assessment resources identified and reviewed to document the extent to which these electronic learning assessment resources contribute to improved instructional planning.
