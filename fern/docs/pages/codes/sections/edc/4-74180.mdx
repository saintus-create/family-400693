## Text

Where an action to reorganize or form districts would affect territory which is located in more than one county, or which is under the jurisdiction of more than one county superintendent of schools, the proceedings to be conducted or the actions to be taken by county officers or agencies shall be conducted or taken according to the provisions of Article 3 (commencing with Section 35520) of Chapter 3 of Part 21.
