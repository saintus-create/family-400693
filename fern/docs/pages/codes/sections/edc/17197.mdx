## Text

To the extent that the provisions of this chapter are inconsistent with any other provisions of any general statute, or a special act or parts thereof, the provisions of this chapter shall be deemed controlling.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
