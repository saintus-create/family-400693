## Text

“Faculty” means the instructional staff of an institution, whether these persons are employees or independent contractors.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 310, Sec. 6. (AB 48) Effective January 1, 2010. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 94950.)
