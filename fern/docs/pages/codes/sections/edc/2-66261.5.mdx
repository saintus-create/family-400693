## Text

“Postsecondary educational institution” means a public or private institution of vocational, professional, or postsecondary education; the governing board of a community college district; the Regents of the University of California; or the Trustees of the California State University.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 914, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 1999.)
