## Text

The chancellor’s office may contract with community college districts for the performance of administrative services necessary to implement Section 12419.7 of the Government Code. The chancellor’s office may charge the districts for the costs of those administrative services.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 937, Sec. 1.)
