## Text

Each school district to which an apportionment is made under this chapter shall repay the principal amount of the apportionment in the manner prescribed by this article.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
