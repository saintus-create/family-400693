## Text

The State Treasurer, as required by the federal acts relating to vocational education referred to in Sections 12050 to 12054, inclusive, and Sections 12020, 12220, and 12300 to 12307, inclusive, shall receive and provide for the proper custody of all funds apportioned to the state under these acts. The amount received shall be deposited to the credit of the Vocational Education Federal Fund.
