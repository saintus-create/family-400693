## Text

Commencing with the 1997–98 fiscal year, community college districts, to the extent that funding is provided in the annual Budget Act, may provide counseling and matriculation services for students enrolled in credit courses and students enrolled in noncredit courses, according to a welfare-to-work plan as provided in Section 11325.21 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 270, Sec. 16. Effective August 11, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998, by Sec. 183 of Ch. 270.)
