## Text

Except as provided in Sections 26504 and 26507, the employer shall contribute an amount equivalent to 4 percent of salary for each participant employed by the employer.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1048, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 1999.)
