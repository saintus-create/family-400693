## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the Regents of the University of California shall eliminate all policies that detrimentally and unreasonably affect the employment status of females hired by the university.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 278. Effective January 1, 1996.)
