## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall require a UC campus foundation to disclose information that is exempt from disclosure pursuant to an exemption set forth in Section 7922.000 of the Government Code or in any provision listed in Section 7920.505 of the Government Code.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 615, Sec. 87. (AB 474) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 463 of Stats. 2021, Ch. 615.)
