## Text

(a) A member retired for disability under this part may terminate the disability retirement allowance upon written request to the system.

(b) If a member retired for disability under this part is determined by the board to no longer be eligible to receive a disability retirement allowance pursuant to this chapter, the disability retirement allowance shall be terminated.
