## Text

Where one governing board serves as the governing board of two or more separate districts, an employee may fulfill the service requirements provided in Sections 45382 or 45384, or both, by service in any one or more of the districts under the jurisdiction of such governing board.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
