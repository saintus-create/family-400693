## Text

The Superintendent of Public Instruction shall direct the California Learning Resource Network to implement the provisions of this chapter by prioritizing the use of existing budgeted resources available to the network.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 397, Sec. 2. Effective September 9, 2004.)
