## Text

The county superintendent of schools may contract with an acceptable party who is one of the three lowest responsible bidders for the lease, purchase, or maintenance of electronic data-processing systems and for the lease, purchase, or maintenance of supporting software.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 288, Sec. 2.)
