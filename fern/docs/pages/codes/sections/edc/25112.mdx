## Text

Personnel, including elected school officials, acting on behalf of an employer, may not receive consideration from a vendor in exchange for the promotion of a particular vendor or vendor’s products.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 230, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2009.)
