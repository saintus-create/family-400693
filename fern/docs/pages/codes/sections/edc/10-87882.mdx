## Text

For purposes of this article, “part-time faculty” means any person who is employed to teach for not more than the hours per week described in Section 87482.5.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 187, Sec. 9. (SB 511) Effective January 1, 2010.)
