## Text

A community college district’s offer to sell or transfer the land shall be made to all park districts, cities, and counties in which the community college district is wholly or partially situated pursuant to this article and shall remain open for not less than 60 days. The sale or transfer shall be made to whichever public entity first accepts the offer, or whichever public entity can negotiate satisfactorily for the purchase or transfer of such surplus land.
