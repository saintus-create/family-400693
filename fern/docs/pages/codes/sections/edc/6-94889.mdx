## Text

Except as provided in subdivision (b) of Section 94890, an approval to operate shall be for a term of five years.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 552, Sec. 8. (SB 802) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 94950.)
