## Text

Any disability allowance under this part that has accrued and remains unpaid to a disabled member at the time of death shall be paid to the person entitled to receive a family allowance under this part or, if none, to the beneficiary entitled to receive the death payment under this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 965, Sec. 212. Effective January 1, 1999.)
