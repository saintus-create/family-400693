## Text

In accepting the benefits of the acts of Congress referred to in Section 12050, the people of the state agree to comply with all of the provisions and to observe all of the requirements of those acts.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 758, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1996.)
