## Text

(a) Pledges by or to the authority of revenues, moneys, accounts, accounts receivable, contract rights, or other rights to payment of any other kind made by or to the authority pursuant to this chapter shall be valid and binding from the time the pledge is made for the benefit of the pledges, and the successors thereto.

(b) The revenues, moneys, accounts, accounts receivable, and other rights to payment of any other kind pledged by or to the authority or its assignees, shall immediately be subject to the lien of the pledge without physical delivery, or any further act. The lien of any pledge shall be valid and binding against all parties, irrespective of whether the parties have notice of the claim. The trust agreement, indenture, resolution, or other instrument by which any pledge is created need not be recorded.
