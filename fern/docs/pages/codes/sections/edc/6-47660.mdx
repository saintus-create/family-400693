## Text

For purposes of computing eligibility for, and entitlements to, general purpose funding and operational funding for categorical programs, the enrollment and average daily attendance of a sponsoring local educational agency shall exclude the enrollment and attendance of pupils in its charter schools funded pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 47, Sec. 87. (AB 97) Effective July 1, 2013.)
