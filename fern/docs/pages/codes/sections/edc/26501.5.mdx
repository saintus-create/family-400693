## Text

A person who elects, pursuant to Section 26403, to participate in the Cash Balance Benefit Program shall make contributions, as provided in Section 26501, based on his or her salary or other compensation earned for trustee service.

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1020, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2001.)
