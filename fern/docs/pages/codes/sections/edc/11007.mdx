## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall become operative July 1, 1984.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 8. Effective July 28, 1983. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (July 1, 1984) for Chapter 11, commencing with Section 11000.)
