## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the board may retain a bank or trust company to serve as custodian for safekeeping, delivery, securities valuation, investment performance reporting, and other services in connection with investment of the retirement fund.

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 893, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
