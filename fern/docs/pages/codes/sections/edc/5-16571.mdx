## Text

For purposes of computing the repayment, the state project shall be deemed completed on June 30th of the fiscal year in which the state project is completed and accepted by the state.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
