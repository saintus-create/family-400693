## Text

If the governing board of a California community college district elects to participate in an interstate attendance agreement, it may waive, as a condition to such participation, all or part of the nonresident tuition required by Section 76140 in accordance with the terms of that interstate attendance agreement. Such a waiver shall apply only to students attending a community college maintained by that district pursuant to the provisions of that interstate attendance agreement.
