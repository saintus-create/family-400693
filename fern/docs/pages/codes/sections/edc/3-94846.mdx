## Text

“Instruction” means an institution’s specific, formal arrangements in which its faculty present a part of the curriculum.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 310, Sec. 6. (AB 48) Effective January 1, 2010. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 94950.)
