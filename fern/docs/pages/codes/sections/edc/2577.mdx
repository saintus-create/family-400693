## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, revenue limit funding for county superintendents of schools for the 2012–13 fiscal year and prior fiscal years shall continue to be adjusted pursuant to Article 3 (commencing with Section 2550) of Chapter 12, as that article read on January 1, 2013.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 53. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
