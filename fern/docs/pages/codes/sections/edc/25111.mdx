## Text

Each employer, in consultation with the exclusive bargaining agent of its employees, if any, may develop a process to ensure that employees are aware of, and have access to, information provided in the impartial investment bank maintained by the board.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 230, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2009.)
