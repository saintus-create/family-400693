## Text

The State Allocation Board shall establish guidelines and procedures to be utilized in determining the eligibility of school districts for allowances provided pursuant to this article with respect to facilities and equipment for the education of pupils enrolled in development centers for the handicapped. The guidelines and procedures shall provide that in order to be eligible to receive the allowance the school district has no existing facilities which could be utilized for a development center for the handicapped.
