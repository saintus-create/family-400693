## Text

It is the intent and purpose of the Legislature to encourage, by every means possible, the reduction of class sizes and the ratio of pupils to teachers in all grade levels in the public schools, and to urge every effort to this end to be undertaken by the local school administrative authorities.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 840, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1995.)
