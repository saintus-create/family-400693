## Text

There is hereby established the Community College Part-Time Faculty Office Hours Program for the purpose of providing community college students equal access to academic advice and assistance and to encourage community college districts to provide opportunities by compensating part-time faculty who hold office hours related to their teaching load.

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 933, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
