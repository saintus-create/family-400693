## Text

A local educational agency shall ensure that the parent of each individual with exceptional needs is a member of any group that makes decisions on the educational placement of the individual with exceptional needs.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 56, Sec. 48. Effective January 1, 2008.)
