## Text

The boundaries of each high school district and unified school district shall be coextensive with the boundaries of the component districts, as defined in Section 35515, included within it.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 301, Sec. 2. (SB 1057) Effective January 1, 2023.)
