## Text

This chapter shall be implemented only to the extent that adequate funding for its purposes, as determined by the Regents of the University of California, is appropriated to the University of California in the annual Budget Act or other statute.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 34, Sec. 32. (SB 860) Effective June 20, 2014.)
