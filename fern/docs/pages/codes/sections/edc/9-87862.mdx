## Text

The governing board of a community college district may provide a program of health insurance for part-time faculty, multidistrict part-time faculty, and their dependents.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 572, Sec. 11. (AB 190) Effective September 27, 2022.)
