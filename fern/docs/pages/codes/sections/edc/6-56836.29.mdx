## Text

If special education local plan areas reorganize, including by merger or division, the department shall adjust rates for payments to and from the resulting special education local plan areas so that overall funding neither increases nor decreases from what it would have been before the reorganization.

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 15, Sec. 61. (AB 99) Effective June 27, 2017.)
