## Text

The trustees shall establish appropriate miscellaneous fees for the academy. These fees shall be for purposes unique to the academy, such as the annual training cruise and uniforms.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1298, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 1.5 of Ch. 1298.)
