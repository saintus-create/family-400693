## Text

Each member of a legislative body who attends a meeting of that legislative body where an action is taken in violation of any provision of this article, with knowledge that the meeting is in violation of this article, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 330, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
