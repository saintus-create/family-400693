## Text

Under circumstances in which portions of this statute are subject to conflicting interpretations, Section 300 shall be assumed to contain the governing intent of the statute.

## History

(History: Added June 2, 1998, by initiative Proposition 227. Note: In the text, "this statute" refers to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 300), which was added by Prop. 227.)
