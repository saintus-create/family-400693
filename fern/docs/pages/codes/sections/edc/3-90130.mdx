## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the trustees shall determine eligibility for admission and continued enrollment at the academy.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1298, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 1.5 of Ch. 1298.)
