## Text

Opportunity schools established pursuant to this article by the county board of education shall be limited to counties which have an average daily attendance of less than 8,000.

## History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010. Note: The Jan. 1, 2006, article repeal date (in former Section 48642) was made inapplicable to this section by Stats. 2005, Ch. 118, Sec. 10.)
