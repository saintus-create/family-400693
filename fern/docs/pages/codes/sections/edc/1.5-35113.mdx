## Text

When a governing board has adopted a resolution pursuant to Section 35110, the length of service of each employee shall be counted from the original date of employment by either school district under the jurisdiction of the governing board.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 22, Sec. 1.)
