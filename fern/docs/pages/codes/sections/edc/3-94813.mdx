## Text

“Accredited” means an institution is accredited by an accrediting agency recognized by the United States Department of Education.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 840, Sec. 4. (SB 1247) Effective January 1, 2015. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 94950.)
