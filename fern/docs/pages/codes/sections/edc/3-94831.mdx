## Text

“Degree title” means the designated subject area of the educational program that appears on the face of the document awarded to a student.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 310, Sec. 6. (AB 48) Effective January 1, 2010. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 94950.)
