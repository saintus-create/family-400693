## Text

The State Board of Education shall adopt regulations pursuant to Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, commonly referred to as the rulemaking provisions of the Administrative Procedure Act, to implement this chapter.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 232 by Stats. 1998, Ch. 914, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1999.)
