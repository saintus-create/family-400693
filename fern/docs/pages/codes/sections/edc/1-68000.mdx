## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the public institutions of higher education shall apply uniform rules, as set forth in this chapter and not otherwise, in determining whether a student shall be classified as resident or a nonresident.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010.)
