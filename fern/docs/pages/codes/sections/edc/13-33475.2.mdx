## Text

For purposes of this article, the following definitions shall apply:

(a) “CIRM” means the California Institute for Regenerative Medicine.

(b) “Department” means the State Department of Education.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 185, Sec. 2. (SB 471) Effective January 1, 2010.)
