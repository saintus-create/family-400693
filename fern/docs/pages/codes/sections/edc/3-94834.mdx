## Text

“Distance education” means transmission of instruction to students at a location separate from the faculty.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 592, Sec. 37. (SB 861) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 94950.)
