## Text

The provisions of Section 17540 shall be applicable to a sale of personal property from a unified school district whose boundaries are coterminous with a city or city and county to that city or city and county.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998.)
