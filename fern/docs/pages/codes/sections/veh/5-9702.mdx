## Text

An additional fee of fifteen dollars ($15) shall be charged for each application for partial year registration, or renewal thereof, whenever a person pays the fee under Section 9400 or 9400.1, as provided in Section 9700.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 719, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2004.)
