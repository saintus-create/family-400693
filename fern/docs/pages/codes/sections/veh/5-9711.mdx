## Text

This article does not apply to any owner or lessor of a commercial vehicle based in a foreign jurisdiction which does not grant reciprocity or proportionate registration to residents of this state while operating within that jurisdiction.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 907, Sec. 2.)
