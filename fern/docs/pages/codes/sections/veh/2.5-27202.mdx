## Text

For the purposes of Section 27200, the following noise limits shall apply to any motorcycle, other than a motor-driven cycle, manufactured:(1) After 1969, and before 1973 88 dbA(2) After 1972, and before 1975 86 dbA(3) After 1974, and before 1986 83 dbA(4) After 1985 80 dbA
