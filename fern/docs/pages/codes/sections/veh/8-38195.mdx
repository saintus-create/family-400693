## Text

The provisions of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 5600) of Division 3 shall be applicable to off-highway motor vehicles subject to identification, and the terms “registration,” “registration card,” and “registered” as used therein, shall apply to the terms “identification,” “identification certificate,” and “identified,” respectively, except that Sections 5901, 5902, 5903, 5904, 5906, and 6052 shall not apply.
