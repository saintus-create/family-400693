## Text

(a) Payments to nondesignated public hospitals under the demonstration project shall include, as applicable, the following:

(1) Payments under selective provider contracts with the department negotiated by the California Medical Assistance Commission in accordance with Article 2.6 (commencing with Section 14081).

(2) Disproportionate share hospital payments under Section 14166.16.

(3) Supplemental payments under Section 14166.17.

(4) Payments to distressed hospitals as negotiated by the California Medical Assistance Commission pursuant to Section 14166.23.

(5) Payment of amounts described in Section 14166.19.

(b) Payments under subdivision (a) shall be in addition to other payments that may be made in accordance with law.
