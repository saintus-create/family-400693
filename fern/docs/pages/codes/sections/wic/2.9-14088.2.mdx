## Text

The primary care provider or other entity eligible pursuant to this article with whom a contract has been entered into pursuant to this article shall have responsibility for providing for case management as defined, pursuant to Section 14088.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1212, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
