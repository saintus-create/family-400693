## Text

As used in this chapter, “Youth Authority,” “authority,” and “the authority” mean and refer to the Department of the Youth Authority, and “board” means and refers to the Youth Authority Board.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 183, Sec. 372. Effective January 1, 2005.)
