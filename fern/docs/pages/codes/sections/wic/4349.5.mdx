## Text

Grants that have been awarded prior to the effective date of this section shall continue to be subject to the provisions of this chapter, including the grant recipient, matching, and eligibility requirements.

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 722, Sec. 20. Effective September 15, 1992.)
