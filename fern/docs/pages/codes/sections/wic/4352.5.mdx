## Text

Up to 10 percent of the total state funds available annually for the primary intervention program from all sources may be utilized by the department for administration, training, consultation, and evaluation.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 858, Sec. 13. Effective October 14, 1991.)
