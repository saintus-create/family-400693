## Text

Prepaid health plans, or their subcontractors, shall not bill any enrollee for covered benefits provided under this chapter and for which capitation has been paid, except as provided in Article 7 (commencing with Section 14490) of this chapter. Health care providers shall not seek reimbursement from enrollees for any services provided under this chapter.
