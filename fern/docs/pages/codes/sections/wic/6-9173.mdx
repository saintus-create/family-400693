## Text

For purposes of this article, “older and aging” means an adult 60 years of age or older.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 364, Sec. 2. (AB 450) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 9175.)
