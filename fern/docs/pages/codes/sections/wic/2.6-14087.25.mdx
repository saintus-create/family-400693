## Text

In order to achieve maximum cost savings the Legislature hereby determines that an expedited contract process for contracts under this article is necessary. Therefore, contracts under this article may be on a nonbid basis, and shall be exempt from the provisions of Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 14780), Part 5.5, Division 3 of the Government Code.
