## Text

Pursuant to Sections 14024 and 14124.90, the Director of Health Services may recover for the cost of targeted case management services rendered under Section 14132.44 to eligible Medi-Cal beneficiaries, from any person, corporation, or partnership who, at the time services are rendered, has a contractual or legal obligation to pay for the services.
