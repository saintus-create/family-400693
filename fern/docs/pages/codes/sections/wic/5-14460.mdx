## Text

A schedule of reviews, visits, and audits shall be jointly established by the Department of Managed Health Care or the Department of Insurance, as the case may be, and the State Department of Health Services. Nothing in Section 14456, 14457, or 14459 shall be construed to prohibit the State Department of Health Services from conducting reviews, visits, or audits either jointly or individually, for the purpose of following up on findings resulting from reviews, visits, or audits carried out in accordance with this chapter.
