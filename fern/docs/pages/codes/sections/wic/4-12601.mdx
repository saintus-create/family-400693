## Text

Funds for the costs and administration of Articles 2 (commencing with Section 12525) and 3 (commencing with Section 12550) of this chapter shall be limited to the amounts and controls set forth in the Budget Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1216.)
