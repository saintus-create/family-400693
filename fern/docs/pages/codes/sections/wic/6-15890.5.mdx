## Text

Each health plan contracting with the department pursuant to Article 4 (commencing with Section 15881) shall submit annually to the department rates which it estimates are sufficient to cover the cost of providing major risk medical coverage to its subscribers. The rates shall be submitted on the basis of categories of risk which shall be established by the department.
