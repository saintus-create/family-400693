## Text

Mental hospitals, health facilities, or other institutions, or treating health professionals or psychotherapists who provide reports subject to this chapter shall be civilly immune for making any report required or authorized by this chapter. This section is declaratory of existing law.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 951, Sec. 11.)
