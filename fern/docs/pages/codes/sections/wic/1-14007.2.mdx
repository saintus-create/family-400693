## Text

(a) Any individual who is otherwise eligible for Medi-Cal services, but who does not meet the documentation requirements described in subdivision (e) of Section 14011.2, shall be eligible only for the scope of services made available to persons who are not citizens or nationals of the United States under subdivision (d) of Section 14007.5, and Sections 14007.65, 14007.7, and 14007.8.

(b) To the extent that federal financial participation is available to fund services described under subdivision (a), the department shall file all necessary state plan amendments or waivers to obtain that funding.
