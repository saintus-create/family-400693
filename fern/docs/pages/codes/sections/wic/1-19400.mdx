## Text

It is the purpose of this chapter to extend the opportunities for individuals with disabilities for employment through encouragement of local communities to develop rehabilitation programs and to provide the state with information concerning their effectiveness in providing employment and rehabilitation services to individuals with disabilities.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 937, Sec. 23. Effective October 8, 1993.)
