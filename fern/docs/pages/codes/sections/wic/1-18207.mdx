## Text

Sections 18205 and 18206 shall apply only to experimental projects for in-home supportive services established pursuant to Section 18204 that were in existence on July 1, 1994.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1124, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
