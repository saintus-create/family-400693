## Text

All utilization controls applied to inpatient hospital services by the director in accordance with Section 14133.1 shall continue to be applied to inpatient hospital services rendered under this article, except that the director may waive utilization controls which are no longer necessary in the case of hospitals entering into negotiated, capitated, at-risk contracts under this article.
