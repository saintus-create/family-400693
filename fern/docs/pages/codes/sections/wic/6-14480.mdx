## Text

No prepaid health plan or pilot program contract with an existing or proposed contractor shall be approved or renewed if a state officer or state employee provides legal or management services to the contracting organization. For the purposes of this section no state officer or state employee shall share in the income or any remuneration derived from the providing of legal or management services to a contracting organization.
