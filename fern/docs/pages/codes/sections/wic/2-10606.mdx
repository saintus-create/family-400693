## Text

The department shall cause to be published and made available, at no cost to the public, all of its rules and regulations relating to:

(a) The government of the department.

(b) Any form of public assistance for which state aid is granted to the counties or over the administration of which the department has supervision.

The department shall also provide at no cost such subscription service as may be necessary to assure prompt receipt of all additions and amendments to the rules and regulations of the department and digests of decisions compiled under Section 10964.
