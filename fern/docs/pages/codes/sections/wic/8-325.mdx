## Text

A proceeding in the juvenile court to declare a child to be a dependent child of the court is commenced by the filing with the court, by the social worker, of a petition, in conformity with the requirements of this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1054, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1999.)
