## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to prohibit the compilation and publication of statistical data for use by government or researchers under standards set by the Director of State Hospitals.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 440, Sec. 64. (AB 1488) Effective September 22, 2012.)
