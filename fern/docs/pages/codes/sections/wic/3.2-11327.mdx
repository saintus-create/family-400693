## Text

Any county which fails to provide services according to its plan approved pursuant to this article shall receive sanctions in accordance with Section 10605. A recipient or a group of recipients may request the director to invoke Section 10605.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 306, Sec. 30. Effective August 3, 1995.)
