## Text

Effective for expenditures incurred after enactment of any new demonstration project under Article 5.4 (commencing with Section 14180), any federal financial participation that is available under the federal Medicaid Program, or any related waiver or demonstration project, based on the certified public expenditures of designated public hospitals, as defined in subdivision (d) of Section 14166.1, or the governmental entities with which they are affiliated, shall be paid to designated public hospitals or the governmental entities with which they are affiliated.
