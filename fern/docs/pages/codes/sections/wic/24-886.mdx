## Text

Except as provided in Section 886.5, no juvenile home, ranch, camp, or forestry camp established pursuant to the provisions of this article shall receive or contain more than 100 children at any one time.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 375, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
