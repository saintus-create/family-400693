## Text

The director shall submit an annual report to the Legislature by March 1 of each year setting forth pertinent facts on the operation of the program established by this chapter and its significance in relation to the out-of-home care services of the Medi-Cal program.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
