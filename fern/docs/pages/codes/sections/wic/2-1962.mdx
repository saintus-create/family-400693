## Text

The Office of Youth and Community Restoration may monitor the forms, documents, and information submitted by counties pursuant to Section 1961 and may advise counties and provide technical assistance on the implementation and requirements of Section 1961.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 50, Sec. 8. (AB 169) Effective July 2, 2024.)
