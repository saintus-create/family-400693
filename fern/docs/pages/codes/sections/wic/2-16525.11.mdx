## Text

Each participating county shall recruit foster families that shall be licensed as foster family homes and trained to care for children who are alcohol- or drug-exposed or who test HIV positive.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 296, Sec. 2. Effective August 2, 1993.)
