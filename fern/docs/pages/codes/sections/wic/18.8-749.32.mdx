## Text

As used in this article, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “County juvenile facilities” means county juvenile halls or camps.

(b) “Board” means the Board of Corrections.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 499, Sec. 1. Effective September 15, 1998.)
