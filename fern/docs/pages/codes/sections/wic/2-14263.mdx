## Text

“Marketing” means any activity conducted by or on behalf of a prepaid health plan where information regarding the services offered by a prepaid health plan is disseminated in order to persuade Medi-Cal beneficiaries to enroll or accept any application for enrollment in the prepaid health plan. Marketing shall also include any similar activity to procure the endorsement of the prepaid health plan from any individual or organization.
