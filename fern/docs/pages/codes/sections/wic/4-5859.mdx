## Text

If applications are deficient and not ready for approval, department program staff shall provide specific written descriptions of areas of deficiency to counties and provide, to the extent feasible, any requested training, consultation, and technical assistance to assist the applicant county to achieve necessary compliance and department approval.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 520, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001.)
