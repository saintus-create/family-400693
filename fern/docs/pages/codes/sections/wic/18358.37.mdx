## Text

The department shall develop, in consultation with the counties, providers, and other stakeholders, cost reporting, claiming, and other procedures necessary to maximize federal financial participation.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 486, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2009. Conditionally inoperative on or after July 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 18358.38. Repealed as of January 1 following the inoperative date.)
