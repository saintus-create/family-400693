## Text

Nothing in this article shall prevent a physician from utilizing an agent who furnishes billing and collection services to the physician to submit claims or receive payment for claims.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 278, Sec. 21. Effective July 30, 1991.)
