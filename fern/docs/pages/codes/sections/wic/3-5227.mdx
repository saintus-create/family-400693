## Text

The order for evaluation shall be in substantially the following form: In the Superior Court of the State of California for the County of _____The People of the State of California Concerning andRespondents

No. _____ Order for EvaluationThe People of the State of California to :(Professional person in charge of the facility providing evaluation)______ has appeared before me and appears to be, as a result of ______ (chronic alcoholism, the use of narcotics, or the use of restricted dangerous drugs), a danger to himself, or others, or gravely disabled.Now, therefore, you are directed to evaluate ______ at ______ on the ____ day of ____, 19__, at __ o’clock _m.Witness my hand, this ____ day of ____, 19__.Judge of the ________ CourtReturn of OrderI hereby certify that I received the above order for the evaluation of ______ and on the ____ day of ____, 19__, personally served a copy of the order and of the petition on the professional person in charge of the ______, a facility for treatment and evaluation, or his designee.Dated: ____ 19__.Signature and Title
