## Text

The State Department of Health Care Services and the State Department of Social Services, in consultation with community treatment providers, local mental health departments, and county welfare departments, shall develop joint protocols for the oversight of community treatment facilities.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 65. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012.)
