## Text

When placing foster children, the placing agency shall not decline to place a child with a resource family because of a resource family parent’s actual or perceived sexual orientation, gender identity, or gender expression.

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 967, Sec. 4. (AB 2466) Effective January 1, 2023.)
