## Text

For purposes of subdivision (p) of Section 14132, “rehabilitation services” means services intended to assist physically or cognitively impaired persons to achieve or regain their maximum functional potential for mobility, self-care, and independent living.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1360, Sec. 2.)
