## Text

All decisions of the commission shall be by a majority of four votes, including the hiring of the executive director. The commission shall retain an independent office of legal counsel for purposes of contract review. All contracts shall be reviewed by legal counsel.

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 329, Sec. 11.)
