## Text

Upon receipt of an application for in-home supportive services, the county shall provide the applicant with a confirmation number to serve as documentation that the applicant filed an application for in-home supportive services. The county may use the case number as the confirmation number.

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 402, Sec. 1. (AB 1797) Effective January 1, 2017.)
