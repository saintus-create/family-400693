## Text

The “basic scope of health care benefits” means:

(a) Physician’s services;

(b) Hospital outpatient services;

(c) Laboratory and X-ray;

(d) Prescription drugs;

(e) Hospital inpatient care;

(f) Skilled nursing facility care.
