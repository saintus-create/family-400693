## Text

The purpose of this article is to provide out-of-home care to those recipients of public assistance for whom care in their own homes is impractical; however, the provisions of this article shall not duplicate intermediate care or other out-of-home facility services provided under Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 14000) of Part 3 of Division 9.
