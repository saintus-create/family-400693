## Text

The department shall establish a range of amounts for the personal and incidental needs of recipients in out-of-home care under this chapter provided that the minimum amount for personal and incidental needs shall not be less than eighty-three dollars ($83) per month and that the range of amounts so established shall be subject to the provisions set forth in Section 12201.
