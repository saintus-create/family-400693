## Text

Upon the availability of federal funding, the department shall modify the Medi-Cal program mail-in application form, and other appropriate materials, and the single point-of-entry application form, to allow applicants in counties served by managed care plans to contact the enrollment contractor by using the Health Care Options toll-free telephone number to request and receive enrollment materials before a Medi-Cal eligibility determination has been made.
