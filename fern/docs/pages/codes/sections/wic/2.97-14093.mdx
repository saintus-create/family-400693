## Text

The purpose of this article is to ensure quality of care and to provide increased access to health care services in the most cost-effective and efficient manner possible, to persons who are eligible to receive medical benefits under publicly supported programs other than Medi-Cal.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 722, Sec. 86. Effective September 15, 1992.)
