## Text

Funds appropriated to the State Department of Health Care Services which are designated for local mental health services and funds which the State Department of Health Care Services is responsible for allocating or administering, including, but not limited to, federal block grants funds, shall be expended in accordance with this section and Sections 5710 to 5717, inclusive, except when there are conflicting federal requirements, in which case the federal requirements shall be controlling.
