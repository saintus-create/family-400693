## Text

As used in this article, county psychiatric health facility means a 24-hour acute care facility provided by the county pursuant to the provisions in Sections 5404 and 7100.

## History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 245, Sec. 3. Effective July 22, 1996.)
