## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, for purposes of Section 10850, a team engaged in any activity permitted pursuant to Section 11325.93 shall be deemed to be engaged in the administration of public social services.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 919, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
