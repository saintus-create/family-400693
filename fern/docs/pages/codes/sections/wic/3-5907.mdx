## Text

No later than January 1, 1992, the director, in consultation with the California Conference of Local Mental Health Directors and representatives of institutions for mental disease, shall develop a suggested uniform contract format that may be used by counties for the purchase of services from institutions for mental disease.

## History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 198. Effective June 30, 1991.)
