## Text

Anyone who willfully interferes with any lawful action of the office shall be immediately referred to the appropriate licensing authority, which shall respond within the legally prescribed time period.

## History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1097, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
