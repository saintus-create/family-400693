## Text

The Department of General Services shall grant to the Regents of the University of California in fee simple all of its right, title and interest in the land adjacent to the campus of the University of California Medical School Center, San Francisco, at no cost to the regents, upon which the Langley Porter Neuropsychiatric Institute is located, for the purpose of integrating the institute into the total educational system of the University of California.
