## Text

Nothing in this article shall be interpreted as limiting or interfering in any way with the department’s authority to contract for the provision of incontinence medical supplies pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 14105.3.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 705. Effective January 1, 2007.)
