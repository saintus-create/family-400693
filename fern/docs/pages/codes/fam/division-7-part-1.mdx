**Division 7. Division of Property › Part 1. Definitions**

Family Code §§ 2500–2502 · 2 sections · [Family Code overview](/codes/fam)

## § 2500

Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the definitions in this part govern the construction of this division.

*(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)*

## § 2502

“Separate property” does not include quasi-community property.

*(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)*
