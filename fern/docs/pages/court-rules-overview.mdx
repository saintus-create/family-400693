California court rules explain how cases are filed, scheduled, presented, and decided. They include the **California Rules of Court**, local rules, and rules that apply to particular proceedings.

<Note>
Rules change. Confirm the current version, effective date, and court-specific requirements before relying on a rule in a filing or hearing.
</Note>

## Where to look

- [California Rules of Court](https://www.courts.ca.gov/rules.htm) — statewide rules and rule histories.
- [California Courts self-help](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/) — plain-language procedural information for the public.
- [California Courts directory](https://www.courts.ca.gov/find-my-court.htm) — locate a superior court and its local rules.

## A practical research sequence

1. Find the statewide rule that addresses the procedure.
2. Check the applicable superior court's local rules and standing orders.
3. Confirm filing deadlines, required forms, service rules, and page limits.
4. Verify whether a statute, case, or order creates an exception.
